SCENE - Laura returns

	April, 1996. Laura Barrett was lost, terrified, and had nowhere else to go.
	No sooner had she stepped off before a younger Vince, in his 20s, rushed over and hugged her. She hugged him back, sinking into it.
	"Laura..." he said softly.
	"Vince..." she whispered the name, like a prayer.
	He grabbed her travel bag, and the two began walking.
	"I'm sorry... I'm sorry it was such short notice."
	"No, not at all. You know I'm always here for you, sis." Vince replied.
	Laura smiled sadly.
	"What happened, Laura? What's wrong?"
	"I..." she began, tearing up. "I can't..."
	Vince smiled sadly at her. "It's okay. We'll talk when you're strong enough."
	The pair made it to Vince's apartment. Vince made her a cup of tea, which she held in both hands.
	"So, listen. You can stay here as long as you need. I'll take the sofa."
	"No, no, Vince. I'll take the sofa, it's fine." she protested.
	"Sis..." Vince began, "It's okay. The bed's crap."
	The pair had a much-needed chuckle.
	Vince grabbed a bottle of whiskey.
	"Glass?" he offered her. She shook her head.
	"That's the thing, Vince... I..." she began.
	Vince leaned in, listening.
	"I'm pregnant." she admitted.
	Vince stood in stunned silence for a minute, before pouring himself a drink.
	He downed it, and sat with her.
	"Who's the father?" he asked.
	"No one you know." Laura replied, looking away. "Just... just some bloke over in America."
	"Did he hurt you?" Vince asked firmly.
	"Vince... don't..."
	"Who is he?" Vince continued, his voice cold and wrathful.
	"No." Laura said firmly. "I know you. And if I tell you, you'll go after him. And he *will* kill you."
	"I'll fuckin' kill him." Vince growled. "Who the fuck is he?"
	"VINCE!" Laura barked, snapping him out of it. "You. Can't. Fight. Him."
	"Why not?" he pleaded.
	"It's complicated." she sighed. "All you need to know is that you can't fight him."
	Vince sat there in silence for a moment.
	"So..." he began. "What are you going to do?"
	"What do you mean?"
	"You know damn well what I mean."
	"No. Absolutely not." Laura folded her arms and legs. "Not happening."
	"Laura. We're not in the Bogside. No one gives a shite if you get an abortion."
	"We're still in the UK. I can't prove I was violated, and there's no sign it's a risk to my life." Laura countered.
	"Laura, I fuckin' know people. I can get you the medicines - proper good medicine, from France."
	"I don't care." Laura snapped back. "I don't want to get an abortion, Vince."
	"Why not? And don't start with the whole Jesus crap."
	"I'm not. I don't give a shite about religion, you know that. I just... I don't want one. It's my choice." she replied firmly.
	Vince sighed. "Okay. I just... I want you to know, I wouldn't blame you if you did."
	"I know." she smiled sadly, hugging him. They both sat back, and looked downwards.
	"Look, what happened... it was the worst thing that ever happened to me." she began. "The way I see it, this is a way to make something good out of it. Otherwise, I suffered for nothing. You know?"
	"I get it..." Vince sighed.
	Laura sipped her tea, which had gone cold.
	"I know it's too soon, but... I've already thought of names." she said, forcing a half-smile.
	Vince chuckled. "Have you now?"
	"Yeah." Laura replied. "If it's a girl, I'll call her Sophie."
	"And if it's a boy?"
	"I'm thinking... Connor."

Or at least, not real ones. Speaking of..."

Even with the suit, they can match you for strength; and they're way more agile than you. Worse, they know that suit inside out - they'll know exactly where the armour's weakest, and how to catch you off-guard. If you *ever* see Super-Agents on the field, you get the fuck outta there and call me."

SCENE - Morland's pressure

	That day was a tense one in Vitalis, as Chloe frantically went over some notes. She sipped her coffee cup, her eyes bagged slightly - it was clear she hadn't slept well. Another person, an older, heavyset man in formal attire, entered the room.
	"How's it going, Ms. Derrington?" he asked. Chloe jumped, not having expected company.
	"Oh, Mr. Morland, sir! Yes, I'm ready for the meeting." she replied, nervously.
	"Excellent. You've been told which topics are... off-limits, yes?"
	"Oh, I-- I was under the impression that there were no off-limit topics, sir. I mean, what with them being police and all..." she said, awkwardly fidgeting.
	"Well, dispel that impression." he replied firmly. "This company relies on its IP. The findings we have made here... they are ours, and ours alone."
	He paused, studying Chloe's reaction. She hid it well, but was *sickened* by him. Something about him was just... deeply creepy. Was it the greed? The fact that his PA's were always women decades his junior? Or the fact that even with mutant monsters fighting and dying, he was more focused on IP than the truth?
	"You may explain the findings on the Giant - as they are too distinctive to ignore." he explained. "But, our findings in relation to these 'Agents' - those are not to be divulged. Is that clear, Ms. Derrington?"
	"Yes, sir." she replied curtly. "I shall not speak about the Agents to them."
	Morland may not have realised, but Chloe emphasised the word 'speak'.
	"These findings are more than just data, Ms. Derrington. They represent power. Control. And in the wrong hands, they could lead to chaos. Anarchy. I trust you understand why some knowledge must remain... proprietary."
	Chloe shuddered at his words. Power. Control. Was he... involved? She banished the thought.
	With that, he left the lab. Chloe returned to her notes, before placing them in a binder and sighing. She clutched the binder tightly, deep in thought.
	"What do I do? Why am I here? What is going on? Can't I just be normal?"
	She took out her phone.
	"Heya, here's the address for tonight: 76 Jersey Road, Caldermouth. Don't look it up, it's a surprise, haha!" she texted Connor. She smiled slightly, glad that there was something normal going on in her otherwise abnormal life.
	A message returned almost immediately.
	"Ooh - alright, I won't look it up, if you let me pick you up from yours. I've got a surprise for you too ;)" he wrote.
	She chuckled. Amidst the shadows and the monsters, she still had Connor.
	She took a deep breath, and left the lab.
	As she left, she was watched from the vents by something.
	Two yellow eyes, looking in from above.
	Wolf eyes.

	Sparky crawled through the ventilation shafts, his paws making little noise. He made his way to the office. There, Morland was on the phone.
	"Commandant, I swear - Progeny is a complete mystery. Every discovery we make, 10 more questions follow."
	"Then get to the answers, Morland - I didn't call you to hear excuses. Do not let me down." a voice replied coldly.
	"Of course, sir. Hail Victory." he replied, before hanging up. He slumped back in his chair and sighed. "Mad bastard..."
	Sparky paused. For a moment, he considered ripping out Morland's throat then and there--no. He banished the thought, suppressing his urges.

	He prowled onwards, making his way to a laboratory. There, he saw a scientist with a dictaphone.
	"Subject continues to show no changes in mental state. However, tumorous masses have started to subside." the man spoke. Sparky looked over, seeing a horrifying sight - a bulbous flesh-mound in the rough shape of a man. It screamed in pain, causing the scientist to press a button. Sparky sniffed, and could detect the unmistakable scent of anaesthetics. The creature sobbed and skulked away. Sparky was disgusted. Once the scientist left, he entered the lab, and approached the creature.
	"Poor thing..." he said, sadly.
	"Help me..." it whispered.
	"I wish I could." Sparky replied.
	"You can hear me?" the creature asked.
	"...you can hear *me?*" Sparky tilted his head incredulously.
	"Yes... please... please kill me..." it sobbed.
	"Wait. How can you hear me? Are you an 'Esper,' too?"
	"Please... kill me..." the creature moaned again.
	Sparky sighed.
	Another creature that could hear him. But...
	No.
	Making it live would be wrong.
	He approached the cage, and leapt up, trying to read the notes.
	He couldn't quite make it out - his reading still needed practice. But he could make out a few parts.
	"Subject 2163." "Maintain care when applying pain relief."

	He pressed the Pain Relief button again. And again. And again. Eventually, the creature's face twisted into something approximating a smile, and it ceased all movement. The air reeked of decay and despair.
	He bowed his head in sorrow, returned to the vent, and carried on.
