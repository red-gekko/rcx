SCENE - Awakening

	WAKE UP.
	The words echoed through Connor's skull.

	WAKE UP.
	He saw glimpses. Flashes.
	The first hit him like a truck. His blade swinging down on Junior's neck, taking off his head.
	The second was more subdued. His hands shaking the Super-Agent assassin in the alleys outside the Singing Horse, many months ago. He felt as the neck snapped in his palms.

	WAKE UP.
	The voice was ethereal.
	Inhuman.
	Monstrous.
	Alien.
	A clarion call. Demanding. Ordering.
	WAKE UP. WAKE UP.

	Connor grabbed Laverty, and it felt *good.* So he drove the fire poker up his skull.
	First kill.

	WAKE UP. WAKE UP. WAKE UP.

	More flashes, now.
	He saw Junior ripping him in half.
	No, not him. It was through Sparky's eyes.
	He saw that bastard Commandant killing young wolves, while he--no, Sparky--was chained down.
	The mind-merge they shared. It roared on, like a telepathic storm.
	He saw the moment he punched Tomlinson in the chin, detonating the skull like a shotgun blast.
	He saw Steve's heart punctuated by his bullet; and Eileen's heart break.

	WAKE UP.
	He rose up from the ground. He spotted a trio of men, standing over Shannon. She'd been zip-tied, and pistol-whipped.
	He felt rage. Rage. RAGE!
	He gripped the first through the back, his fingers popping through the skin like paper. He gripped the spine, and ripped it out in a fluid motion, before swiping his arms through, cutting the man to shreds.
	The other two turned, and frantically drew their weapons. It was no use.
	He recognised the leader - Gary Ritter. What was he doing here?
	This wasn't a memory... it wasn't like Sparky had human hands. And he'd never slaughtered people in front of Shannon... had he?
	He delivered a punch to Gary, as strong as he could go. The fist went straight through the chest, popping out the other side and leaving a massive hole around his forearm.
	Worse than that. The force of the blow created enough motion, it sucked Gary's body through it. As it fell through, it tore into giblets of gore, which sucked through the hole and sprayed all over the room.
	The final man fired a shot right at Connor's head, which dug into his cheek. He delivered an uppercut with launched the head upwards. The skull shattered on impact, splatting and sticking to the ceiling.
	He delivered a kick to the torso, disintegrating it into paste. The legs seemed to take a step forward, before collapsing.
	He took a deep breath, his chest heaving. Shannon looked up in horror.
	"Trey?" she asked, panting in fear.

	Oh.
	Another memory.
	But...

	WAKE UP. WAKE UP. WAKE UP. WAKE UP.

	WAKE UP.

SCENE - Waking up

	"Shh, shh, don't try to talk." Chloe rushed over. Sveta was there too, and she pulled out the feeding tubes, before flashing a pen-torch into his eye.

	"It's a bandage, don't---" Sveta began, but Connor took it off.
	"Oh, yeah..." he sighed.
	"You took some really bad beating to your head, dorogoy." Sveta began softly. "You... you might've lost some things."

	"I'm sorry, I--I called Sveta, I didn't know what else to do, I..."
	"It's alright." he smiled softly. "You did the right thing."

	"Where are we?" he asked. "My, uh... pigeon-senses aren't working yet."

	"Mr. Barrett... Connor. I know it doesn't feel like it, but you saved so, so many lives. What happened today... was a victory."

	"No." Chloe cut in. "It was both."
	Connor looked at her, a tear forming in his eye.
	"As far as we can tell, that monster--" Bevan began.
	"Junior." Connor interrupted. "His name was Junior. And he had blue eyes and brown hair, and he was gonna be 7 in May. He was a victim too."
	The others looked at him, confused. Bevan nodded deferentially.
	"As far as we can tell, Junior was already going to be released, sooner or later. Documents recovered from Murphy's gang implies he wanted to prove himself to Paladin though mass murder."
	"Where's Murphy now?" Connor asked, rising slightly.
	"Missing. I wish I could say 'presumed dead,' but..."
	Connor sighed, and turned to Chloe. "Can I see Sparky?"
	"Yeah, of course." she smiled, helping him up.
	"I'll see you later, Mr. Barrett." Bevan nodded.
	"Yeah... see you around." Connor nodded back. Chloe helped him out of the room.

The biggest challenge has been getting food into his system without a stomach or intestinal tract - we've been providing him with parenteral nutrition directly into his bloodstream. Normally, we'd have to calculate the mix exactly - but thankfully, his body is filtering it for us. We're dialysing him in lieu of kidneys, and..."

	"Chloe?!" William gasped. "Oh god, please tell me you're--"

And people are afraid - and they want the men who did this to suffer. I expect right now, co-conspirators are quietly cutting ties to avoid being caught up in your - our - retribution.
