
Paradrenaline states:
	Berserker Rage (Acute Paradrenaline Psychosis)
		The subject is overcome with intense, vicious wrath. They will fight with unparalleled ferocity, but struggle to differentiate friend from foe.
	Terror (Acute Paradrenaline Panic Disorder)
		The subject is overcome with intense fear. They will take extreme measures to escape the triggering situation.
	Catatonia (Acute Paradrenaline Catatonia)
		The subject is unable to decide on the best course of action, and so takes none, becoming catatonic.
	Fawning (Acute Submission)
		The subject immediately submits to the trigger of the episode, surrendering and making appeasement behaviours
	Black Hunger (Acute Hyperphagia)
		The subject is consumed by feral hunger. They will try and eat any available food, not caring from the source.
	Numbness (Hypoaffective Apathy)
		The subject's emotions are severely blunted, resulting in minimal emotional reaction.
	Seizures (Acute Epileptiformia)
		The subject will experience grand mal seizures with minor warning
	Nosebleeds (Epistaxis)
		The subject will suffer regular bleeding from the nose
	Migraines (Neuralgia)
		The subject will feel intense pressure pains in their head
	Strokes (Acute Cerebral haemmorhagia)
		The subject will experience temporary loss of brain function due to aneurysm
	Heart attacks (Acute cardiopathy)
		The subject will experience random cessation of cardiac function
	Confusion (Acute Delerium)
		The subject will become disoriented and absentminded
		Fugues
			The subject may forget key details or their entire identity for a short period
	Acute Hallucinations
		The subject may see or hear things that are not there
	Flaggelation (Pathological self-injury)
		The subject will be overcome with a desire to inflict pain on itself
	Healing Coma
		The subject will fall into a deep sleep, as their body tries to regenerate
	Death Flush (Acute thermodysregulation)
		The subject will experience a positive feedback loop in their thermoregulatory system, rapidly heating to deadly levels
	Nausea
		The subject will feel intense nausea, experiencing regular vomiting, including vomiting bile
	Klazomania
		The subject will lose the ability to control their volume, constantly shouting at the top of their lungs
	Hysterical regression
		The subject will, in situations of extreme stess, regress to childlike behaviour (seeking a 'parental' figure, irrational avoidance of danger, etc)
	Synesthesia
		The subject will experience extreme synesthesia (moreso than they normally would), causing sensory overload
	Acute Mutism
		The subject becomes unable to speak for the duration of the episode
	Pathological Impulse Dysregulation
		The subject loses most of their inhibitions, acting on impusles they would otherwise repress
	Aphasia
		Similar to mutism, but affecting the subject's mental dictionary instead of their ability to speak aloud
	Agnosia
		The subject loses the ability to process a particular form of sensory input. They still receive the input, but do not consciously process it. Their reflexes can still react, as this happens outside of the CNS.
	Incontinence
		The subject becomes unable to reliably control excretory functions
	Pica
		The subject absentmindedly begins chewing or attemting to consume inedible objects
	Pathological Rumination
		The subject will uncontrollably vomit into their mouths and then swallow again, like a ruminant chewing cud
	Akinetopsia
		The subject loses the ability to reliably perceive motion, though other visual aspects are unaffected
	Acute hypermania
		The subject experiences a manic episode, displaying hyperarousal, high energy, and disregard for risks.
	Apraxia
		The subject will find either gross, fine, or both gross and fine motor activities much more difficult
	Paralysis
		The subject becomes completely unable to move the affected area
	Acute Demotivation
		The subject experiences severe depression, despair, and demotivation
	Moroseness
		The subject experiences a grim, morose state of mind, feeling only sullen anger.
	Ocular Haemorrhagia
		Subject experiences bleeding in the eyes, resulting in red irises or sclera, or even 'crying blood'
	Arthritis
		The subject experiences severe joint pain
	Strabismus
		The subject's eyes fail to orient properly
	Nystagmus
		The subject's eyes shake uncontrollably
	Vertigo
		The subject experiences feelings of motion not based on reality
	Hypersalivation
		Typically connected to the Black Hunger, the subject begins drooling uncontrollably
	Zealotry (Hyperconviction)
		The subject becomes deeply convinced of their own moral infallibility, and eschews moral limitation in pursuit of their goal
	Doubtfullness (Hypoconviction)
		The subject becomes deeply *unconviced* of their own morality, and constantly agonises over their actions
	Pathological Skepticism
		The subject becomes unable to be convinced of any concept. They will, without fail, not believe any information given to them by others. Information received by their own observation will still be absorbed
	Prosopagnosia
		Try as they might, the subject cannot reliably tell people apart
	Grudginess
		The subject will not tolerate perceived slights, and will take irrationally long times to forgive or forget
	Hyperforgiveness
		The subject will unreasonably tolerate wrongs committed against them, refusing to express or even feel any negative reaction to unfair treatment
	Cytokine Storms
		The subject will experience sudden onset, severe cytokine storms, resulting in fever, fatigue, vomiting, chills, respiratory distress, or delerium.
	Maladaptive Daydreaming
		The subject experiences intense daydreams, being mentally asleep despite physically awake
