Casting any spells requires at least a 5 score in Psionic Affinity.
Basic Spells - Can be cast without Psychic Discipline skill
	Psi Blast: Blasts the target with raw psionic energy
	Psi Vision: Sees psionic energy - can see the specifics of spells cast, and can detect the presence of souls nearby
	Psi Blade: Generates a psionic lance from the hand, which can be used as a bladed weapon
	Broadcast: Projects the caster's thoughts into an aura around them, allowing their Will to be used by others
	Possess: Can possess a vacant or dominated body
	Void Rift: Lets loose a vortex of psionic energy, damaging enemies and casting random known spells around the caster
	Sadism: Special talent that grants Focus when dealing damage
	Masochism: Special talent that grants Focus when receiving damage
	#Special: Psychic Block: Counters a hostile psychic action. On critical success, damages the hostile caster. Does not require psychic power, instead requires specialist training. Uses Will stat rather than Psi stat

Kinetic Paths
	Telekinesis - manipulate motion
		Telekinetic Grip - lifts the chosen object from afar
		Shove - Sends a bolt of telekinetic energy at the target
		Compress - Crushes the target into itself
		Shockwave - blasts out a wave of telekinetic energy
		Barrier - Creates a shield that absorbs incoming attacks
		Explode - blow up the target object
	Cacophony - manipulate noise
		Sonic Boom - Create a shockwave that harms and disorients targets
		Scream - Screams at the target, dealing damage and disorienting
		Vibroblades - Generates blades of pure vibration - orders of magnitude sharper than normal bladed weapons
		Thunderous Howl - Blast a deep howl, scaring and disorienting enemies
		Resonance Field - Creates a field of noise in a location, reducing the impact and accuracy of weapons
		Deconstruct - Causes the target object to start falling apart and disintegrating
	Translocation - manipulate space
		Blink - Teleports the caster a short distance
		Phase Shift - Becomes intangible for a moment, avoiding attacks
		Portal - Opens a portal to any known location
		Skip - Teleports the target a short distance
		Chaos Skip - Teleports multiple targets to random locations nearby - can skip into objects
		Pocket Dimension - Creates a "bigger on the inside" space that can be used to store stuff, as a base of operations, or a prison for enemies. Can only have one Pocket Dimension active at a time.

Empathic Paths
	Telepathy - interface with minds
		Read Mind - Probes the target's mind, reading thoughts and secrets
		Implant Thought - Projects a thought into the enemy's mind, confusing them, or making them more suggestible
		Brain Drain - Syphons the target's mental capacity, taking their WIL and INT and adding it to the caster
		Madness - Severely messes with the target's head. Can cause berserk state, panic, sleep, or disorientation.
		Mind Merge - Joins minds with the target. 25% of all effects, statuses, or stat drains/regen felt by one is instead felt by the other. All Mental rolls are performed using the one with the highest stat or skill.
		Dominate - Takes control over the target. Can also brainwash captured targets, making the control permanent. Controlled targets count as Minions for gameplay purposes.
	Demiurgy - interface with machines
		Repair - Fixes a target machine
		Overdrive - Increases the power of a machine, but also damages it
		Interface - Psychically connects with a machine, allowing the caster to access it without needing a terminal or computer. Also makes it more suggestible, making hacking attempts easier
		Subversion - Subverts the target machnine, causing it to malfunction
		Breakdown - Causes the target machine to start breaking down
		Override - Controls a targeted machine, effectively hacking it
	Mutarchy - interface with flesh
		Restoration - Either heals the target, restores their stamina, or removes health conditions
		Invigorate - Strenghtens and fortifies the target, increasing their physical stats and perception
		Drain Life - Steals ATP from the target, dealing damage and healing the caster
		Enfeeble - Weakens the target
		Plague - Creates a toxic plague, which can be dispersed via different means. Damages and weakens targets
		Mutate - change the target's biology. Difficult to control, can end up causing changes incompatible with life. Higher experience means more stable mutations

Thaumaturgic Paths
	Enchantment - control objects
		Empower - Infuses the object with magic, improving its stats
		Degrade - Drains the object's psychic imprint, reducing its stats
		Embed Spell - Embeds a known spell into an object, causing the spell to be cast under certain conditions. The object counts as the caster for mechanical purposes
		Golem - Animate an object, turning it into a minion
		Alchemy - Grant new properties to an object, or even transmute it into something else
		Soulstone - Binds the target's essence into an object. Upon death, the target's consciousness re-awakens inside the Soulstone. Said Stone can always cast Possess even if they are not a Psion.

	Chronomancy - control time
		Slow Time - Slows down time in a given area. Does not affect the caster
		Retry - Rerolls a skill or stat roll
		Stasis Field - creates a bubble of frozen time around the target, immobilising them and making them invulnerable
		Rapid Aging - speeds up timeflow on a target, aging them prematurely
		Loop - Creates a Time Loop in a given area. Sapient beings retain memories between loops. The loop must be constantly sustained by the caster
		Time Shift - Shifts the caster to an alternate timeline of their choosing.
	Conjuration - control reality
		Familiar - Summon a minor entity from beyond to assist the caster
		Phantasmic Structure - Creates basic physical objects like barriers and blades from pure, hardened psychic energy.
		Simulacrum - Creates a psychic copy of the target that serves the caster
		Rain of Fire - Conjures a psychic storm in
		Contract - Summons a major entity from beyond and binds them with a contract
		Heaven Strike - Conjures a psionic storm in the target location, calling down bolts of energy
		Fusion - Merges 2 summoned minions together, or merges 1 with the caster

	Divination
		Forewarning - provides the target with premonitions of danger, increasing their Dodge and Parry chances
		Second Sight - the target gains extra Perception, and can cast Psi Vision regardless of their current Psy power
		False Vision - implants false premonitions in the target's mind, reducing their Dodge, Parry, and Perception
		Change Destiny - raises or lowers the target's Luck stat
		Show The Way - Upon casting, specify a given event or action. The three most likely outcomes of that event will be made known to the caster.
		Fated - When targeting allies, their next success is critical, and the next critical success against them becomes normal. On enemies, the effect is reversed

	Sanctification (Only available to religious psions)
		Aura - Projects a specific Aura around the caster. Different Auras can have different effects, like Strength, Fortitude, Intellect, Terror, Agony, etc.
		Purge Soul - Damages the target. This damage is increased if the caster's faith considers the target to be a Heretic, Pariah, or Abomination
		Blessing - Blesses the target, healing them and improving their stats
		Curse - Curses the target, harming them and reducing their stats
		Chosen One - The caster's Luck stat is raised significantly
		Devotion - Etch a special rune dedicated to a god. If successful, a single favour or boon will be granted


Energetic Paths
	Illumination - empower light
		Flash - Creates light of variable intensity in an area
		Beam Attack - Fires a laser beam at the target
		Irradiate - Blasts the target with radiation, causing major damage over time
		Laser Eyes - Fires a continuous beam from the eyes, damaging targets
		Hardlight - Creates physical structures out of solid light
		Gamma Strike - Blasts the target with a laser that also irradiates
	Conflagration - empower flame
		Fireball - Launches a ball of fire at the enemy
		Incinerate - Unleashes a beam of fire at the target
		Flame Vortex - Surrounds the caster in a fiery tornado
		Magma Bolt - Melts an object into liquid form and launches it at the target
		Hellion - Summons a basalt sheathe over the caster's body, then wreathes it in fire, buring anything that gets close while leaving the caster unharmed
		Inferno - Create a firestorm on a target area, causing explosions of flame periodically
	Electromagnetism - empower electricity
		Magnetic Grip - Manipulates electromagnetic fields to perform primitive telekinesis, or create a makeshift Gauss Gun
		Lightning Bolt - Shocks the enemy with a charge of lightning. Can be set to 'stun' instead
		Disrupt - induces a seizure in organic targets, and a major electrical interference in machines
		Static Skin - Shocks all targets in melee range
		Plasma Bolt - Generates a bolt of plasma and launches it
		Stormzone - Electrifies a targeted area, shocking anyone who enters
Antipsionic Paths
	Cryotics - negate heat
		Frost Bolt - Cools a sphere of air into extreme cold, and launches it at a target
		Cool Air - Freeze the water in the air around the caster, making shields or shards
		Chill Touch - Deals freezing damage to the target in melee range
		Frost Armour - Covers the caster in frost, making them immune to frost damage, reducing Sharp damage, and causing frost damage to nearby enemies
		Freeze - Freezes the target, dealing extreme frost damage and paralysing them
		Blizzard - Freezes an entire area, dealing damage and slowing down targets
	Entropism - negate magic
		Entropic Shielding - protects the caster from incoming spells
		Dispel - Removes magic effects on a target
		Silent Shell - Stops all casting in a given area
		Synaptic Feedback - Redirects enemy spells, causing major Psychic damage to enemy spellcasters when they cast against the caster
		Banish - Destroys a magical construct or being
		Veil - Absorbs light, making objects and targets invisible. Can also remove radiation in an area and stop energy weapons
	Necromancy - negate life
		Soulspeak - Consults with the souls of the dead, allowing new wisdoms to form, holding a seance, or calling forth vengeful spirits
		Decay - Necrotise the target, causing flesh to rot and metal to rust
		Animate Dead - Raise a dead body into service, animating it with psychic essence. Animated Dead count as Minions.
		Sacrifice - Sacrifices a Minion, willing target, or subdued target. Gains lots of focus, removes Synaptic Pressure, and heals and buffs the caster. 
		Fleshcrafting - Combines dead flesh together to produce Flesh Constructs
		Lich Lord - The caster becomes an immortal lich, resurrecting when killed

Class Paths (Special paths available to psions who have mastered a baseline class)
	Destruction - (Heavy Path)
		Quake - Shakes the ground beneath the target, stunning them and dealing damage
		Concussive Firebolt - Launches a mix of telekinetic force and flame at the target, dealing blunt and fire damage
		Detonate - Cause an explosion at the target, dealing damage
		Psi Volley - Fires a mass volley of Psi Blasts, hitting multiple targets
		Rain of Fire - Calls down meteors on the target location, dealing massive damage
	Invasion - (Trooper Path)
		Thundersprint - Massively improves the speed of the caster
		Stonewall - Fortifies the caster's body, making them take less damage
		Rend - Launch a frenzied attack with a Psi Blade
		Shock and Awe - Every time the caster takes damage, there is a chance that the attacker is hit with a lightning bolt
		Taunt - Screams a taunt, drawing focus on enemies. Automatically Fortifies the caster.
	Obscuration - (Covert Path)
		Confuse - Dazes the enemy momentarily, allowing you to sneak past
		Vanish - Dazes alert enemies and teleports into cover, evading combat
		Illusion - Creates a Simulacrum of the caster that cannot attack, but can distract enemies
		Invisibility - Become completely invisible for a time
	Protection - (Support Path)
		Mend - Heals the target
		Fortify - Fortifies the target's body, making them take less damage
		Word of Inspiration - Raises the target's Morale
		Word of Strength - Empowers the target, Fortifying them and increasing their speed
		Heaven Strike - Conjures an Airstrike in the target location, using Focus instead of Supply

#Special: Blood Magic (only available to Vampires. Uses blood instead of Focus)
	Coagulate - Block your target’s blood in their veins, making them immobile and defenceless
	Shadow Veil - Fade into shadows and become invisible to most enemies
	Glamour - Subtly changes the caster's appearance, scent, and other physical attributes, making them irresistable to the target
	Hypnotic Glare/Enthrall - Stares into the target's eyes, can either freeze them in place as long as eye contact is unbroken, or cast Implant Thought on them. If this lasts long enough, Enthralls the target, turning them into a Minion of the caster
	Beast Form - Erupting into a frenzy, the caster teleports to all enemies around them, striking maniacally
	Blood Boil - Boil the target’s blood, causing it to violently explode, killing the target and anything nearby.
