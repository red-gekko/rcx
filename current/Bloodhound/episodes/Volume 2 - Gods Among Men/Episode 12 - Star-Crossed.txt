============================================================

It was the 1st of June, 2003. The sun shone over Derry, and in the Bogside, a small house stood.
Two masked men rushed away from the car, parked in the driveway. One turned to the other.
"No surrender, brother." he nodded.
"Aye. Hail Victory." the other replied.
They both ran away.
Connor sat in the living room. "Why can't I come?" he asked sadly.
"Sure, you'll embarrass us, boy." Eamon replied, gruffly.
Geraldine sighed. She knelt down, and stroked Connor's shoulder. She was wearing her Sunday best.
"Don't worry, darling. I'll read you the verses just like always once we get home." she smiled softly.
Connor hugged her tight. She held him for a moment.
"Geraldine, come on!" Eamon barked. She rolled her eyes out of sight, and stood.
"Remember, Connor." she turned at the door. "What do we say if Prods come knocking while granny and grandad are gone?"
"We say nothing, and we play hide-and-seek until they're gone." Connor recited. Geraldine rubbed his hair proudly.
"Get in the fuckin' car!" Eamon snarled.
Geraldine frowned, and headed to the car.
"Always giving out to me, like... sure, I'm only your wife, for God's sake..." she muttered quietly.
Connor held the door open, and watched as they entered the car.
"Shut that door now!" Eamon barked. Connor sighed.
Eamon turned the keys into the ignition.
It clicked, ran, and stopped. He cursed, and turned again.
It clicked, ran, and stopped.
"Fuckin' thing!" he snapped, smacking the dashboard, before turning the keys once more.
It clicked, ran, and--KA-FHWOOM.
The shockwave of the explosion knocked Connor onto his back. For a moment, his ears rang louder than anything, and his vision was blackened and blurry.
The small boy struggled to his feet. He stood, and fell, his knees buckling.
He crawled towards the car, as a foul stench flooded his nostrils. Petrol, melted rubber - no. Something worse.
Burning. Burning flesh.
He looked up. The ringing in his ears subsided just long enough to hear screams. Not just screams of fear, but of pure, unadulterated agony. Geraldine frantically rolled around on the ground, before going still as the fires took her.
Eamon's headless body slumped down, the fires claiming it.
Neighbours rushed to the wreckage, only to turn white in shock.
Undaunted, Connor crawled over to his grandmother's corpse, and tried desperately to put the fire out. His hands turned red and bloody from the burns, but he didn't stop until the fire turned to smoke.
He held her body for what felt like a thousand years. He pressed his ear to her chest, listening for a heartbeat.
It never came.

The sun rose above the valley, and after what felt like a short while, set again. Come the evening, Chloe arrived at Riverside.
Connor met her in the reception, and gave her a deep embrace.
"Haha, hey..." he smiled. "I missed you."
"Missed you too..." she whispered, before blushing. "You treat all your customers like this?"

"No, not really. We're still in the black." he smiled. "Anyway..."
He smirked, and held up a DVD of Red Rebellion. "You wanted to watch this?"

She sat down on the sofa, making herself comfortable.
"Where's Sparky?" she asked.
"Oh, and here I thought you were here to see me." Connor snarked. She rolled her eyes and chuckled.
"He's out with Amy - she said it's her turn with the big bad wolf."

Beneath their feet, Sparky's super-hearing made his ears prick up.

Spartacus, you're on glider duty over Caldermouth - Doctor Rosenbaum sent a message in, there's been a string of thefts of rare medicines lately. I wanna find out if it's the Optimates, or just Skinners trying to get some more drugs. Either way, we need to stop them."
