VOLUME - Wolves Among Sheep
EPISODE - Blood Money
============================================================

SCENE - Migraines/IES

	For the first time in weeks, Drackhill actually had a nice, sunny afternoon. Naturally, the UV-starved residents of the valley had flocked to Rexton Bay to get some much-needed vitamin D. The gang were no exception.
	Connor tapped a rhythm onto his steering wheel as they waited patiently in traffic. Trey, decked out in gear far more appropriate to the summer he wished they were having, rather than the miserable autumn they'd got, had appointed himself as the car DJ - and thus, he called shotgun, much to Amy's chagrin. She got her revenge by placing herself right behind him, and took great pleasure in kicking the back of his seat. Beside her were Chloe and Bea, both eager to see the shores. Despite the relative warmth, Chloe had still opted to wear a long-sleeved hoodie, but had opted to unzip it for a change - wearing a turtleneck shirt underneath. Bea, on the other hand, lounged in her tank top, glad to get some sun on her skin. Last but not least, Sparky's head rested on the backs of the seats, as he rested in the boot.
	Finally, they found a car park that hadn't been completely colonised. Connor pulled into a space, and the gang disembarked.
	"Wow!" Bea grinned, looking out at the horizon. "This place looks amazing."
	"Welcome to Rexton!" Connor gestured out. "Originally a Saxon settlement called 'Cyninges Tun' - King's Town. When the Normans came here, they changed it to Rex Tun - the village was crown land before it was sold to House Calder by Richard the Lionheart in order to finance the Third Crus--"
	Trey did a fake yawn, and Connor rolled his eyes, grinning. "It's a beach town, basically." he shrugged.
	The girls chuckled, and Connor popped open the boot. Sparky hopped out and stretched.
	"Oh, biiiig stretch!" Amy cooed.
	"Thanks for noticing!" he barked. The gang made their way towards the beach.
	"So..." Trey began. "I'm thinking we do a nice walk over the beach, then go get drinks at Cindy's."
	"Cindy's?" Chloe tilted her head.
	"This is what happens when you don't let me infodump." Connor snarked. "Cindy's is a nightclub here."
	"How are we gonna get back?" asked Bea, as she finished putting some protective cream on her lighter patches.
	"I'm not drinking." Connor shrugged. "If any of yous spew in my car, you can clean it with a toothbrush."
	"Fair enough." Amy grinned, rushing out to the beach.
	"How come we're here, by the way?" Chloe asked. "I thought Torbrook was closer to you guys. Even Angleport would be closer."
	"Ooh, you've gone native." Connor chuckled. "Rexton's much nicer. Torbrook beach is all shrubland and salt bogs; and Angleport's all silty and gross. Rexton's the only nice, sandy beach in the valley - unless we go over to Brynmore, which... I'm not sitting on a ferry for god-knows-how-long just to go to the beach."
	Chloe smiled as the others made their way down the steps, but Sparky stopped dead in his tracks.
	"Connor!" he barked. "I don't like this stuff. I... it's weird, I..."
	"Hey, it's okay!" Connor rushed over, kneeling down.
	"No. I don't wanna walk on that. I..."
	"Sparky..." he whispered. "It's alright. It's just sand, okay? It's not gonna hurt you."
	"Everything okay?" Chloe asked, walking over slowly.
	"He, uh..." Connor paused. "He's a little nervous about the sand, I think."
	"I don't like it. It feels weird on my paws, I can't get a proper grip, it's getting in my fur..." Sparky whimpered.
	Connor thought for a moment, then smiled softly.
	"Here, watch me." he began. He took his shoes off, then his socks - remembering to actually take them off, rather than unmorph them.
	"Look. My paws are on the sand, but it's okay." he smiled softly. He pushed his toes down into the sand, then picked some up in his hand, piling it down. He rolled around in it, covering himself in grains.
	"See? It's all okay."
	Sparky hesitated, but finally, pressed his paws down. He smiled softly, and tip-toed down into the beach.
	"You know how you're not allowed to dig in the flat? Well, you can dig here as much as you like." Connor added, before demonstrating. "Go on - can you smell anything?"
	Sparky hesitantly sniffed the fresh hole. "I think there's something metallic down there."
	"Go get it then!"
	Sparky grinned, and began rapidly shifting sand outwards. Connor rose, brushing himself off. He looked up, expecting jeers and snark.
	Instead, his friends looked at him, smiling and proud.

	It was late now - well past midnight. Connor and Sparky were resting in their room, while Amy was drunkenly passed out in hers. Both 'werewolves' were in pain.
	"That was fun." Sparky groaned.
	"Glad you enjoyed it. We can do dog walks with them more often if you like." Connor smiled through the pulses of pain. He morphed a black globule out of his hand, and it burst - then, he let out a deep sigh.
	"What are you doing?" Sparky tilted his head.
	"Trying to make a smoke bomb." Connor shrugged. "I'm morphing out biomass, then trying to hyperpressurise it into aerosol, before dispersing it. Problem is, the casing I'm making isn't strong enough. I'll have to experiment a bit."
	"What would you want a smoke bomb for?"
	"Evasion, I guess." Connor shrugged. "Control the battlefield, and you control the battle."
	"You could just try getting better at stealth."
	"...did you just sass me?" he grinned. "You little--aaagh!"
	Hot blood ran from his nose. "You're getting these headaches too?" he asked, rubbing his temples.
	"I've had them for as long as I can remember." Sparky nodded, chewing on a bone to try to relieve the pressure.
	Connor shuddered. "I've always had migraines, but... they got worse ever since I got my powers."
	Sparky sighed, the pain drumming in his skull. "It's another thing these monsters have done to hurt us."
	"They certainly have form--Aghh!" another spike of agony, worse than last time. Connor's nose bled, and he looked over at Sparky, who was chewing on his own leg.
	"Oh shit, are you okay?" Connor asked. Sparky tilted his head.
	"I'm okay--I learned ages ago that when the headaches get bad, if you hurt yourself, they get better."
	"Hmm." Connor muttered. He pulled out his lighter, headed to the balcony, and began to burn his wrist. The pain was bad, but then--endorphins. It felt much better, both on his wrist and, more importantly, his head. He stopped once he could smell his wrist's smoke. He headed back inside.
	Sparky was relaxing now, his back leg cut up, but healing. Connor looked at his wrist, and it had already started to heal.
	"Do, um..." he began, hesitantly. "Do you ever hear... voices?"
	"Yes?" Sparky tilted his head. "I can hear yours right now."
	Connor chuckled. "I mean, voices *inside* your head. Telling you to fight, or... or to kill."
	"Oh, yeah." Sparky nodded. "That's normal, I think. It's worse when my head hurts. I think it wants me to hurt things, and if there's nothing for me to hurt, my only option is to hurt myself."
	"Makes sense." Connor shrugged. His phone started buzzing - his burner.
	"Privet, tovarishch!"
	"Hey, Ivan. Oh, should I say Verdict?"
	"This is a secure line - Ivan's fine." he chuckled.
	"Oooh, is that Ivan?" Sparky said, excitedly. "Hi, Ivan!"
	"Hah, I can hear the wolf barking. How is he?"
	"He's good." Connor smiled. "I'm learning a lot from him."
	"Well, I have something for you. A job."
	"What kind of job?"
	"Contract killing."
	"What?!"
	"Listen. Your target is a man named Omar Hattab. Algerian general with a chip on his shoulder. Plotting to overthrow the government, institute a military dictatorship, and go to war with Tunisia. Government can't do anything, Western agencies are backing him, the KGB can't touch him - but you can."
	"Why should I do this?"
	"Aside from him plotting a coup and a war? The payout's 3 million. Plus, we'll be owed by some power players."
	"You're asking me to kill a man, potentially the next leader of Algeria, for money?"
	"No." Ivan replied curtly. "I'm asking you to prevent a war that would kill hundreds of children, you'll just so happen to be rewarded for it."
	"Nice appeal to my morals. You're still asking me to kill for money."
	"Fine, yes - but he deserves it. And you need the cash."
	"What would the police say, if they saw some poor boy from the slums suddenly getting 3 million?"
	"They've thought of that. A shell company would be created, and the money paid to it - you just so happen to be its managing director. The money'll be tied up in assets - it'll take time to liquidate, but you'll be measurably better off."
	"Who's paying the money?"
	"Construction firm. They could lose billions in contracts, so they're having Hattab taken out."
	"Can't the government arrest him?"
	"With whose army? His own?"
	Connor steeled himself. "When do I head out?"
	"Not long - so get some sleep. Taxi will meet you outside at 6 AM. Will take you to a private airfield."
	"How does the KGB have this kind of mission going? Thought you guys'd be tied up with the civil war."
	"It's not KGB." Ivan explained. "There is a... a kind of company, known as International Executive Solutions, or IES."
	"That's the most shell company-arse name I've ever heard." Connor quipped.
	"Well, their HQ is in Switzerland for a reason, bratan." Ivan chuckled. "They will handle travel, alibis, room and board. All you have to do is kill the bastard."
	Connor hesitated. "Can I bring Sparky?"
	"Of course."
	"I'm in."
	Sparky looked up, excitedly. Connor relayed what had been told.
	"I'm ready." Sparky smiled. "If it helps us find the people who hurt us, we should do it."
	Connor breathed in, and they both got some rest while they could.

SCENE - Hattab briefing

	Morning came and bled into afternoon. Swimming through the skies above the Balearic Islands was a small private aeroplane, having left from Drackhill that morning. Inside, Connor was sat wearing a white shirt, green cargo trousers, and boots. Sparky was laid at his feet, panting slightly in the warmth. A man in a suit was talking to them, gesturing to a corkboard filled with photographs and maps.
	"You okay, buddy?" Connor asked, wary.
	"I'm in a big metal bird moving faster than anything I've ever seen. Why wouldn't I be?" Sparky replied, shaking slightly.
	"Hey, at least you're being sarcastic. You can't be that bad." Connor grinned. He stroked his packmate's ears gently.
	The suited man cleared his throat.
	"They did tell you the dog can speak, right?" Connor cocked an eyebrow.
	"They, um... they mentioned something along those lines." the man replied awkwardly. "Nervous flier, I'm guessing?"
	"I've never flown either." Connor shrugged. "Is it normal to feel like gravity's been inverted?"
	"At the risk of being impolite, I'm not sure you should be searching for 'normal' in your life, sir."
	"Fair point." Connor chuckled, morphing his costume on and off.
	"First part of the briefing is, well, brief. It's customary for IES operatives to receive a quick rundown on the local area - politics, history, culture, et cetera."
	"What, so I can pronounce 'couscous' correctly as I kill him?" Connor quipped.
	The man couldn't help but crack a smile. "Algeria - officially, the People's Democratic Republic of Algeria. Historically part of various Muslim empires, including a 300-year stint as a mostly-independent tributary of the Ottoman Empire. That is, until the French rocked up in 1830. The process of colonisation wasn't any different to most empires - mass killings, ethnic cleansing, establishment of settlements... unusually for most African nations under European rule, the country was annexed by the French, and integrated as a province of France - not that the locals got citizenship."
	"Sounds familiar." Connor nodded grimly, his mind flashing back to his childhood in Ireland.
	"Of course." the man bowed his head. "Preaching to the choir, I know. Anyway, it remained a province of France through both world wars - but after the second, Algerian nationalism became a more significant force. A 7-year war kicked off in the 50s, culminating in a failed coup by pro-war generals in France itself. After this, the French cut their losses. The country carried on like most post-colonial regimes until the 90s, when an Islamist political party called..." he flipped through his notes, "...the Islamic Salvation Front. They were on track to win the 1992 elections, so the army launched a coup d'etat, kicking off a decade-long civil war."
	"Who's in power now?"
	"The army won the war, but not without several atrocities. Not that the Islamists weren't brutal too, but they were by all rights the legitimate government. After the war, new elections were held, and president Ibrahim Sifi consolidated his control. He resigned in 2009 - the elections that year led to the Socialist Party of Algeria, or SPA, taking power. Terms in Algeria are 5 years long ever since Sifi's constitution, so in the recent elections, the SPA's current leader, Abdal Mohammed el-Bennani, spoke of impartial investigations into atrocities committed by both sides in the war. This unsettled the chiefs of staff - Hattab being the figurehead of this movement."
	"So they're carrying out a coup, just to cover up the crimes they committed in the last coup." Connor noted grimly.
	"Hattab may be the ringleader of this coup, but..." the man paused, "There are suspicions that ex-president Sifi is involved, too."
	"What, he couldn't be arsed to run for office again?" Connor quipped. "Anyway - is there anything I need to know about the region as a whole? I heard mention of war with Tunisia."
	The man sighed, and checked his notes again. "How much do you know about North African politics?"
	"Just give me the basics." Connor chuckled.
	"The two biggest rivals for power in the MENA are the United Arabian Federation - led by the Arab Socialist Ba'ath Party - and the Islamic Republic of Egypt - led by the Muslim Brotherhood. As you can guess from the names, the Ba'athists support secular pan-Arabism, while the Brotherhood supports Islamism. The Ba'athists themselves are divided into the 'Arabian' faction that is opposed to North African expansion and ethnic chauvinism, while the 'Arabist' faction is... not that. You may know from the news that Libya is embroiled in civil war - the main factions are Islamist insurgents backed by Egypt, nationalist, Gaddafist militants backed by the UAF, and a small democratic movement caught in between."
	He pulled out a map of the region, to better illustrate his lecture.
	"Tunisia is a democratic, multi-party republic - one of the few in the region. They mainly side with the UAF on regional politics, though - since both are secular modernists. When the Algerian Civil War ended, most of the rebels either went out to Libya to fight there, or sought refuge in Tunisia. From what we know, the military establishment in Algeria, especially Hattab's clique, don't want to get stuck in a quagmire of intervention in Libya - but they *do* want to 'settle the score' with the remnants in Tunisia."
	"Right... so who's backing Hattab, and who's backing us?"
	"Hattab is backed by South Africa - primarily because the SPA supports pan-Africanism, which is a massive threat to Apartheid." the man replied. "The UAF and Turkey are split - Hattab's anti-Islamism would ordinarily appeal to them, so the Kemalists in Turkey are providing token intelligence to him; but the UAF and South Africa are rivals, and the Arabian faction support Pan-Africanism too. Not to mention, the UAF is allied with Tunisia."
	"Complicated." Connor chuckled.
	"Geopolitics often is. Especially when having to choose between ideology and pragmatism." the man nodded. "We have reasonable suspicion that Iran is supporting Hattab, too."
	"I thought Iran was halfway to collapse?" Connor tilted his head.
	"They are - in part, because of the UAF. When the Khuzestani Arab Republic declared independence, the only country to recognise it was the UAF. It borders Iraq - one of the UAF's federal states - and the Arabist faction seeks to annex it. Naturally, the Shah is not happy with this at all - so he's aligned with South Africa in MENA politics largely as a middle finger to the UAF. Iran is mostly Shi'a, though, so the Salafists in Egypt hate him just as much as they hate the 'Godless Arabians.' That's not even getting into the so-called 'Kurdish Question' - Turkey, Iran, and the UAF are constantly flip-flopping on that - but that's not relevant to us."
	"...any other national histories I need to learn?" Connor snarked. "How long's this damn flight?"
	The man chuckled. "As for who's backing us, we were approached by French Intelligence, who back the SPA."
	"Why do the French back an anti-colonial party?"
	"Because a civil war just across the Mediterranean from them would be bad for business." he shrugged.
	"I thought this contract came from a construction company."
	"It does, too. A Swiss company is footing the actual bill - they have several contracts with the current government, specifically building railways. Those contracts would fall through in the event of civil war. French Intelligence provided us with our brief, and aside from that, we're also receiving minor support from Tunisia, who want Hattab gone before he can threaten them. No word yet on if the Brotherhood has anything to do with this, but I doubt it."
	Connor paused, hesitant.
	"What's wrong?" Sparky asked.
	"Nothing, buddy." he said quickly. "Alright, what's the plan?"
	"Hattab was last seen here." the man said, pointing to a grainy photo of a stern-faced man speaking on the phone in an office. "The headquarters of the DRS. That is, Departement du Renseignement et de la Securite - the intelligence agency. He's likely negotiating with key officials to secure their backing for his coup."
	"Overall, how supported is Hattab by the Algerian people and establishment?"
	"The military largely support him, with a couple of glaring exceptions. He's also supported by the Algerian deep state, naturally." the man shrugged. "As for the people... Algiers is currently wracked by protests in the streets - from what we can tell, they're demanding investigations into the army's conduct in the civil war, and a number of other government reforms targeting the deep state."
	"Glaring exceptions?"
	"The commander of the Gendarmerie Nationale is, to our knowledge, a government loyalist. He's so far refused to deploy the Gendarmerie to quell the protests - so we assume Hattab intends to arrest him and take direct command. Aside from him, the head of the Republican Guard is also loyal to the government - so we at least don't have to worry about the Praetorians as well."
	At that, he received an alert on his phone - a message from the pilot.
	"We'll arrive at Algiers in a few minutes - according to our estimations, he'll next be heading to the suburb of Cheraga to rally the Gendarmerie Nationale - their HQ is there. After that, he'll be going to El Mouradia - he'll be travelling personally to the Presidential Palace to arrest the executive branch, while his other forces secure the SPR - Service Public de Radiodiffusion - the state broadcaster. Once he's captured both sites, he'll bring the president to the SPR building, and take over the airwaves, forcing the president at gunpoint to declare Hattab as the new leader."
	"If we know all of this, how is the coup even gonna happen? Surely the Algerian government knows this, too." Connor asked, tilting his head. "Why don't they have a plan to stop this?"
	"They do. You're the plan." the man grinned.
	Connor nodded. "Well... sounds like a lot of transit. Plenty of opportunities to intercept him."
	"Well spotted. Your objective is to intercept his convoy as it travels to Cheraga. We've provided an ATV for interception - you should meet him halfway, in the fields just west of Dely Ibrahim. Said spot is a crucial junction for him to go from the DRS site to Cheraga, and also from Cheraga to El Mouradia, so they'll have blocked off all traffic there by now - meaning civilian presence will be minimal to nonexistent."
	He pulled out a machine gun, and a pack of explosives.
	"Neutralise him. No witnesses."
	Connor flinched, rejecting the weapons. "I'm killing Hattab, and only Hattab. I'm an assassin, not a terrorist."
	Sparky tilted his head.
	The man chuckled, and relented. "Fine. As long as the mark is dead."
	He handed a small radio device.
	"For secure comms. My call sign is Rattler. Yours is Werewolf."
	"Ha ha." Connor snarked, taking the device. "'Rattler' is somehow even worse than Verdict."
	"Says the man with the comic book name." Rattler snarked.
	Connor pointed down at his canine companion. "What about his codename?"
	"Unless he can start talking over the radio--"
	"He can hear it. And I might need to refer to him, specifically." Connor pushed firmly.
	Sparky looked up and smiled - grateful for his packmate's advocacy.
	"How about Spartacus?" he suggested.
	"Good idea." Connor smiled. "Spartacus."
	"...Spartacus it is." Rattler nodded incredulously.
	"Gentlemen!" the pilot said over the intercom. "We're landing in 30 seconds. Better hold onto something!"
	The handler took his seat. Connor took one too, but picked up Sparky, holding him through the landing.

SCENE - Hunting Hattab

	The fields stretched out endlessly under the burning sun. Connor and Sparky crouched behind a rocky outcrop, their ATV parked a short distance away. The stillness was broken by the distant hum of engines.
	"They're coming." Connor muttered. Sparky growled softly, his fur bristling.
	The convoy came into view: three MRAPs rolled in, their dark windows glinting in the sunlight.
	Connor stood, and stepped onto the road, morphing up his costume. Sparky likewise morphed up, only his mouth and tail uncovered. He bared his fangs in preparation.
	The lead vehicle screeched to a halt, the others stopping behind it. A gunman, perched out of a hatch on the roof, trained his rifle on the pair. Sparky skulked around, circling to the back of the convoy.
	"Ibt'aidu 'an al-tariq!" he shouted, his voice sharp and commanding. "Nahn mukhawalun biaistikhdam alquat 'iidha lam ta--"
	Before he could finish barking his command, Connor bolted forward in a zig-zag. The soldier opened fire, but Connor was like a blur. In a few heartbeats, he leapt up to the top of the car, grabbed the man by the throat, and launched him to the ground.
	Just as this happened, Sparky pounced on the gunman on the third vehicle, launching him in a similar way. His teeth tore the man's uniform shirt from his torso as he slammed into the ground.
	All hell broke loose.
	Soldiers poured out from the three vehicles, opening fire. Connor jumped down the hatch of the car he was on, slid up to the armoured door, and kicked its hinges. It budged, so he kicked again, knocking it free. He held it aloft, using it as a shield to charge the squadron, before bashing them in melee.
	Sparky, meanwhile, took a more vicious approach. A swing of his claws carved up the face of one soldier, while a well-placed bite tore up another's trigger-hand. Neither men would die from those wounds - but it wasn't pretty.
	"Hey! Easy with the bleeding!" Connor yelled across the battlefield. Sparky huffed, and locked his jaws on another soldier's arm, pulling back as much as possible to avoid cutting him. Then, he twisted his head, and a disgusting *SNAP* followed by blood-curdling screams marked the end of that man's contribution to the fight.
	The remaining soldiers carried on shooting, but Connor was already moving, weaving through the chaos with superhuman speed. He disarmed one man with a brutal twist of his wrist, sending the rifle clattering to the ground, then landed a bone-crunching punch that sent the man sprawling.
	A hail of bullets ripped through the air, several landing in Connor's back. He reeled from the pain, but it... it hurt less than he'd expected for assault rifle rounds. He swiftly spun around, pressing his back against the MRAP and holding his makeshift door-shield aloft. It made distinctive clanging sounds as bullets slammed into it or ricocheted off.
	Then, Connor raised the door into the air, and swung it into a soldier's head, making a satisfying *THUNK* noise as the man drifted into a deep sleep. Sparky gripped a soldier by his uniform, and threw him into the air. As he landed, the sound of bones popping out of their joints was overtaken by his cries of pain.
	Another hail of gunfire - this time, from an LMG. The mutants ducked for cover as the high-calibre rounds slammed into their hides.
	"Fuck..." Connor spat, poking his head out.
	"We need a way to fight back at range!" Sparky barked. "Any ideas?"
	Connor squeezed his eyes shut, focusing. Ink-black globules morphed above his wrists, which spread out like wings. Mechanical components slowly staggered into existence, and they hardened into the new design.
	Crossbows. Wrist-mounted crossbows.
	He formed bolts - blunt ones, meant to maim rather than kill - and took a deep breath. He waited for a split-second gap in the machine gun's fire, and jumped out.
	Time slowed to a crawl. He saw the gunner quickly readjust his aim, ready to tear him to pieces.
	He raised his fists, and saw the crossbows ripple as the bolts shot forwards.
	*phit-SHIK!*
	The gunner screamed in pain, as the bolts buried into his clavicles. He clutched them, groaning.
	"NOW!" Connor roared.
	Sparky pounced. In moments, he made it to the last remnant of soldiers, and before they could even fire back, he took them down.
	Finally, a young, pimply soldier, who accidentally dropped his gun, tried desperately to crawl away. Sparky advanced, his eyes glowing deep blood-red. The soldier frantically kicked out, but Sparky grinned, baring his teeth.
	"Istaslim! Istaslim! Ana 'astasilim! ʿAfak, ma t-'adarnish!"
	Sparky, undeterred, advanced, possessed by his inner demons.
	"SPARTACUS!" Connor roared, grabbing his scruff. Sparky replied by snarling viciously, turning his head to face Connor. The two locked eyes for a moment, before Sparky's red glow faded, and he looked away, calmed and in control again.
	Connor looked around frantically. He had made sure that nobody escaped during the chaos...
	So where the fuck was Hattab?!
	He pulled out a radio.
	"Rattler, this is Werewolf." he began. "Hattab is a no-show."
	"What?!" his handler barked back. "You're sure?!"
	"Dead certain." Connor replied grimly.
	"Hattab?!" the panicked soldier asked. "Rah machi hna! Rah rayeh l'El Mouradia!"
	"Ah..." Connor paused. "This would be easier if I knew Arabic."
	The soldier hesitated. "Um... il est alle au palais presidentiel!"
	"...Or French." Connor sighed again.
	His mind flashed back to Stoker. How he could read him like a book when in his grip...
	He pressed his hand against the soldier's chest.
	"Where is Hattab?" he asked, his voice echoing inside the man's head.
	"[He's gone to El Mouradia! We were sent as a diversion in case any loyalists came after us. He's on his way to the palace to detain the cabinet!]"
	Connor shuddered. Somehow, he understood every word. He pulled back, letting the two minds separate, and took a breath.
	"Rattler, this is Werewolf. Hattab knew we'd be here - he's ignored the gendarmerie, and has gone straight to the palace."
	"Shit... we're running out of time. Get on the ATV, and get to the palace, ASAP. Do not under any circumstances let Hattab kill the President. I've just received word that the minister of defence is in on this, too - so the other politicians are at best, collateral."
	Connor paused, but nodded. "Received."
	He launched himself back onto the ATV, as Sparky clutched onto his back. They sped off as fast as they could.

	Arriving into the suburb of El Mouradia, Connor spotted a military checkpoint between him and the Palace. The guards at the gate aimed their rifles.
	"Hang on tight!" he barked, as his costume began to harden and calcify. Sparky did the same, as the bullets began to sail towards them.
	Connor grimaced with the change, as more and more biomass wormed its way out of his pores and into his suit. The kevlar took a thick, almost bony form; while his hood tightened to his head into a jagged helm. A honeycomb shell formed over it - a shock-absorbent ballistic carapace.
	Sparky's own form likewise changed. The spikes that previously adorned it smoothed out like the sloped armour of a tank. His beaked cowl tightened into a similar style of helm, extending forwards to cover his snout.
	The twin Werewolves' eyes glowed a bright red as the chitinous armour finished encasing them. The bullets either slammed uselessly into their hides, or slid away against their exoskeletons.
	"NOW!" Connor screamed. The pair leapt from the buggy, careening above the checkpoint and making an audible impact on the tarmac below. They sprinted on, undeterred, approaching the steps of the Palace.
	Before continuing, Connor fell to his knees, de-armoured his suit, and caught his breath. He took off his mask, and his eyes faded to grey.
	"You okay?" Sparky panted.
	"Yeah... we... okay, I guess we can't use the armour power for long... drains us..." he said between breaths.
	"We can figure out more about our powers later! Come on!" Sparky rallied. Connor grinned, and rose.
	The pair breached the palace gates, and saw a platoon of soldiers, and behind them, a pile of bodies - bodies wearing the insignia of the Republican Guard. The palace had fallen.
	Instantly, the platoon raised their rifles, preparing to fire.
	A grin cracked under Connor's mask. A globule morphed from his glove.
	"SMOKE BOMB!" he shouted, as if emulating a character in a fighting game. He threw the glob down, and...
	*pffffft...* it pathetically hissed.
	The air stood still for a moment, as Connor took a deep sigh.
	"You've gotta be fuckin' kidding me..." he muttered, as the incredulous soldiers unleashed a hail of lead.
	The pair darted away from each other, taking cover behind marble pillars that became riddled with bullets. If they weren't being attacked by coupsters, Connor would've lamented the priceless architecture being ruined.

	"Doctor." Oxford began.

	"Oh? Then you've killed the Commandant and purged PaladinCorp?"

	"Do *I* need to remind you that I am not your puppet?!"

	Oxford's lip curled, and his eyes flashed a brilliant violet. Immediately, Neumann began choking, as though an invisible hand gripped his throat.
	"Think very... *very* carefully... about what you say next, Neumann." he snarled.
	"You--agh! You think I don't have a--a backup plan?" he spat. "I die... all your dirty business goes public."
	"Bullshit." Oxford scoffed.
	"Web servers... hidden over the world... release everything without my intervention..."
	"You're bluffing." Oxford shook his head.
	"Then call my bluff, little lord..."
	Oxford's intangible grip tightened, before it released, and his eyes settled back to yellow. Neumann coughed a little, but stood up straight.
	"I've always admired you, Neumann. Your tenacity, your cunning, your flexibility... you have always impressed us, despite your... unorthodox origins. But if you continue to irritate me... I'll kill you, and deal with the consequences myself. Understood?"

	"Get the fuck out of my house." he growled.
	Oxford scoffed, turned, and left.
