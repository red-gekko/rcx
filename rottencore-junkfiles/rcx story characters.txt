Backstory: Mo was once the foremost drug chef in the Redbridge Syndicate. His operation accounted for a full 38% of the Syndicate's revenue, bankrolling several of their community outreach programmes - ironically, including addiction rehab. Mo was kidnapped by EPF-aligned Skinheads, beaten, tortured for information, and sold to the Artist to be used in his "Experiments."
The Artist found some amusement in Mo's knowledge of chemistry, and so he implanted a number of glands that could produce any number of toxic gases. He also added a pipeline that allowed him to puke up stomach acid and projectile-vomit it, and finally, implanted a truly irresponsible amount of uranium into his body - alongside a radiotrophic fungus that grew on his skin, which was needed as the amount of radiation threatens to melt him from the inside without it. He now wears a suit with a number of implant-controlled vents, which he uses to make "Rad-Blasts" - which are as lovely as they sound.
Powers: Can produce virtually any gas-deliverable biotoxin, from hallucinogens to pheremones to nerve agents to sedatives. Can release blasts of radiation to kill normal humans and disable Superhuman healing factors.
Weaknesses: Despite his augmentation, he's not much stronger than a normal human - a single punch from Connor could reduce him to paste.


Owen Hooks - the Roach

Backstory: Like so many other poor unfortunates, Owen was snatched by the Artist to fuel his sick experiments. Unlike the others, however, Owen was a carrier for Fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva. The Artist's experiments activated this gene, and twisted its effects into something new...
Powers: The Roach is hands-down the toughest sonofabitch on Earth. In addition to his exoskeleton (which has the same properties as Connor's bones - that is, almost unbreakable), his nerves are multipurpose, allowing him to survive decapitation. He's also unique in that unlike Connor, his healing factor isn't stopped by radiation. Also unlike Connor, his metabolism is much slower on account of not having much soft tissue left. He will walk through any obstacle - rain or shine, snow or storm, desert or ocean - if he wants to get to you, he *will.*
Weaknesses: Aside from blindness caused by his eyes literally ossifying, he's in constant, horrendous agony - even worse than some of the Artist's  #other "masterpieces." He's insane, incredibly slow, in a permanent Rage, and barely able to register his surroundings due to all his sensory organs being calcified. However, his multipurpose nerves include sensory neurons, allowing him to still "see" and "hear" through his "skin" (and the scare-quotes are very much intentional.)


Michael Waters - the Wraith

Backstory: Similar to Owen, Michael was kidnapped by the Artist to 'play' with. The Artist wanted to see just how versatile the Morphing Suit could be - so he flayed Michael, kept him alive, and bonded a Morphing Suit to his skinned hide. From that, the Wraith was born.
Powers: The Wraith's morphing suit is the most advanced of its kind, bonded directly to his CNS. He can mimic other people, create intricate tools and weaponry, and form tougher material from it. His brainwashing included training on how to perfectly mimic individual habits, making him a perfect infiltrator.
Weaknesses: Under the suit, Michael is no tougher than a normal Super-Agent - his strength is inferior to Connor and he knows it.


Luke Miller - Blitz

Backstory: Miller was once a mercenary, working for International Exectuive Solutions PMC. His unit had been hired by a local warlord who'd carved himself a nice little fiefdom in the Caucuses in the wake of the Eurasian Wars. He developed a reputation for his gimmick of using several trick-grenades, including gas grenades, flashbangs, EMPs, and Incendiaries. During a routine patrol, he was attacked by a group of Super-Agents who were supporting rival warlord Vadim Zamaldayev. He was mortally wounded, but his fighting impressed ODESSA, who made him an offer he couldn't refuse - become a Super-Agent and lead the Broken, or become the Artist's next "Masterpiece." He chose the former without delay.
Powers: Aside from being a Super-Agent and an experienced combat veteran, has access to a number of gadgets, grenades, and firearms, making him a versatile combatant. In particular, he's an expert in using his grenades in tandem to take out mutant foes like Connor.
Weaknesses: He's still nothing stronger than an average Super-Agent - his CQC training means he can give Connor more trouble than a normal Agent, but in a straight fight with no tricks, he'd die in seconds.
