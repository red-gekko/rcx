Bloodhound Episode Splitter

Splits the WIP manuscript into per-episode files, organised by numbered volume, and bundles them into a timestamped ZIP.

📄
Drop the .txt file here, or click to browse
Expected: bloodhound story scenes-WIP.txt
How it works: The script looks for lines matching VOLUME - [name] and EPISODE - [name]. Everything between one episode header and the next is bundled into that episode's file. Episodes are numbered sequentially within each volume, and volumes are numbered sequentially in the order they appear.

Formatting: Within each episode, SCENE headers are placed at column 0, and scene content is indented with a single tab. Any deeper indentation from the source is collapsed to one tab to keep the reading clean. All processing happens in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
