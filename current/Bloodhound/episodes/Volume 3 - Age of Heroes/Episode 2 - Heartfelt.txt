It was close to sunset out in Texas. A couple of armed sentries stood outside a stately manor house, smoking.
"Ugh..." one groaned. "I still don't know why they posted us."
"Hey, beats bein' in there." the other chuckled. Then, they spotted a man approaching up the drive. In the dark, with the sun behind him, they couldn't make anything beyond a trenchcoat out.
"Can we help you, buddy?" the first called out. No response.
"Hey! You hard'a hearin', or somethin'?" the second barked.
The man flicked out his hand. A knife came tumbling from it, stapling the sentry to the wall.
Before the first could ready his gun, their attacker was on them, and a second knife slit his throat. He pulled his first knife out, and both sentries made noticeable thuds as they hit the ground.
The man grinned. He took off his coat, revealing a suit of bulletproof armour and a thick black mask, with two small white lenses over the eyes. He drew his pistols, and pushed the door open with his foot.
He lined up his shot perfectly, and fired. The first sentry in the hallroom hit the ground as his head popped apart.
The other guards scrambled to grab their guns. The attacker shot one, held his body up, and used it as a shield while he shot another. He dived into cover as more men rushed in, before shooting one more, pistol-whipping one next to him, firing out into the hallway at his last standing assailant, and then executed the bleeding, pistol-whipped man next to him.
Grinning, he holstered his gun, walking into a fancy office room.
The man inside - an older man, in a sweaty suit - threw his hands in the air. The attacker simply pulled his knife, and slashed.
There went the throat.
"This is Miller." he said into his radio. "Target down."

"Here's to the boss!" a big, bulky man cheered, popping open champagne.
"Go Miller! Go Miller!" another man - a soldier - chimed in.
Miller grinned, taking a glass of bubbly. He had black veins over his eyelids - a Super-Agent.
Their handler smirked, taking a glass for himself.
"Great work as always." he smiled. "Enjoy your money."
"Jackals! Jackals! Jackals!" the team chanted. The handler left, and made a call.
"Sir, the Jackals have done it again. Yeah, that's correct, Mr. Doherty's down. Collateral was... nothing we can't handle."
Some chatter came from the phone.
"Yes sir, that serum worked a treat. Miller's officially gone from assassin to killing machine. Business is booming. Still - would be good if we could get that one guy to join the team. The one from the Hattab job?"
More chatter.
"Yeah, I know, he was a little green. But still, take his track record on collateral damage. Plus, his powers... you tell him, if he's strapped for cash, he knows to call IES. Later, sir."
The phone cut off.

Then, a knock at the door.
Connor's eyes flashed red as his super-vision scanned the front. Then, he rushed over, and opened it.

Trey chuckled. "Well, buddy..."
