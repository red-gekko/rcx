"Though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil; for thou art with me... thy rod, and thy staff; they comfort me."
Ian stared a thousand paces into the distance. Wherever his mind was, he begged it wasn't *here.*
"We now commit Harriet's body to the ground." the priest said sombrely, as a small coffin was lowered down. "Earth to earth, ashes to ashes, dust to dust, in the sure and certain hope, of the resurrection to eternal life."
Davina had promised she'd stay strong for this. It was her daughter's day - taken far too soon. She was going to keep it together.
Instead, she was an inelegant, blubbering wreck. Of course she was.
Ian was the same, but different. Everyone in the service seemed to have that feeling. Either shellshocked numbness, or uncontrollable sorrow - neither was what they'd said they would do. And it was all they could do. The pain was just too much.

Come the wake, Ian sat on his chair, frozen in time. His pint had been untouched - he hadn't drank since it happened.
He wanted to feel it. Pain, Sorrow. Agony. *Hate.* Nothing would dull it.
"Hi, Ian..." an older relative began, patting his back gently. "I'm... I'm so sorry about Harriet."
Ian initially didn't respond. Not even a flicker. He just stared at a photograph of her, like he was trying to telepathically animate it.
"It's okay... whatever you're feeling right now, it's okay." the older man said, softly and warm.
Again, Ian didn't react. Then, he took a deep breath.
"I keep thinking about the weather." he began, his voice hoarse and choked.
"The... weather?"
"Today was perfect for it. Clouds, rain, freezing cold. I mean, it's nearly March now, right? Of course it's grim."
"I... don't follow."
"It was the wrong *fucking* day for it!" Ian snapped, slamming his fist on the bar. "Snowing! A beautiful snow day! She... she loved the snow..."
The room went quiet.
"Sorry..." he whispered, tears streaming down his cheeks. The old man embraced him.
"No, it's okay, lad. Let it all out."
"I just... it should've been me." he finally said. "And it should've been *fuckin'* raining."
