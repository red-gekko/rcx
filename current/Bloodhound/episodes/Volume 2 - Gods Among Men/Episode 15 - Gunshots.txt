EPISODE - Gunshots
============================================================

It was a bitter, cold day in Drackhill. Rain pattered against the roof of a covert building in the east of Belson.
Within, the Commandant had met with Gregory Morland. He was *not* happy.
"YOU HAD YOUR CHANCE, YOU WORM!"
"Commandant, sir... with respect, it was your decision to let the Werewolf escape captivity."
"WHAT?!"
Morland shrank into himself. "I... I only meant that the attack on our facility was a natural outcome of our overarching mission..."
"You are skating on thin ice with me, Morland. Wafer thin. An entire shipment of serum, gone! The production facility, destroyed! Our plans in RUINS!"
"Rest assured, Commandant, I will do everything I can to find out how he was able to access our lab... and I will restart production as soon as possible."
"No. Now we do things my way." the Commandant growled. "We need the data from your labs."
"I will have it sent over, Commandant. It will take a few days to avoid audits, but..."
"ENOUGH! Take him away."
Morland begged for mercy as the guards dragged him out. A Super-Agent stepped forward. His black veins pulsed with mutant strength.
"Agent Banner." the Commandant nodded.
"Your orders, Commandant?" he asked.
"Go to the labs. Take what we need, and cause a scene. Take the scientists hostage. I think I know who did this... you will take the one known as Chloe Derrington, and bring her away. Let our wayward friend come running to us."
"As you wish, Commandant. Hail Victory." Banner nodded.
"Hail Victory." the Commandant nodded back.

That evening, Chloe was working in the lab with Jones.
"Mondays, eh?" she smirked.
"Did you hear?" he began. "Apparently the Werewolf was here a last night. Trashed the labs downstairs."
"Wow, really?" Chloe asked, trying to act surprised. "That's crazy, I was here yesterday. What did he break?"
"No idea. You know what downstairs are like - need to know, etc."
"Hah, you can say that again." Chloe chuckled. With that, an alarm went off.
"Shit - fire? Okay, let's go." Jones said hurriedly. Chloe nodded and left with him. They headed to the main hallway, where other staff had gathered, only to hear a noise - gunshots.
"Fuck! Hide!" Jones barked, only for armed men to pour in.
"NOBODY FUCKING MOVE!" the men roared. Banner approached. His eyes were even more blackveined than the average Super-Agent - a sign that this one had been twisted far beyond the norm.
"Now, who here goes by the name Chloe Derrington?" he asked. No one answered.
"No? Okay. Number 5, shoot that man there." he said. The other Agent readied his gun.
"WAIT!" Chloe interrupted. "It's me. I'm Chloe Derrington."
"Ah! Lovely to meet you at last. You're coming with me. The rest of you, my men will look after you." he grinned. His soldiers grabbed the other scientists and forced them to their knees. He dragged Chloe with him, heading up a flight of stairs.

At the same time, Connor was sat in the basement, on his laptop reviewing some files.
Shannon giggled. "Have you guys seen the forums?"
"Hm?" Sparky tilted his head.
"Ever since Zack put out that documentary on us, people have been speculating - and now, there's a forum. Apparently, there's a massive rift between 'Team Stunlocke' and 'Team Whiplash.'"
"What about Team Werewolf?" Connor asked meekly.
"Everyone's Team Werewolf. They're arguing over who to ship you with."
Connor and Amy exchanged looks, then gagged.
Shannon grinned. "There's fanfic, too. Apparently, people think I'm an AI construct, rather than, you know, a hacker. And... oh, God."
"What now?" Connor groaned.
"There's toxic Yaoi of you and Ivan." she giggled. "It's been marked NSFW - oh, this is good. They think Verdict and Slamshot are gay lovers."
"Eugh... if I was gonna go for a man, 'middle-aged Russian dude' wouldn't be top of the list." Connor shuddered.
"I dunno... that beard sure is fetching on him..." Shannon mused, before reading aloud. "Slamshot gently reached out, as Verdict stroked his chin. 'Oh, is that for me?' he asked, before gingerly unzipping--"
"That's it. I'm becoming a supervillain." Connor grimaced.
"You think they *don't* get slash-fics written about them?" Shannon cocked an eyebrow.
"...I am *so* glad I'm a dog." Sparky quipped. "You guys are *weird.*"
Then, an alert.
"Oh, shit!" Shannon gasped.
"Please, tell me it's Yuri of me and Gwen." Amy snarked. "Hey, don't look at me like that."
"Reports just came in - there's an attack going on in Vitalis."
"WHAT?!" Connor gasped.
"Let's go." Sparky barked firmly.
"Agreed." Amy nodded, rushing to her suit.
"Amy... this'll be dangerous." Connor warned.
"I don't care. If Chloe's in danger, I'm going." she snapped, drawing the Vibroblade.
Shannon approached, carrying a large, boxy device. It looked like a flare gun - oversized barrel, angular frame, thick, insulted grip - with a bulky rear housing mounted at the back.
"I call it the Pulsar." she explained. "It's still a prototype - I got the schematics off of a hack on Eidolon's servers. Anyway - it hooks up to your suit's battery. You can stun people at short range with it, or short-circuit electrical equipment."

"You'd best get going." Shannon said. "Ivan's out of town at the moment - and Gwen's not on shift tonight. It's just you guys."
"Let's do this." Sparky growled lowly.
Amy finished suiting up, and hopped onto her motorbike.
"I'll race you." she quipped, putting on her helm.
Connor would normally snark back. Instead he nodded, and morphed his glider. It inflated as he leapt into the air.
*THWUMP!*
The Werewolf ripped into the sky, his eyes aflame.

As they moved, Connor felt his phone ring.
"Connor! Look at the news!" Trey began.
"I know! I'm on my way!"
"Good man. Look, don't worry about your secret, okay? Just get there, and save Chloe."
Connor paused for a split second. "I will." he vowed, and hung up. It rang again.

"For fuck's sake..." he muttered before answering. "Hello?"
"Bratan - big news."

"Werewolf." Naismith nodded. The armed officers beside aimed their guns at him.
"Hold your fire!" he barked.

Upon entering, he saw the bodies of security guards who'd been shot. This wasn't America - security guards were unarmed. The Agents just wanted to kill someone. The heroes climbed the stairs.
"Let's get these bastards." Sparky growled, his tail wafting in the air.
The pair burst into a squad of Super-Agents. The Agents opened fire, but the heroes were on them in an instant.
The dark urge beckoned. Connor grinned at it, agreeing silently.
The fight was brutal. Connor ripped a knife from one Agent and used it to slit the man's throat before turning on another. Sparky tore through two more in one swipe.
Amy charged forward, the bullets pinging and ricocheting off of her suit. She ripped out the Pulsar, and fired.
The weapon kicked in her hand with a sharp capacitor whine. At the muzzle, a compressed pulse of ionised gas burst into existence, wreathed in flickering red and white arcs. The projectile shot forward, trailing a shimmering tunnel through the air. For a split millisecond, the ionised channel connected the barrel to the Agent's body - then the discharge hit.
A jagged arc of electricity cracked down the channel and slammed into the Agent, locking his body rigid. He convulsed violently, before Sparky ripped off his head.
Connor grabbed a grenade from a fallen Agent's belt, stuffing it into the man's mouth and kicking him into another, killing them both.
Finally, a swipe from the Vibroblade opened a sucking hole on the last Agent's chest. Connor's punch went right through.
With the carnage over, Connor dug a bullet from his hide, his breath ragged.
"Spartacus. Go downstairs. Kill them, and secure the labs. I'll go to the hostages." Connor ordered.
Sparky barked in acknowledgment, running off to carry out his task.
"Stunlocke - you okay?"
She looked up. "Y-yeah."
He gently approached. "Do you need to pull out?"
"No. I need to kill these bastards."
"Me too."
They continued upward, finding the hostages knelt down in the hallway. As they stepped in, a Super-Agent revealed himself.
"STOP RIGHT THERE!" he barked.
Connor rushed past him, slamming into a soldier and spraying his guts on the ground.
Amy's Pulsar stunned one, but a shotgun blast knocked her to the side. Connor sent his fist through the shooter, then his claw through another.
One Agent got a lucky shot, and the bullet slammed into the side of Connor's head, sending him stumbling. A crossbow morphed from his wrist and he shot his attacker dead. He shook his head to clear the fog.
"My god... you..." one of the scientists began.
"Stay here. Are there any more hostages?" he asked, his voice trembling.
"TRAITOR!" a voice screamed over the intercom - Banner's voice. "LISTEN AND OBEY!"
At those words, Connor shuddered.
"COME TO THE ROOFTOP. WE HAVE YOUR ACCOMPLICE." the voice boomed. "COME ALONE, OR SHE DIES."
Connor's hearts stopped. He froze in place.
A voice came over the intercom again. "Don't worry about me! Just save the people down below!" Chloe shouted. Her voice was interrupted by a thud and a yelp of pain.
"HAH! SO NOBLE... NOW COME, OR SHE DIES!" the voice screamed again.
Connor heard a ringing in his ears. Time slowed to a crawl. Hot blood poured from his nose. He shook his head.
"I'm coming, Chloe." he whispered.

Connor ascended the stairs, taking a moment to un-morph his mask and wipe his nose. The bullet to the head didn't crack his skull, but it still hurt.
He had to save her. He *had* to.
"Stunlocke - escort the hostages out of the reception. I'm going up." he said over the radio.
"Copy that. There's a few wounded here - I'll apply first aid, and join Spartacus when I'm done." she audibly nodded. "Are you gonna be okay?"
"I have to be. Chloe needs me."
"Vanguard - this is Whiplash. I took to the sky as soon as I heard the news."
"You're a voice for sore ears." Connor smiled. "What's your status?"
"I'm gliding over the roof with Blindspot. There's an Agent holding someone at gunpoint. Want me to intervene?"
"No - he'll kill her if anyone other than me shows up. Maintain aerial obs, but do not engage."
"Copy that."
He took a deep breath, re-morphed his mask, and headed up to the rooftop. There, he saw Banner, clad in armour, pointing his gun at Chloe.
A flurry of snow breezed from above, as Gwen rode an updraft out of sight. It melted as it touched him, and he let out a breath of hot steam. His eyes burned like coals from Hell.
"Ah! Welcome back, Traitor!" Banner laughed.
Connor responded by morphing a crossbow on his wrist, and aimed it directly at Banner's head. It was a message - if you hurt her, you will have half a second to regret it.
"*LET. HER. GO.*" he roared, his voice slamming into Banner's chest like a sonic weapon.
Chloe thought to herself - if that voice ordered her to jump from that rooftop, she would. She shook her head, glad that it was directed at Banner instead.
Banner sneered, clearly rattled but not broken. He straightened, and chuckled darkly. "Hah... nice try."
He holstered his weapon with a deliberate slowness, as if dismissing Connor's power. "You think you can intimidate me with your little tricks?"
"*I'm not here to intimidate you. I'm hear to coat my boot with your brain.*" Connor snarled.
"Come then, Traitor - show me your wrath! SHOW ME YOUR RAGE!"
Connor's eyes glowed a deep, furious red. His muscles tightened, rippling with supernatural strength. With a primal roar, he charged.
He threw a punch with such force that the air seemed to crack around it - aided, no doubt, by Gwen's power from above. Banner barely managed to block, but the impact sent him sliding back. Connor pressed the attack, launching another punch, then a devastating uppercut that could take a human's head off. Each swing felt like it could tear through steel, each blow a promise of death.
Chloe looked on, horrified and transfixed by the scene. But only one thought was in her mind.
In his Rage, Connor didn't bother to hide his voice. Sure, he may have been shouting with a supernatural growl, but still...
"That voice..." she whispered, trembling - not wanting to realise the implications.
Banner was no amateur. He was fast, and brutal. He counterattacked with a kick so vicious it seemed to explode from his body.
The impact landed directly in Connor's chest, and a sickening crunch filled the air. The force knocked him back, and he stumbled, gasping for air. Blood dripped from his mouth.
Chloe, her heart pounding, saw an opportunity. She tried to run, but in a split second, Banner's hand whipped out, throwing a knife with deadly precision. It sailed through the air towards her. Time seemed to slow as she twisted, barely avoiding the blade. It was a sign to stay put.
Connor's Rage flared. His fists, still trembling from the blow, morphed out a blade. With a guttural roar, he lunged, stabbing Banner in the thigh. The blade sank deep, but Banner grinned through the pain. He grabbed the blade and twisted it free. Without hesitation, he plunged it into Connor's abdomen.
The agony was unbearable - Connor could feel the blade scrape against his ribs, tearing through muscle and flesh. Blood poured from the wound, but his resolve only hardened.
The Rage groaned, spurring him onwards.
Undaunted, he gritted his teeth, his fingers tightening around Banner's blade. With a feral snarl, he shifted, forcing himself forward along the blade. The two warriors were locked in a brutal embrace, each one trying to kill the other. Connor morphed claws on his hands, slashing wildly. Banner screamed as the blades cut across his face, sending a spray of blood into the air. He staggered back, but Connor followed, relentless.
His stomach heaved as his eyes burned bright.
*HAWK-ptoo!*
Acid sizzled over Banner's face, burning out an eye and turning the skin red. He fell backwards, ripping the blade out of Connor's gut before dropping it.
The Werewolf snarled in fury.
He had to finish this. He had to save Chloe.
The Rage roared, spurring him on. His constant humming migraine faded for a moment - even his flesh wanted this man dead.
Banner scowled, and jumped on his back. Connor stumbled as Banner's knife sank into his throat.
Without another breath of hesitation, he picked up Banner's machete and ran himself through, impaling both.
For a moment, there was silence, save for the ragged breaths of two men on the verge of death. Blood pooled around them, dark and thick.
They both fell away from each other, and struggled to stand up, blood pouring from their chests and mouths. Connor planted the blade in the ground, and used it to help him stand.
Chloe rushed to him, her eyes wide with horror and relief. She helped him to his feet, but just as they thought they had a moment of calm, Banner drew his gun again, aiming it directly at her head.
"Ah, ah, ah!" Banner laughed. He motioned for Chloe to step away, and she reluctantly did so. Connor growled, like a true Werewolf.
Banner approached and grabbed her, holding the gun to her head.
"Nice try. But the heart always was your weakness, traitor." he laughed. "On your knees."
Connor sank down.

"Connor... CONNOR!" she screamed.

"Wh-who are you?!" she stammered.
"Call me Whiplash." Gwen nodded. "I work with Connor."

Not a lie. So, that's how he does it.
