SCENE - Chloe in hospital post-OD

	Autumn, 2012. Not long after it. Chloe was fifteen years old.
	Her scar was present - and far, far worse than the present day. It looked as though someone had taken a carving knife from her left eye down to the corner of her jawbone. She stared a thousand yards ahead, her expression vacant.
	It had only recently happened.
	...No, not 'happened.' Cancer 'happens.' This was *done* to her.
	Her arms were wrapped in fresh bandages, and in front of her was an assortment of pills.
	Downstairs, William was sat at the table, rubbing his temples. Catherine was reading the bible.
	Sveta came in from Oliver's bedroom. She sat down with them.
	"I'm worried about her." William finally began.
	"Of course." Sveta nodded sympathetically. "The poor girl..."
	"What good am I?" he lamented. "I--I don't know how to talk to her about it. Every time I try, she just starts crying."
	"Then let her cry." Sveta replied. "If that is what she needs, then let her. Just... be there with her."
	William nodded, smiling. "Thank you, Sveta."
	At that, Catherine scowled slightly. "We pay you for nursing, not to tell us how to raise our daughter, Ms. Popova."
	Sveta rolled her eyes out of sight.
	William's ears pricked up. He could hear Molly whimpering and pawing at the door.
	He stood, and went upstairs. He saw Molly frantically digging at the carpet.
	"Molly, enough!" he snapped. She whined, and carried on anyway.
	He sighed, and knocked on the door. "Chloe, darling? Can... can we talk?"
	Silence.
	He knocked again. Molly barked nervously.
	"Chloe? Are you in there?"
	Again, silence.
	"I'm coming in." he said, opening the door. He entered, and saw the room was empty.
	Sveta and Catherine arrived too.

	"Chloe, darling? Are you in there?"

	"I... alright, sweetheart. We'll be downstairs when you're ready to talk."

	"Sveta... can you try talking to her? She... Chloe looks up to you."
	"Of course." Sveta nodded. Catherine scoffed as the parents went downstairs.
	She walked over, and sat down by the door, giving Molly a comforting rub on the ears.
	"Chloe, dorogaya? Ty v poryadke?"
	Silence, once more.
	"Are you alright in there?"
	Again, nothing.
	"Chloe... can you please make a noise, so I know you're okay?"
	Still nothing. Molly whimpered.
	Sveta closed her eyes, tensed, and breathed out.
	"Chloe... I'm going to come in, okay? Please don't be afraid." she called out. She pulled out her credit card, and used it to flick the lock on the door.

	"Bozhe moy!" she shrieked, pouncing on Chloe. She checked her pulse - it was barely there. 20 BPM, tops.

	"Call an ambulance!" Sveta barked, beginning CPR.
	William pulled out his phone, and did so. Catherine walked in the bathroom, pale as a ghost. She looked down at the counter.
	Empty pill bottles - anticonvulsants, antidepressants, vitamin C, ibuprofen... her entire insulin needle had been used up, too. And next to it all...

	She looked at it, looked over at Chloe, and her eyes flared.

	Sveta rushed over. "Shh, dorogaya... don't speak."
	She looked around, and realised she was in a hospital bed. William and Catherine were stood there. William looked forlorn. Catherine looked away.

	"Hi, Chloe." the nurse began. "How are you feeling?"

	"I-I'm c-c-cold." she stammered.

	"Can you follow my finger?" she asked. Chloe did so.
	The nurse shook her head. "Are you struggling to see?"
	"N-no..." she whispered.

	"Yes?"
	"Can I have a word?"

	"W-what?"
	"I saw the pregnancy test, Chloe." she scowled.
	Chloe cried. "I-I h-had t-t-t--"
	"You're a baby-killer." Catherine snarled. She stood.
	"Mum... p-please..." Chloe begged.
	Catherine paused, before storming away. Chloe sobbed deeply, her whole body shaking in pain.

SCENE - Bea and Chloe argue/Troll and Goblin move in

	Well - he'd seen cellars. He'd seen a lot of cellars, actually, and most of them had been cold and dark and far too small for him. But this one was different.
	It had shelves, jars, and stinky barrels. Books, wafers, and brass objects.

He'd passed through here, as he climbed out of the catacombs beneath - but now, he really got a chance to take a look. After all, 'Mr. Dad,' as the blueskin knew him, was working upstairs with Sister Mary - so he and his beloved brother had to hide, just for a moment.
