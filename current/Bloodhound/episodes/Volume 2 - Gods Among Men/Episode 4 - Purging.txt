SCENE - Killing Laverty

	It was mid-2005, and a young Connor was sat in a police room. He was wearing plain clothes that were far too big for him - and his hands were covered in dried blood. He was flanked by two middle-aged women - a solicitor and a safeguarding officer. He stared into the middle distance, unaware of his surroundings.
	"Connor." the solicitor's voice echoed, snapping him into reality.
	"Connor. Officer Flanagan was asking you a question?" she said softly.
	Connor looked up at the officer.
	"Can you describe, in your own words, what happened this evening?" the officer asked.
	Connor looked back into the middle distance.

	Connor and Amy sat at the pickup point of their primary school.
	"Are you okay?" he asked her. His accent was distinctively more Irish than the present day.
	"I'm fine." she forced herself to nod.
	"Okay." Connor smiled, oblivious. A car pulled up, and a middle-aged man came out. Amy shuddered as he approached.
	"Right, see you when you're done with Nia, Connor." the man said. "Amelia, come on. I'm on duty this evening, so I reckon you and I can get up to some fun!"
	Amy shuddered at that - at the news that she'd be home alone with him. "Okay, Mr. Laverty."
	"I've told you, sweetheart - call me James." he said, putting his hand on Amy's back. She tensed at his touch.
	Connor smiled and nodded, but even he had alarm bells ringing in the back of his mind.
	Amy reluctantly entered the car.

	A short while later, another car pulled up. A large middle-aged woman came out.
	"Hi Connor!" she smiled warmly.
	"Hi, Nia." Connor waved. He entered the car, and the two drove to a nearby cafe. There, Vince was waiting. He stood, and Connor rushed over, hugging him.
	"Vince!" Connor shouted in excitement.
	"Hey, my lad..." Vince said, smiling warmly.
	"Hi, Vince!" Nia waved.
	"Thanks for sorting this, Nia." Vince nodded gratefully.
	Nia headed to the counter and got Connor a hot chocolate. He sipped it absently, staring off into the distance.
	"So, how's school?" Vince asked.
	"It's okay. I was confused in RE though."
	"Ahh, I always got confused in RE too." Vince grinned. "What's the mix-up?"
	"I don't understand it. So, they were telling us that the head of the church is the King. But I thought it was the pope? Also, they say the Lord's prayer weird."
	Vince chuckled. "Well, my lad... you go to a Church of England school. They're... they're a little bit different to us Catholics."
	"You mean they're Prods?"
	Vince winced slightly.
	"Connor... it's not nice to call them that word." he began. "I know you had... bad experiences... with Protestants, growing up, but over here, Protestant just means a different faith to ours - nothing more than that."
	Connor sipped his hot chocolate, taking in the thought.
	"So, Connor." Nia chimed in. "How do you think you're getting on, staying in Ashcliffe House?"
	He shrugged. "I still don't understand why I can't stay with Vince."
	"Oh, sweetheart..." Nia sighed. Vince gave him a sad look, and placed his hand on his shoulder.
	"Hey, lad... I know it's confusing. But it's only temporary, okay? Once all the paperwork gets sorted, you'll come to live with me. And until then, I'll make sure I always come to these meetings, okay?"
	"Thanks." Connor smiled. "I like Amy, anyway. She's funny. And she knows what it's like to grow up without a mum. A lot of the other kids have mums, so it's nice to have someone else who doesn't."
	Vince smiled sadly. "How about the staff? Do you like them?"
	"Mrs. Kulwin is nice. And so's Mr. Ekwensi. And Ms. Patel. But... there's something off about Mr. Laverty."
	Nia made a note on her paper. "What do you think is wrong with Mr. Laverty?" she asked.
	"He's moody." Connor shrugged. "And he changes a lot. One moment he's all friendly, the next he's grumpy, and the next he's annoying."
	Nia nodded sympathetically. "It can be hard to adjust, especially coming from your situation."
	Connor nodded, and sipped his drink absently.
	"You're quiet today." Vince tilted his head sympathetically. "Is everything okay?"
	"Some boys at school were being mean about my accent." he sighed.
	"That's horrible." Nia said sadly. "I'm sorry, Connor. Some kids just don't realise it's wrong to treat others like that."
	Vince stroked Connor's back affectionately.
	"Amy seemed a little sad as well."
	"She did?" Nia asked, looking worried. "That's a shame. Do you know why she's feeling sad?"
	"I don't know." he shrugged. An idea crossed his mind. "Can I please buy her a brownie from the counter? I think that would cheer her up."
	Nia smiled. "Of course, darling. I'll get one to go. Tell you what - why don't we go home early, and we can surprise her?"
	Connor hesitated. "Can Vince come?"
	"Of course! Visitation is for 2 hours - it doesn't matter where."
	Connor nodded, and smiled.

	Ashcliffe Children's Home.
	Founded in late 1997 to keep up with 'increased demand' - the outbreak of maternal deaths the preceding year meant a lot of children needed somewhere to stay.
	They rarely stayed long - except for one.
	Amy Zhao. One of three kids to survive their birth during the incident. No known family, she'd lived there for several years now. She'd seen many carers come and go. Some she liked, some not so much.
	One, she hated. Feared. Despised.

	"I... I don't want to." she protested, trembling.
	"Do as you are told." Laverty growled.
	"Please, Mr. Laverty, I--"
	He grabbed her hair, and she cried silently.
	Before he could say or do anything else, he heard a knock at the door.
	"Stay here." he snarled. "If you leave this room, I'll kill you. Do you understand?!"
	"Y-yes, Mr. Laverty." she nodded through trembled breaths.
	He let out a sick smile, and headed downstairs.
	"Hi, Mr. Laverty. Connor wanted to pop back round to see Amy?" Nia began.
	"That's very thoughtful, Connor." Laverty nodded, faking a smile. "Amy's feeling really poorly today though, I'm afraid. I've put her to bed, and she mustn't be disturbed."
	"Oh..." Connor frowned. "Can you give her these when she wakes up?" he asked, holding up the bag of treats.
	Vince beamed. "Don't worry, lad - you can give them to her yourself when we get back."
	"*If* she's feeling better." Laverty hurriedly added. Vince rolled his eyes.
	"Alright, well tell Amy we hope she gets well soon." Nia smiled. "Connor, why don't me and Vince take you to the park for the rest of the afternoon?"
	"Oh, yes please!" Connor beamed. They headed back to the car, and Laverty closed the door. He turned around, and looked at Amy's door. A deep breath passed, and he went back upstairs.

	"Higher! Higher!" Connor demanded.
	Vince grinned, pushing the swing even further than before.
	"More!" the little dictator barked. Vince obliged, shoving again.
	The swing launched up, coming dangerously close to looping back around. Thankfully for Vince's blood pressure, it came sailing back down, and Connor giggled as it came to a halt.
	"Phew! I thought you were gonna go flying into space there, lad." Vince chuckled, ruffling Connor's hair.
	"I'd be an astronaut!" Connor noted, smiling widely. "Can I go play on the slide?"
	"Of course you can, lad. I'm gonna go sit down for a minute and talk to Nia, okay?"
	"Alright! See you in a minute!" Connor cheered, racing away.
	Nia chuckled, sipping her water.
	"He's a good kid." Vince said proudly.
	"He sure is." Nia smiled. "At the risk of sounding unprofessional... I think it was a mistake for them to deny you custody rights."
	Vince smiled at hearing that. "Well... let's just hope these visits evolve into that, eh?"
	"Still... I think it's really good for Amy, as well. The poor girl's been bounced from home to home... I'll admit, that was part of the reason we decided to place him in Ashcliffe specifically. Both of them really needed each other."
	Vince nodded sadly. "Connor said it himself - it's nice to have a friend who's been through the same thing."
	"Well, not completely the same." Nia began. "But you've helped him so much. When I read his history, I must admit I was worried - but he's remarkably well-adjusted, all things considered."
	Vince looked at Connor, his eyes softening.

	"You're doing really well, Connor." Officer Flanagan said calmly.
	He stared downwards. "I don't like this next bit."
	"It's alright." the solicitor began, going to put her hand on his shoulder. The safeguarding officer silently batted her hand away, shaking her head.
	Connor looked up at both women, and nodded in appreciation, before continuing.

	It was late, now. Despite the rules, Connor hand managed to get an extra hour of visitation. Vince hugged his nephew tightly, and dropped him back off at Ashcliffe.
	"Sleep well, lad. I'll see you on Thursday, yeah?"
	Connor nodded sadly, and hugged him back. Then, he went inside.
	"Hello, Mr. Laverty." he waved halfheartedly.
	"It's late." he muttered, not looking up from his book. "You should be in bed."
	"How's Amy feeling?"
	"She's still not well. Don't disturb her."
	"Okay."
	Connor softly walked up the stairs. He went to his room, but then stopped. He turned, and tried Amy's door.
	He pushed, but it wouldn't budge - locked.
	"What did I just tell you?!" Laverty snapped, grabbing Connor's arm.
	"Sorry." Connor whispered, looking away.
	"Go to bed. *Now.*"
	Connor nodded, and headed to his room.
	He sat for a while, deep in thought. He pressed his ear to the wall, and heard Laverty descend.
	His eyes darted side-to-side. Then he crawled over to his window, and opened it.
	The breeze was cold and bitter. He hesitated, but a look of determination crossed his face. He held the bag of brownies in his mouth, and crawled out.

	Connor was far, far from the agile superhuman he would one day be horrifically reshaped into. But that didn't matter - Amy was alone, and he was worried about her. He gripped onto the ledge as strong as he could, and shuffled his way over. Finally, he reached her window, and gingerly tapped on it thrice.
	Amy was shook from her stupor, and crawled over to it. She looked out, gasped, and slid her window open, grabbing his hand to help him in.
	"What are you doing?" she whispered.
	Connor released his jaw, handing over the bag. "I wanted to get you a brownie."
	She tilted her head incredulously, before wrapping him in a hug.
	She couldn't hold it any more - the tears poured out.
	"What's wrong?" he asked, confused. "Mr. Laverty said you were sick."
	"*He's* sick..." she hissed.
	"What do you mean?"
	"Nothing!" she quickly added. "J-just forget I said anything."
	"Amy... you can tell me."
	"He said if I tell you, he'd do it to you, too."
	Connor pulled his head back. "Do *what* to me?"

	Laverty turned the page of his book, reading intently. The fireplace had been on, but was now dim embers. The poker rested next to it.
	His focus was interrupted by the sound of small feet descending the stairs.
	He looked up, and rolled his eyes.
	"Go to bed, Connor."
	He didn't reply. Instead, he walked into the living room, his pupils constricted.
	"What? Do you want something?"
	"Did you hurt Amy?"
	Laverty's eyes widened, and his lip curled. "Wh-what?"
	"Did you hurt Amy?"
	He looked away. "Go back to bed."
	"Did. You. Hurt. Amy?"
	"I said, go. To. Bed." he rose, scowling.
	"Not until you answer me. Did you hurt Amy?"
	"You two need to get a grip. Your imaginations must be getting the better of--"
	"DID YOU, OR DIDN'T YOU?!" Connor screamed.
	Immediately, he felt a backhand clip across his face. He fell to the ground, and clutched his cheek.
	"You... ungrateful little shits." he snarled. "I look after the pair of you, after you kill your own mothers, and this is how you repay me?!"
	He went to strike again. Connor grabbed the incoming wrist with all his little strength, and bit down on a finger.
	Laverty roared, slamming his head into the floor. Then a kick to the chest - and then another. And another.
	"Please, let him go! Don't hurt him!" Amy begged.
	"You! What did you tell him?!"
	"I--I--" she stammered, shrinking back.
	Laverty sent another kick - this one to Connor's head - and Amy snapped. She pounced, jumping onto his back like a raging monkey, and tried to emulate a chokehold.
	Laverty snarled, and slammed his back against the wall. The air knocked from her lungs.
	"YOU LITTLE BITCH!" he roared, slamming her to the ground and wrapping his hands around her throat.
	Connor forced himself to stay conscious. Hot blood dripped from his head and nostrils.
	He closed his eyes, as his heartbeat drummed in his head.

	"After that, I can't remember anything." he shrugged.
	"You have to try." Flanagan replied.
	"Officer Flanagan." the safeguarding officer stepped in. "Connor had a blackout after seeing what he saw. Ask the other witnesses."
	Flanagan nodded softly. "Of course. All right."
	"Am I in trouble?" Connor asked timidly.
	Flanagan hesitated. "I, um... based on what you and the others have told me, I don't think there's any reason to press charges. I'll consult with the CPS, but... I can't see any chance of them seeking to charge you. You'll be held here for a maximum of 5 more hours."
	Connor sighed, and looked down. "I can't remember it clearly... but..."
	Flanagan leant in, taking notes.

	Amy looked up in terror. All she saw was *his* face. Her vision was going blurry, and her chest burned. She kicked as hard as she could, but he only tightened his grip.
	Desperately, she reached her tiny arms up, and dug her thumbs into his eyes. He screamed in agony as blood dripped down, and throttled her harder.
	Her eyes started to roll back, and close. It was getting dark, now.
	Part of her was glad. It would all be over soon.

	*SHIK-SQUELCH!*

	Her heart was trembling. But she could feel the death grip weaken.
	Her eyes were still shut, and she forced herself to open them.
	Blood dripped from Laverty's head - lots of blood. His expression was contorted - still twitching, even. Like a pig right after slaughter.
	Connor's chest heaved. He gripped the poker's handle tight, and his eyes were fixated on the sharp end, pointing out from the top of Laverty's skull. Bits of bone, brain, and scalp had stuck to it.
	The walls gave way. Fragments of tooth and flesh shook freely from his head, and he collapsed. Amy rolled out of the way, as Connor released the poker. He stood there for a moment, covered in blood - Laverty's, his own - before sitting down, almost tranquil.
	Amy shakily crawled over, and sat with him. Both stared at the body, their expressions vacant. Time passed - maybe it was a few seconds, maybe it was a million years. It was impossible to tell.

	"Hello? This is the police. We've received reports of an altercation - we need to come in. Can you open the door, please?" a voice called from outside.
	Neither child moved. They didn't even hear it.
	"This is the police! Open up, now!"
	No. Still nothing.
	"Alright, we're going to break the door down. Stand clear!"
	The battering ram smacked twice, and the door gave way. Officers entered, and went pale at the sight.

	Connor walked out of the interview room, and sat next to Amy. She paused, and rested her head on his shoulder.
	"Thank you..." she whispered to him.
	Connor just nodded, still in shock. Finally, he spoke.
	"You don't have to thank me." he said softly.
	"You saved me." she replied, almost confused.
	"You're the one who pulled him off me." he retorted.
	"I... I don't know where that came from. I just saw him hit you, and I snapped." she shrugged. "Just like you did for me, I suppose."
	"I didn't mind him hitting me. I minded what he was doing to you." Connor nodded.
	She smiled softly. "*I* minded him hitting you."
	He paused. "I minded it a little bit."
	She chuckled, then giggled happily. He did too, and they let the moment sit.
	"You're a good big brother, you know." she finally said.
	Connor tilted his head, confused. "I'm not your big brother."
	"Yeah, you are." she smiled. He shrugged, then nodded.

	Across the room, Vince was arguing with a white-haired woman, with Nia stood next to them.
	"The decision was made due to your criminal record, Mr. Barrett." the white-haired woman sneered.
	"With all due respect, shut the fuck up." Vince snapped. "You let my nephew - the only living member of my family - in a house with that nonce?!"
	"Vince, please - calm down." Nia stepped in.
	"I will not be calm until I receive written confirmation that Connor will be staying with me."
	"Mr. Barrett, as well you know, there are rules in place. Your home must be found suitable, and a social worker *independent* of the case must verify you are safe for Connor to live with." the woman snapped.
	"'Safe to live with'... are you shitting me?!" Vince growled.
	"Clearly, Connor is now a complex-needs child." the woman continued.
	"He saved Amy from a paedo! A paedo *you* put them with!" Vince snapped.
	"That's enough, Mr. Barrett! Shouting like Ian Paisley isn't going to do you any favours." the woman snapped back.
	Vince snarled, before storming off to sit with the kids.
	"Hey, you two." he began, smiling softly. "How you holding up?"
	"Where are we going to go now?" Amy asked, forlorn.
	"I'm not sure, darlin'." Vince sighed. "Hopefully, Connor will be allowed to stay with me."
	Amy looked down, saddened. "I'm scared, Vince. I don't want to live with someone like Mr. Laverty again." she sobbed.
	Vince sighed deeply. "I'm sorry, Amy. I..." he hesitated.
	"We can't leave her. Not after this." Connor finally spoke. "She's my sister."
	Vince looked at Connor. He sighed, and nodded.
	"You're right, lad." he said. He looked down, then looked both kids in the eyes.
	"Listen, you're both probably gonna be put in temporary housing. I'll visit you both every day - and if anything like this happens again, you tell me right away." he said. "I'm gonna move heaven and earth to have you both come live with me."
	The kids looked up, and smiled.
	For the first time in a long time, Amy felt safe again.

SCENE - Infiltration

	"Me too." Connor nodded, chuckling.
	"I've done it before." Tommy grinned. "Me and Vince, right before the Rexton job."
	"Me, too." Ivan smirked. "My first assignment in the KGB - me and some comrades in Abkhazia blew up a separatist depot."
	"Great, so I'm the superhero here, and I've not done it yet." Connor snarked. "Let's hear it."
	Shannon grinned, and revealed her document - a blueprint floorplan.
	"This is the layout of the Gambit field lab." she began. "Like our now-hospitalised friend Jimmy said, it's a barn conversion."
	"Paladin work through those, a lot." Connor nodded. "When they tortured me, they held me in one. Him, too."
	"I guess they really like barns." Sparky shrugged.
	"Oh, I'm so sorry." she said sadly.
	"It's fine. Let's just figure out our plan."

One constraint - make sure to stay undercover. If they realise they've been duped, they'll call off the shipment, and we might never find them."

	"Stop pacing." his superior snapped. "The last thing we need is to look unprofessional in front of them."
	"They're here!" another yelled, watching as the car pulled up outside. The three quickly stood to attention, and walked over in line.

	"You need to stop this. Now." he wrote.
	"I'm trying, sir. But they secured the whole site. Trying to remove evidence once it's in storage is almost impossible." he received.
	"Then make it possible. Do something! Or we're ALL going down."
	"I'll do what I can. Hail Victory."
	"Hail Victory." he typed, and leant back.
	He hesitated, and then typed again.
	"It's time. Invoke the Act."

	In his dinky flat, DI Naismith was sipping a beer bottle, while Max slept at his feet.
	"Good boy." he smiled softly.
	A knock came at the door.
	He took another swig, then stood to answer it.
	"DI Naismith, I am detaining you under the Terrorism Act. You do not have to say anything... I expect you know the rest."
	"What the fuck is this?" he tilted his head.
	Dent responded by pulling out his sidearm.
	"Don't make this difficult."
	Naismith grimaced, then walked out. Max whimpered and whined.

	To most, it was a normal night. As usual, Pawel was checking IDs at the entrance to the Singing Horse. The bass thumped through the walls, spilling out onto the street.
	"You're clear. On you go." he smiled to a clubgoer.
	The gang approached the queue.
	"Ey! Pawel! Big man!" Trey cheered.
	Pawel laughed. "Trey's drunk and the sky is blue!"
	"Hey, the sky's actually black." Shannon quipped, her laptop bag slung over one shoulder. She'd been quiet all evening - still working through the last of the Gambit files in her head - but she'd come out anyway. Trey had insisted. It was nice, she thought, to have people who insisted.
	"Alright, brainiac!" Pawel grinned. "Come on in."
	Then, they came.
	Three black, unmarked vans, screeched to a halt at the curb. Armed officers poured out, guns drawn.
	"PAWEL RAKOWSKI! HANDS BEHIND YOUR HEAD!" they barked.
	"The fuck?" Amy asked, shocked.
	Pawel knelt down and complied.
	"Pawel Rakowski, I am detaining you under the Terrorism Act." the lead officer barked.
	They cuffed him, and one officer struck him in the stomach.
	"HEY!" Connor barked. "GET AWAY FROM HIM!"
	"Connor, don't!" Chloe protested, fearful.
	"THAT'S THEM!" an officer snapped. "The other suspects!"
	"What?!" Connor snarled.
	Too late. The officers drew, and trained their guns on Connor's head.
	For a second, he considered fighting back - no. That'd blow his cover. He let himself be cuffed.
	To his horror, he saw Trey and Shannon get grabbed as well.
	"I am detaining you all under the Terrorism Act." the officer spat.
	"Terrorism?! The fuck are you on about?" Amy barked.
	"Amy, don't." Connor said firmly. "Tell Sparky I'm okay, please."
	"What the FUCK is going on out here?!"
	Tommy stormed out of the club, his face red with fury. He stopped dead when he saw the scene.
	"Thomas Crane." the lead officer barked, stepping forward.
	"You can't do this!" Tommy barked. "This is my club, my--"
	A baton cracked across his stomach. He doubled over, wheezing.
	"The fuck is your problem?!" Trey snapped.
	"Take 'em away, boys."
	The cops dragged the Hoods away. Unable to contain their glee, they began beating on Trey and Shannon with their batons.
	Bea pulled out her phone and began filming, before another officer snatched it, grinned, and stomped on it.
	Connor exploded. He slipped out of his handcuffs, and caught the baton mid-swing, just before it could hit anyone again. He headbutted the assaulting officer, causing his grip to release. Then, he took the baton, and jabbed him in the face, knocking out his teeth. He brought it down on his head, knocking him out cold. He swung at the second officer, hitting him in the abdomen, before swinging again at his back, taking him down too.
	With that, the other officers trained their rifles on him. Connor stood there above the bodies, and panted like a rabid dog, steam puffing from his mouth.
	"CONNOR!" Chloe begged. "Please, just do what they say!"
	"We're okay..." Trey groaned in pain.
	"Don't get yourself exposed over this." Shannon nodded.
	Connor thought hard for a moment. Finally, he dropped the baton, and surrendered.
	They shoved him into the van alongside the others. Shannon was pressed between Trey and Pawel, her lip split and her eye already swelling. Tommy sat across from them, blood trickling from his nose.
	"Cuff him." one ordered, nodding at Shannon.
	One of the officers grabbed her wrists. He paused, looking her up and down with a sneer.
	"Liam Taylor, right?"
	"It's *Shannon,* you fascist pig." she spat, only to be beaten again.
	"Liam." he repeated viciously. "Connor. Trevor. Tommy. Pawel. I am detaining you all under the Terrorism Act. Basically... you're fucked."
	The van doors slammed shut. The engine roared, and they sped off into the night.
	"FUCK!" Bea exhaled at last.
	Amy took a deep, deep breath.
