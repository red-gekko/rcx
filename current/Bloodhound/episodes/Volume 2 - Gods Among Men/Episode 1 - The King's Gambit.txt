The moon shone brightly through the pouring rain, shimmering in the valley below.
It was the first night of the year. Typically, the night after New Year's is quiet, subdued, and peaceful, as people recover from the night before.
This night would've been the same - were it not for the fucking alarm bells.
The tired citydwellers groaned at the racket - the blaring sirens of Drackhill City Bank.

The armoured truck's doors swung open, and a gaggle of kevlar-clad gunmen dismounted.
"Alright boys, you all know the drill." their point man barked. "Go in, grab, get out. We have ten minutes before the cops arrive."
These men were clear professionals. They swarmed into the hall and placed their charges on the safes and secure doors.
"Fire in the hole! 3... 2... 1!"
*BOOM!*
The bank rattled as its prizes were laid bare. The men began filling their bags.
"Alpha team, with me! We're going up! Bravo team, get as much as you can!" the leader ordered. His squad followed in lockstep as they ascended the stairs.
Alpha team kicked down the heavy oak doors, and quickly occupied the upstairs offices.
"There - secure door." one noted, pointing at the metal shutters that had deployed as soon as the truck breached the walls.
"Cutter!"
One man holstered his gun, and pulled out an electric buzzsaw. Sparks flew as he got to work slicing past the door.
"You think... *he'll* come here?" one whispered nervously. "There's no way we can take him..."

"I heard stories, you know. Rumours." one began. "They say he fought some sort of cyborg ogre."
"Oh yeah? I heard he shits rainbows and breathes fire." another snarked.
Despite the bravado, he couldn't shake the feeling. That he was being watched.
That... *he* was out there.

Connor's scarlet gaze flashed as he rose from his rooftop perch.

Connor's trenchcoat fluttered in the breeze, as his eyes burned a little brighter. He rolled his shoulders in anticipation, and gripped its hem. Then, he pulled the coat out, and the material wriggled up his arms, forming into his glider-wings. His arms rose up like a gargoyle, and he rode the wind forwards.
"Hey... what the fuck is that?" one gunman asked, pointing at the black mass falling from the office block across from him.
"Probably nothing. You gonna help, or what?!" another snapped.
Connor sailed through the air, and his bracers shifted. Crossbows formed atop his wrists, and grappling ropes winded into them. He aimed above the window, and fired.
They quickly went taut and started reeling. He shifted so his feet went first.
"HOLY FUCK! IT'S *HIM!*"
The Werewolf's boots shattered the reinforced glass, and slammed into the gunman's chest. He launched backwards, and sank down, out cold.
"Shit, shit, shit!" his partner gasped, firing his pistol wildly. Connor tanked the hits, his eyes aflame. Then, the gunman felt the impact of a monster's fist, and his world went dark.
Alpha team heard the howls.
"Oh god, oh god..." the sawman panicked.
"Keep sawing! We've got you covered!" the leader barked. "Men, safeties off! Flashlights on!"
The squad moved out, beams of light shimmering in the dark. They arrived at the shattered glass, and saw their comrades knocked out and cuffed.
"Where the hell did he go?!" one gasped.
"Whatever got in..." the leader began grimly, "Don't let it get out."
The squad began their search, kicking down cubicles and workstations as they searched for their assailant.

*WHOOSH!*

He stepped forward slowly, and...
*WHOOSH!*

Connor flexed his calves, and gave chase. His grapple attached to a street light, and tensed as he braced himself. Then, he slingshot upwards. Using his momentum, he launched out, and reformed his glider.

"Is that him?"
"Did he see us?!"

A man ran out of the shop next to it, and screamed in horror.
"ARE YOU KIDDING ME?! MY INSURANCE IS *FUCKED!*"

"FUCK! HE'S HERE!" the driver gasped.
"Floor it! Run him down!"
Connor crossed his arms. They made a sharp *SHIK!* noise as a pair of throwing knives formed in his hands. Then, he threw them out.
The blades sank into the heavy, military-grade tyres, and air immediately screamed out of them. The truck began to turn uncontrollably, and then...
*SMACK!*
It landed on its side, shaking the ground with the impact.
Connor walked over - slowly, grimly - and shattered the windscreen with his fist. He grabbed the driver and his passenger, and threw them to the curb.
He walked around to the back, ready to do the same to the last man standing. Then...
*CLICK-CLACK! BANG!*
The shotgun's blast slammed into his chest, and he fell backwards, staggered and dazed. The gunman looked down at the bloodied Werewolf's exposed chest, and went pale in shock and horror.
He went paler still, as Connor rose up. Blood poured from the holes in his chest, as the suit struggled and squirmed around him. In moments, he was covered again.
"What... what are you?" he asked in shock.
He was answered by four knuckles to the temple.
"I'm the Werewolf." Connor spat.
"Don't... don't do cheesy one liners." Ivan groaned.
"Fuck you."
"ARMED POLICE! STAY WHERE... uh... st-stay where you are..." the late-as-always cavalry stammered.
Connor grinned, walking towards them. "All yours."
He walked on past, and faded into the night.
