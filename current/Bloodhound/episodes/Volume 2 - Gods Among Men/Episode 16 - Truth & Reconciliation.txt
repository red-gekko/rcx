I don't know how, but I think there's some endorphin-analogue that counteracts the Paradrenaline."

"It was dead on. I can hit as hard as a jackhammer, just like you said. That is, outside the Rage - but the Rage doesn't count."

"Stop downplaying it." Connor insisted. "You figured out my limits before I even knew them. And you did it all on your own. That's incredible, Chloe."
She glanced up at him, a small, shy smile forming. "Thanks... that means a lot."
They collected the final papers. Then, they heard Sparky howl.

They rushed to the source of the noise, Connor in front and suited up. There, they found a particularly twisted creature - it looked like a large fetus with tendrils randomly popping from its hide.
"Jesus..." Connor shuddered.
Chloe squeezed his hand. "We... we should do what we did last time, no?"
"Yeah..." Connor nodded, before Sparky stopped him.
"Put your hand to his head." he said.
Connor looked at him, before nodding. He knelt down, and placed his palm on the creature's head.
Immediately, he felt a memory - though not his own. He could see the memories of this thing's torment at Vitalis's hands. It was the smallest of the clones - degenerate beyond degenerate. Cast off and forgotten.
He pulled his hand away, and tears streamed down his face.
"Connor?" Chloe asked, shaking slightly.
"I saw it..." he whispered. "I saw it's memories."
Chloe swallowed. "What, like... like a mind merge?"
"Yeah, something like that." Connor nodded softly. "The same as before. But weaker. It... it doesn't have enough of a consciousness for me to merge into. I can look in, but... there's nothing to look back."
Chloe shuddered. "So... telepathy? You're telepathic?"
"Kinda. You remember Whiplash?"
"I'd just barely avoided getting shot in the face after finding out my boyfriend was a superhero when she grabbed me out of nowhere and carried me down to street level. Also, that happened literally last night." she snarked.
Connor let out a chuckle. "She's an 'Esper' - it's the term for people with psychic powers. Mine are very low-level - it's limited to mind-merges like that, mostly. Same with Sparky - that's why until recently, he couldn't communicate with non-Espers."
"Does it work based on touch?"
"For us, mostly." Sparky nodded. "Did you ever notice, when you were stroking me, you could understand me a little better?"
"I---Yeah, actually." she gasped. "When we met Charlie with Bea. You were warning me - I'm not sure how, but... I knew what you were saying."
"Since his power boost, he can do it more clearly, and at range." Connor added. "Gwen calls it 'tactile telepathy' - I mostly use it when I'm interrogating someone. I can tell if they're lying through it."
"...I think I just thought of, like, 15 different experiments I'm gonna run on you guys."
Connor smiled. He lifted the fetus-thing, took a syringe of pain relief, and injected it. It shuddered, and went completely still.
"Shh..." he whispered. Sparky let out a mournful howl.
Chloe hugged both again. Connor took a deep breath, and resumed his professional demeanour.
"Okay, one more thing - this place was connected to the Optimates. So, we need to go to your boss's office." he said.

They arrived, and saw a laptop on his desk. Connor swiped it, and logged in using credentials he'd found on the server.
"Bingo - okay, I can see here - holy shit. This guy wasn't just connected, he was in direct contact with the Commandant."
"The guy who came by the office once?" she tilted her head.
"Yeah - that's how we figured this place was connected." he nodded. "The Commandant. Real name unknown - but presumptive leader of PaladinCorp. Morland had regular meetings with him."
He paused. "Okay, this is where we part ways. I'm going to go to Morland's house, and get some answers."
"Are you sure you don't need me there?" Chloe asked, eager to help.
"No, I'm sure - firstly, he may have Super-Agents there, and there's no way I'm risking your safety like that. Secondly, I need you to go through the data we found--" he said, handing her the hard drive and papers "--and compile it all into a database. Sparky will escort you back home. Just plug it into the computer there, and Shannon can handle the rest remotely."
"Got it." Chloe said, nodding. "Good luck. And... give that bastard Morland a piece of my mind."

With that, Connor headed off to Morland's house - a big, upmarket country home in Upper Caldermouth - the poshest part of Drackhill. He entered, sneaked upstairs, and found a blood trail. He followed it, and saw Morland's body, with a shotgun in his hand and the top of his head missing. Connor sighed.
"Damn it... hang on." he said, looking closer. The shotgun was loaded. How could he have reloaded after shooting himself? That would only happen if someone shot him, and reloaded out of reflex before placing the gun in his hand. Hell, the shot even looked weird compared to his position...
"It's a setup." he mused, and called Naismith's burner.
"DCI Naismith." he answered.
"Detective. I've found something." Connor said in his Werewolf voice.
"A billion quid? Come on, let us have some good news for a change." Naismith quipped.
"One day." Connor chuckled. "Greg Morland. Dead in a clearly staged suicide. You have his address?"
"We do. Okay, thanks mate - I'll come over first thing. I'm back in action now - that serum worked a treat."
"Good to hear - Contact me when you find something. I'm guessing your case is about to become complicated."
"You have no idea. With all the coppers who died yesterday, there's a real chance we might have to just refocus on other cases. We'll end up turning a blind eye to you and yours. Well, *more* of a blind eye, anyway." Naismith mused.
"That works for me. Detective..." Connor began, hesitating a little. "Our collaboration works both ways. If you need a hand, just phone me."
"Will do. Thanks... for a brutal serial killer, you're half decent." Naismith replied, his smirk audible.
"And for a stuck-up fed, you're not so bad." Connor snarked back. With that, he headed home.

Chloe sat comfortably in the basement, sorting through the data on Connor's laptop. She searched a keyword - 'Esper' - and found a few results.
"Hey, um... Sparky?" she began.
"Yeah?"
"What's ODIN?" she tilted her head.
"Oh, they're connected to the people we fight, I think." he shrugged. "When you meet Gwen, she can tell you more."
With that, Connor arrived, and she ran up and hugged him.
"Did you find anything?" she asked.
"He's dead - staged suicide. I've called Naismith - he'll investigate while he still can." Connor explained.
"Oh my god..." Chloe said, surprised. "Well... sounds like his plots caught up to him."
"Seems that way. They didn't go to any real effort to disguise it - presumably, they intended to send a message, somehow."
"That whatever he did to them was tantamount to committing suicide, perhaps." Chloe noted sombrely. "Anyway, I've sorted through about a quarter of the data."
"Excellent - let's call it a night." Connor smiled. "We'll work on it together tomorrow."
"Sounds good... hey, can I stay at yours tonight?" she asked.
"Of course you can. You can stay as long as you want to. In fact, given what happened... I'd feel more comfortable if you were nearby. Not in a creepy way..." he interrupted himself. "But... well... I just..." he stumbled on his words.
"Hey..." she smiled, putting her hand on his chest. "It's okay. I know what you mean. I'll stay for a few more days, until the heat dies down a bit. And if things kick off again, I'll come back."
Connor smiled, and hugged her. She embraced him.
She sniffled a little, her eyes slightly teary. "I just... it's hard, not being able to talk to Bea about this." she said.
"You... you can tell her if you like."
"No... not yet. I understand why you didn't tell me." she shook her head.
"You can talk to the others, though." he smiled softly. "In fact, maybe you should meet up with them tomorrow? You guys can talk about it properly, without me there."
Chloe smiled softly. "That's a good idea."

"Anyway, changing the subject somewhat... my birthday's in a week - on the 6th of Feb. I was going to go up back home, and... would you like to come with me?"
"Your birthday?" Connor looked surprised, then a warm smile spread across his face. "You'd want me there?"
Chloe nodded, suddenly shy. "Of course. You're part of my life now."
"I'd love to!" Connor replied. "You'll be 19, right?"
"Yes, well remembered!" she smiled. "When's your birthday again?"
"26th of September."
"Oh, we missed it - did you do anything nice?" she asked, tilting her head slightly.
"We met." Connor smiled. "Remember that night out you had with Amy and Bea? The one where I dropped you off?"
"Oh yeah! The second time we'd met. You had to rush off for a work thing, right?"
Connor paused sadly. "Yeah... that was a bad night. It was one of my first outings, back before anyone called me the Werewolf." he explained. "I lost control that night. Hurt people. Killed them..." he said, his voice becoming a whisper of shame.
Chloe smiled sympathetically. "I'm sorry. I didn't mean to pry."
"No, please - pry." he smiled. "I'm not going to hide things from you anymore."
He paused for a moment. "That night, I... I thought about ending it all. I had a gun, and I put it to my temple, but... I just couldn't. Too cowardly, I suppose."
Chloe put her hand on his shoulder, tears glistening in her eyes. "Brave, I'd say. I... I've been in that kind of mindset before."
Connor looked up at her, saddened. "I'm sorry... do you wanna talk about it?"
Chloe paused. "Maybe later."
He nodded. "Anyway, funny story - after the third time of putting it to my temple and failing, I decided I wasn't gonna do it. And then, Amy phoned - and I was so startled, I accidentally did it. In the process, I found out both that gunshots to the head can't kill me, and that they still really fuckin' hurt."
Chloe chuckled. "I... I never thought I'd see the funny side of you shooting yourself in the head."
Connor snickered. "A couple days later, I reforged it into this."
He drew the coagulator, and pulled out its cartridge.
"Is that haemostatic gel?" she tilted her head.
"Ever the scientist." he grinned. "I... I took a life that didn't need to be taken with this. So now, I can save lives with it instead. Plug holes, instead of making them."
She smiled softly. "At least you can't shoot yourself with it." she quipped.
He chuckled, then laughed heartily, before taking a deep breath.
"It helped, in a way. Amy's call, I mean. Giving yous all a lift made me feel... human."
"That's why you've kept this a secret, isn't it?"
He nodded. "Yeah, I... it's nice to just pretend to be a normal person sometimes. I know it was selfish, but..."
"I get it." she smiled, hugging him. "So... what would you have done if it weren't for everything?"
"I, um... I don't really celebrate my birthday that much. I mean, I went out with Trey on my 18th, but... yeah." he shuffled uncomfortably.
"Why is that?" Chloe asked, curious.
"Well, um... you remember how my mum died when I was born? Well, when I went to live with my grandparents, they kind of blamed me for it."
His voice tightened slightly. "No, that's not true. My granny was just sad, she never blamed me... my grandad, however... anyway..." he forced a small laugh to banish the memory. "So, I guess I never had it drilled in that my birthday was something worth celebrating."
Chloe looked at him, her eyes betraying her sadness. "I'm so sorry... I didn't even think about your mum."
"It's okay." Connor smiled warmly. "I never knew her, obviously - and besides, it's a me thing. Trey still celebrates, as does Amy. My uncle and me... this'll sound morbid." he chuckled grimly. "When I started living with him, we made a ritual - I found out, every year on my birthday, he'd been visiting her grave and telling her what I'd been up to. It was his way of coping, I guess. On my 9th birthday, I followed him that night. From that, it just became a regular thing."
Chloe smiled sadly, and hugged him.
"It doesn't sound morbid." she said. "It's... it's really sweet. I mean, it's sad too, but... you know what I mean?"
"Of course." Connor nodded, smiling. He headed over to a shelf in his room, and took out a small picture-book. He opened it to a page he knew off by heart - well, hearts.
"That was her." he said, pointing to a photo of his mother. "Her name was Laura."
Chloe took the book, and smiled softly. Connor was the near spitting image of her. The only notable difference was her sea-blue eyes compared to his steel-grey. Well, that and she was a woman, but still.
She hesitated. "Do you, um... do you know your father?" she asked.
"No." Connor shook his head. "I did a little bit of digging a couple years ago, but found nothing. Vince has no idea either. Apparently he was some bloke in America... and from what she told him, it sounds like he was a bad, bad man."

"I think so." Connor shrugged. "I'd like to give him a piece of my mind... at the least."

"Anyway... enough of the depressing stuff." he chuckled.
"Connor, I... I understand why you kept this to yourself. But either way, I'm involved. Which means I... I want to help. I'm not going to sit on the sidelines while you risk your life every day. I might not have superpowers, but I'm a scientist. A biologist. Maybe I can help you understand how your powers work... I mean, I did a pretty good job just from recovered samples. I can run tests on you - discreetly, of course. And maybe we can learn how it all works - and you can learn how to use it to its full potential."
Connor looked at her, his pupils dilating. "Chloe... you would do that for me?"
She gave a small, determined smile. "Yeah. Because that's what people who... who care about each other do. They figure it out. Together."
She leant her forehead against his, and they embraced.
"So... what's next?" she asked.
"Tomorrow night, there's another weapons sale." he began. "Sparky - you up for some gun-smashing?"
"Of course." Sparky grinned.
"Good lad. While we're out, can you help Shannon sort through that data? I know it's gonna be exhausting, but..."
"Yeah, of course." she smiled. "Once I've got it all organised, we can look into studying a little."
Connor smiled. She hugged him, and then paused.
"Just... promise me, and swear on something that matters, that you won't hide away from me. And I promise, I'll never hide away from you." she asked.
Connor put his hand her shoulders. "I swear, on Vince's life, I will always tell you the truth from now on."
She smiled, and embraced him again.
