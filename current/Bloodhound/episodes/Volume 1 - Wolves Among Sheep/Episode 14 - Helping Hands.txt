VOLUME - Wolves Among Sheep
EPISODE - Helping Hands
============================================================

Morning came, then afternoon, as Connor jogged through the high street. A voice caught his ear.
"Oh, hey!" Bea called over. Connor slowed, turned, and smiled.
"Hey, guys!" he waved. She and Chloe walked over.
"Fancy seeing you here!" Bea smiled. "Out for a run?"
"Yeah - honestly, I appreciate the break." he grinned.
"Do you run a lot? You don't seem out of breath at all." she noted.
"I train when I can." he shrugged.
Chloe smiled. "We're just out for our lunch break. Do you wanna come along?"
"Sure!"
The group headed into the nearest bistro, and took their seats.
"So, how's school?" he asked.
"Yeah, pretty good." Bea smiled. "I've spent my whole morning studying the British constitution - or, lack thereof."
"Fun stuff." Connor grinned.
"We just finished a lab session." Chloe chimed in. "Dissections, this time."
"Gross." Bea chuckled, putting down her food. Chloe let out a giggle.

They finished their lunch, and headed out of the bistro. There, a man in military uniform with a bright red poppy held a collection box.
He marched up to the group. "Poppy appeal. Donations, please."
"Oh, uh..." Bea fumbled at her pockets. "Sorry, I'm out of change!"
"We take card." he said firmly.
"Right." Bea nodded awkwardly. She reached for her wallet.
Connor looked the man up and down, and his pupils constricted slightly.
"Guys, let's go." he said hurriedly.
"What's wrong?" Chloe asked.
"Nothing, just... let's go."
"Wow." the soldier scowled. "You feel like a big man, disrespecting the Army like that?"
"You feel like a big man, shooting little kids?" he snapped back.
"Connor!" Bea gasped.
"What the *fuck* did you just say to me?!" the soldier barked.
Connor stood his ground, his jaw clenching. "I see the medal, you Tan fuck. Did you enjoy yourself--"
"Come on, let's go." Chloe cut in, grabbing his arm. "Now."
Connor took a deep breath, staring into the soldier's soul. He nodded, and they moved.
"Very brave, kid!" the soldier jeered. "Mouthing off at real men who fought for your freedoms!"
Connor's head snapped around, but Chloe jerked his arm.
"Don't." she said firmly. He sighed, and carried on walking.

The trio came to a halt, and leaned against a wall.
"Fuckin' Tan. Fought for my chains, more like!" he hissed.
"Okay, what's going on?" Bea asked firmly.
Connor raised an eyebrow.
"I've not known you for long - but you've never seemed like the type to pick fights with strangers on the street." she pressed. "What's wrong?"
He let out a deep breath. "Sorry... sorry."
Another deep breath, before he explained.
"That guy's medals - one of them was the General Service Medal for Operation Flying Fox - the occupation of my hometown."
"Oh..." Bea realised.
He sighed. "Seeing a man in uniform cornering us isn't a happy memory even ignoring that. To me, a soldier is someone who kicks down your door, screams in your face, smashes your windows, and beats you in the chest. And he had the nerve... the fuckin' nerve to shake his little collection box in our faces and ask for money?"
"It's the poppy appeal..." Chloe shrugged. "I mean, they do help people--"
"They help *soldiers.*" he corrected. "Call me heartless, but they're not better than anyone else. They don't deserve their own special charity, some massive public campaign, or every single person wearing a special flower just for them."
He let out a breath, and bowed his head. "Sorry - I don't mean to rant about it. I know yous both get it."
"We do." Bea nodded softly. "Look, we've gotta get back to uni, but... are you gonna be okay?"
"Yeah, why?" he tilted his head.
"Connor... what you're describing, it... it sounds like PTSD." Chloe said gently.
He looked away.
"There's no shame in it." she added quietly.
"I know." he nodded. "I don't think colonised people have a word for PTSD. I think we just call it 'normal.'"
Bea let out a sad smile. "We'll stay out a little longer."
"No, guys you--you have classes..." he meekly protested.
"Doesn't matter." Chloe smiled warmly. "Come on, let's go for a walk down the park, and talk about random stuff."
"Sounds like a plan." Bea nodded.
Connor hesitated, but smiled back.

It had been a long, long day at the cafe. Too long. Unsurprisingly, Amy's face lit up as she saw Connor and Sparky waiting outside for her.
"Oh my goodness!" she beamed, rushing straight over to Sparky. "Hey, buddy! How've you been all day? Did you miss me?"
"Yeah! Today's been good. Connor's been teaching me how to read a little better!" he barked back.
Oblivious, Amy smiled warmly. "Such a good boy..." she cooed.
"Nice to see you too, dickhead." Connor quipped.
"Next time, just send the dog." she snarked back. She stood, and pulled him in for a hug.

Connor finished tinkering away at the now-fixed wristwatch on his bedroom desk, and came into the living room. There, Amy was making a fuss over Sparky, as usual.
"Who's a good boy? Whosagoodboyyy?" she asked in a baby voice.
"Me, so I've been told." Sparky grinned. "Can I have the bloody treat now, or what?"
"Paw?" she asked.
Sparky sighed, giving a very graceful paw-shake.
"Beg?"
He hopped up, planting his front legs on her forearm.
"Roll over?"
"This bacon twist better be worth it." he grumbled, obeying.
"Good boy! Catch!" she cheered, throwing the treat in the air. He inhaled it.
"Hmm... yeah, not bad. " he shrugged.
She laughed. "He's so good."
"Sure is." Connor chuckled, endeared. "It's nice, seeing yous two get on so well."
"Yeah, well if you remember, *I* was always Luna's favourite." she grinned.
He laughed heartily, as did she.
"You know..." she began. "Ever since getting him, I've noticed you've been doing better and all."
Connor smiled. "Yeah, well... perfect excuse to get out of the house, isn't he?"
Sparky rolled back onto his back, and was immediately attended by Amy's belly rubs.
"Did you hear? Apparently Social Services came round to number 38, downstairs. Ms. Pilton was supposedly abusing her daughter - and the Werewolf himself burst in and stopped her."
"Damn. At least the kid's safe now." Connor nodded.
"Yeah... listen, I get why you cut a deal, but..."
"I knew nothing would come of it." he shrugged. "And nothing did."
She awkwardly played with her hands.
"How was your day, by the way?" she asked, changing the subject.
"Oh, *now* you ask!" he snarked.
"Fuck off, I don't actually care." she grinned.
He chuckled. "Yeah, not bad. Ran into Chloe and Bea earlier."
"Ah, nice. How are they?"
"They're good. We, uh... we got cornered by some poppy appeal guy."
"Oh." Amy nodded sympathetically. "You okay?"
"Yeah. They helped me get over it." he nodded.
"Other than that... I know you've not said much about it, but... how are you holding up with Eileen's passing?"
He looked away. Hated that he couldn't tell her the whole story.
"I'm okay, I guess. I'm just glad I got to be with her at the end. I've stored some of her stuff at Riverside - along with her old bike. The rest, I'm donating to charity, like she would've wanted."
"Bless you. It's really nice that you were looking after her. How'd you say you met her, again?"
"Uh--at the cemetery." he nodded. "Her son's funeral."
"What were you doing there?"
Before he could answer, his ears pricked up - his burner phone was ringing.
"One sec." he said, rushing into his room.
"Hello?"
"Hi, Connor."
"Commandant..." Connor snarled, his face going white.
"Commandant?!" Sparky jumped up, barking and curling his lip.
"What's wrong?" Amy asked, confused.
"What do you want, you fucking cunt?!" Connor snapped. "Wanna show your face? Let me pay you back for what you did to me?!"
"'Did to you?'" Amy tilted her head. "Wait..."
"Yeah, that's not a half-bad idea." the Commandant audibly grinned. "Meet me in Belson. Marsh Road. Unit 4-7-81/P."
"You're a dead man. You hear me?" Connor growled. "*DEAD!*"
A drop of hot blood came from his nostrils, and the pain in his head intensified.
"I'll tear your throat out!" Sparky added through a roar.
"See you soon!" the Commandant laughed, cutting off the call.
Connor turned to Sparky. "Let's go."
"Whoa, whoa..." Amy cut in, standing in front of the door. "You're not going anywhere."
"Amy..." Connor began, suppressing himself. "Get out of my way."
"No fucking chance. Not until you tell me who that is, and what they did to you."
"Connor, we don't have time for this. He could have 217! My sister!" Sparky barked.
"Amy, I'll explain it all later, I promise. But please, let me through. It's important."
"No way." she shook her head. "I heard you there. And I know that look in your eye. If I let you go, you'll kill them."
"He needs killing." Connor growled lowly.
"Tell me why." she pressed.
"Amy..." he said through gritted teeth. "Move."
"Or what?" she tilted her head. "You gonna make me?"
Connor turned away.
"Exactly. Now start talking."
"I don't have time. I'll tell you when I get back. I swear."
"Tell. Me. *Now.*"
"Please, just... just drop it..." he begged, his headache pounding like a drum in his skull. "I'll tell you later. I *don't have time.* I need to go. *NOW!*"
"No. You're telling me. There's no reason why you can't! Something's going on, and I'm your fucking sister!" she barked. "I'm sick of this! Sick of you hiding this from me! You know all about what happened to me in foster-care, but you can't trust me enough to just TELL ME!"
He felt the pressure building up under his skin again. Like he was going to explode.
"Just please... get outta my way." he pleaded.
"Not. Fucking. Happening." she shook her head.
"*GET OUT OF MY WAY!*"
She went pale. Not just pale, but practically white. She stumbled, and fell backwards.
He could hear her heartbeat. It was going a mile a minute. He turned, and spotted his reflection in the window.
"Oh... f-fuck." he whispered.
The chitinous exoskeleton had encased him once more. And his eyes glowed bright red, lighting up the room. His claws - not hands, but claws - glinted in the light, like his fingers were knives. Embers of fire flickered from his mouth - his roar had been punctuated by the flame.
He took a deep breath, and ripped the covering from his face.
"Amy, I--" he stammered.
She didn't speak. Instead, she struggled to her feet, refusing to turn her back to him.
"I... I'll let you go, just... p-please don't... don't hurt me..." she begged, tears streaming down her face.
"Amy, I'm not gonna hurt you." he said as softly as he could. She made her way to her room, and rushed in, bolting the door.
"Amy, wait!" he begged.
"CONNOR!" Sparky barked, similarly encased in his battle-form. "We have to go!"
Connor sighed, but nodded. The twin Werewolves headed out.

Across the city, Connor and Sparky perched atop an industrial unit. Sparky's fur flowed in the cold breeze, and their eyes flashed red as they scanned the fourth unit below.
"Electromagnetic signals." Sparky noted. "Some electrical equipment in the walls."
"Probably just wiring. Though, I'm not sure - that there looks like a radio receiver." Connor pointed.
"Smells like WiFi." Sparky shrugged. "I doubt it's anything important. Can you get that plastic smell?"
"Plastic? I thought it was oil." Connor tilted his head. "Well, this place is an industrial unit. Probably just old product."
Sparky nodded, then his eyes darted. "See that?"
"Yeah - cluster of heartbeats."
He flexed his throat and unhinged his jaw, as did his packmate. They both let out an ethereal, alien song - one high-pitched enough that the bats in Wyrmrock went crazy.
"Canines." he nodded. "A couple of them. A humanoid, too - must be the Commandant."
"217 must be in there. She *has* to be. As for the other... they must be another sibling. More of my people."
Connor gently stroked him between the ears.
"This is the big moment. We get our revenge, and you get your sister back. After that... we hunt the rest of them down."
"No time to dawdle, then." Sparky smiled. The two rose, and stretched their legs out.
Connor closed his eyes, and focused. He flexed his thighs and calves, and felt his hot blood rush to them, saturating them with mutant power.
Whatever the Commandant had done to him... it was going to be his undoing.
The two rushed forward, and jumped. Their legs kicked into the roof's edge, smashing the metal downwards as they launched upwards. They landed on a skylight, crashing down.
They landed on the ground, their arrival punctuated by raining shards of glass.
"COMMANDANT!" Connor roared. "SHOW YOURSELF!"
His scream echoed in the cold, metal unit. He was answered by the sound of scraping, as a Super-Agent walked out from a storage crate, wielding twin machetes and clad in thick kevlar armour.
A television screen, which hanged from the wall, flashed to life. The Commandant's image flickered onto the screen.
"Good evening, freaks." he grinned sadistically.
"YOU FUCKING COWARD!" Connor howled. "SHOW YOUR FACE AND *DIE LIKE A MAN!*"
"It's cute, honestly. You think you two little shits could kill me." he laughed. "And honestly, I think I may have tortured you a little too well. I tell you I'm somewhere, and mere minutes later you come running? You must miss me."
"WHERE'S 217? WHERE'S MY SISTER?!" Sparky roared.
"Oh, Six! Good to see you again." the Commandant smirked.
At that, the Agent was joined by a pair of snarling, muscular mastiffs. It was barely visible past their fur, but the smell of their blood confirmed it - there was super-serum in their black veins.
"She's not here, but don't worry - you have company. Project Fenrir wasn't a *complete* failure - that blood we harvested was given to these... mangy, rabid mutts."
The twin Werewolves assumed a fighting stance.
"You take the dogs - don't kill them." Connor whispered firmly, morphing blades from his wrists. "I'll fight the Agent."
"Save some for me." Sparky snarled back, eyes burning red.
"Sic 'em, boys!" the Commandant barked. "Agent Weiss - kill him."

Connor wasn't here to fool around - he was here for blood. He spun forwards like a vengeful tornado, his wristblades ripping through the air. Weiss darted back, then knelt down and crossed his machetes, catching Connor's right.
Under his mask, Connor grinned. His left blade burst forwards, aimed straight for Weiss's gut.
Not fast enough. Weiss turned, twisting his blades so Connor's fell forward, and then he countered. Connor bent sideways into an L-shape, dodging the first machete. The second nicked his neck.
First blood. Connor felt the wound close up in moments, and snarled viciously.
He roared, charging forwards again.

At first, Sparky's wolven form intimidated the lesser canids. But then both remembered what the Commandant would do if they failed, and their black-veined eyes darkened with furor.
"Listen to me! I don't want to hurt you!" he pleaded, as he slammed the backside of his paw around the head of one. He was careful not to catch it with his claws.
The other used the opening to sink his teeth into Sparky's back leg, tearing hot blood and red meat.
He howled in shock and pain, before turning and beating that one down. It got back up in short order.
Sparky turned his head, and saw Connor's fight. His pupils shrank as they caught the Commandant's image.

"DIE!" Connor demanded, as the tip of his blade sank into Weiss's thigh.
Most men - hell, most Super-Agents - would've been thrown off by that. Weiss simply used his proximity to drive his own blade through Connor's hand.
Connor screamed as the blade shunted in between his carpal bones, popping out of his palm. Weiss pulled the blade backwards, splitting his hand in two.
He flipped, and clutched his hand tight, setting the bones back and morphing a bandage to bind them. His eyes flashed crimson, and he formed bucklers over his wristblades.
"Ah, finally - you've figured out you're not invincible, eh?" the Commandant taunted. "In fact, I'd say you're quite vincible."
"Fuck you." Connor spat, his armour thickening and calcifying. He raced forwards, and the two monsters - one in form, one in soul - clashed again.

Sparky snarled as another chunk of flesh tore out from his hide. He turned, and bit the scruff of the one who did it. He raised up on his hind legs, and threw it across the unit.
Breathing space. He pounced on the other forcing it to its back.
For normal canines, that would be it. The loser would submit, accepting that the winner would have its throat otherwise.
The super-mastiff went right for Sparky's throat, instead. Sparky darted back just in time.
"Please! Stop fighting me! I'm on your side! I want to stop the Commandant!" he begged.
No answer. This wasn't one of his people - just an animal. A raging, monstrous animal.
Sparky closed his scarlet eyes for a second.
"I'm sorry." he whispered, as he ripped out its throat.
He turned, as the other was sprinting towards him. He bowed his head in shame, and went for the kill.

Connor snarled as Weiss's machete was knocked backwards by his buckler. He saw his opening and pounced forwards, paying him back in kind with a slash across the chest.
Weiss's armour was shredded and torn, and his skin was raked and bloody - but he wasn't beaten. Not yet.
He brought his blades together, holding them side-by-side. He half-closed his eyes, and breathed out slowly.
Connor charged, pointing his own blades forward. He was going to impale this son of a bitch.
Weiss held position. He felt his heart beat with anticipation. Then, he struck.
Connor was tired. Exhausted, even. So exhausted, his reflexes were a nanosecond too late.
Weiss's blades swept in a horizontal motion, clashing into Connor's forearms. The bucklers absorbed the damage, bending and cracking as his arms swung outwards.
Connor went to close the opening, to put his guard back up, to do *something.*
Too late.
Weiss twisted his blades to their sides, and drove them through a gap in Connor's ribs.
They sank through his armour, then his upper lungs, and punched out through his back.
Connor went to scream - instead, blood poured from his mouth and nose, as he gurgled in pain.
Weiss gripped the hilts of his blades, and pushed Connor to his knees.
"Hah..." the Commandant chuckled. "I expected more. Goodbye, Connor."
Weiss placed his foot against Connor's sternum, and pulled his blades free - slowly. Grinning.
The blades glinted in the dim light, as he crossed them - holing them like shears ready to take the Werewolf's head.
The blades tore through the air, sailing inward.
Their impact sent a shock through the air, as they crashed into Connor's swiftly-raised forearms.
"No..." he gurgled. "NO!"
Weiss struggled, but couldn't break past the Werewolf's determined guard.
Connor was a lot of things. A lot more, lately.
He was not someone who knew when to die.
"GRAAAAGH!" Sparky howled, slamming into Weiss's side. He skidded to a halt as Weiss crashed into a wall.
Connor spat a globule of blood, and forced himself to rise.
Weiss planted his blades in the ground, using their support to get back up. He looked at the dead mastiffs, and laughed cruelly.
"Oh dear, Six. It's not the first time you've seen your misbegotten kind die, is it? First time you've killed them, though." the Commandant chimed in.
"You killed them. We just put them out of their misery." Sparky snarled grimly.
"And if you wanna see 'killing,' then we're happy to oblige." Connor added. He readied his blades, as his lungs scabbed over.
Weiss gripped the hilts tightly, as his eyes frantically darted side to side.
The Werewolves charged in.
Sparky hit first. Weiss's blade cut across his muzzle, but the crimson-eyed beast was unfazed. His teeth sank into Weiss's side, ripping out chunks of hot meat.
Connor, meanwhile, sent his own blades down - ripping off Weiss's right hand. He screamed in pain, but then Sparky's claws sank into his throat.
Connor grabbed the fallen machete, and swept as hard as he could.
Weiss's body fell to the ground. His head landed a few feet away.
"Credit where it's due." the Commandant grinned. "The data we've gained from this encounter is... invaluable."
Sparky's hackles rose.
Connor didn't move. His blades stayed forward, tense. "We're coming for you. We're going to make you feel everything you did to us. But before we do - where's 217?"
The Commandant tilted his head, as if considering it. "Still on that, huh?"
A faint crackle ran through the walls.
Connor noticed it first. Not heard - felt. A low, irregular pulse under the metal floor. His eyes narrowed.
"Well, now." the Commandant grinned. "Isn't this a nice trip down memory lane?"
Connor's stare tightened. "What did you do?"
"Not the first time Six has watched his kind die because of me. And definitely not the first time you've been buried."
Connor went still. He tilted his head, but then, his irises shrank to pinpoints. His twin heartbeats turned to hums of terror.
"Ah... the penny drops."
Sparky's red gaze darted to his packmate. "Connor...?"
"RUN!" he screamed.
"What?! Why?!"
"PLASTIC! IN THE WALLS!"
Too late.
The sound came first: a deep, mechanical *thump* from inside the walls.
Then heat.
The world around him erupted into flame. A massive explosion shook the building, and down it came.

Sparky pulled himself out of the wreckage first. He frantically started digging, cutting his paw pads apart on the shrapnel. Finally, he unearthed Connor, who took a sharp intake of breath.
The ringing in his ears was deafening. His vision blurred as he came to, the world around him a hazy mess of rubble and smoke. He groaned, disoriented, but alive.
"Connor! Come on, we've got to go! The police will be here soon!"
The sound of sirens wailing in the distance cut through the haze. Connor tried to rise, but looked back in horror. His hand was pinned under an entire wall's worth of rubble.
His hearts raced as panic set in. He tugged at the debris, his breath coming in sharp gasps, but his hand remained firmly caught.
"No... no, no, no..." he stuttered.
He shifted his weight, tried to use his enhanced strength, but it was useless. His hand was trapped, and couldn't leave unless he freed it.
Sparky put all his might into shifting the mess, but he could barely budge it.
"Connor, I... I'm not strong enough." he cried.
A grim realisation dawned on him.
He morphed a serrated dagger from his free hand. The blade's edge gleamed under the light. He gritted his teeth and pressed it into his trapped forearm.
Squelch.
*SHIK!*
The world slowed. The pain was like nothing he'd ever felt before - sharp, intense, and relentless.
It coursed through him, amplifying every sensation, turning his vision dark at the edges.
Squelch again.
*SHIK-SHIK!*
The flesh had been severed. Only bone left - too strong for even his knife to saw through.
He grimaced, pale as a ghost, and his stomach heaved.
*HAWK-ptoo!*
The bone hissed as the acid bit through it. After a while, it was weak enough.
*SHIK-SHIK!*
*SHHIK!*
*SNAP.*
With a final, brutal motion, he freed himself, his body recoiling from the sheer agony of it all. As he pulled free, the flesh ripped, and his wrist-joint popped.
He rose to his feet, breathless, bloodied, and battered.
The world slowed down to a standstill. If normal pain could sharpen a mind - what could this pain do?
He tensed, squeezing his eyes shut. Despite his eyelids being closed, his irises glowed with such a bright, brilliant red, that they shone past the skin. The glow faded, and the bleeding finally stopped. A bark from Sparky shook him from his stupor, and the pair sprinted up a wall back to their overlook, just as police and fire engines poured onto the site.

It had been a hundred years. It had been a couple of seconds.
Connor woke up on the roof. As he did, Sparky rushed over, and licked his face frantically.
"Are you okay?" he asked, worried.
"Hah... I'm fine. Just blacked out for a moment." he nodded, his head feeling both light and heavy.
He morphed back down into his civilian clothes, and reached into his pocket. He pulled out a small pack of cigarettes.
"Thank god these weren't squished back there." he chuckled, holding one in front of his mouth, and taking a deep breath.
*FWOOSH!*
"Tell you what, the fire-breath thingy is a little weird, but it's better than trying to light it one-handed." he chuckled, inhaling.
"Why do you burn those stinky sticks?" Sparky asked.
"Honestly? Not much use to me these days. Downside of being a super-powered mutant is you can't get anything out of nicotine." he shrugged. "Still, the ritual of it... calms me down, I guess."
Sparky nodded, and sat down with his packmate. "What happens now?" he asked.
"Now? Now we see if I can grow back limbs." Connor laughed and coughed. "If I can't... we may need to rethink our operation."
He leant back. "I've been putting off asking you... maybe because I'm scared about what you'll say. But... this whole thing with the Commandant..."
"He was... he was right." Sparky said, nearly gagging on the words. "We thought we were better, but all it took was one phone call, and it's like we're back in the cages again."
Connor looked away for a minute, before turning back.
"My memories of it all... they're jumbled. I can't remember us escaping the first time. But... you remember, don't you?"
Sparky thought for a moment.
"All my life, I remember being in this cage. Being hurt by the bad men." he began. "And none of them ever cared. They just kept hurting me. Sometimes it was for fun, sometimes I think they were being curious, but it was always cruel. And..." he paused. "And then, one day, the bad men brought you in. You were hurt, badly - but alive. They chained you up. I wasn't in the room, but I could hear you screaming as they hurt you too."
Connor leant forward, listening intently.
"I kept hearing them talking about programming. I think they wanted to train you. They kept saying these specific words - 'Listen and Obey.'"
Connor flinched at the words. Even hearing them telepathically from a wolf didn't soften their blow.
"Every time they did, they hurt you." Sparky continued. "One day, your screaming was louder than ever before. I heard you thrashing and smashing about, and you burst out of the door they held you in. You came running out, and I knew you were different. You looked at me, and I didn't see cruelty, just sadness. I begged you to let me out, and you heard me, and you saved me."
Connor rubbed his temples with his intact hand, processing. "So... we broke out, together?"
"Yes. We ran for miles and miles." Sparky nodded. "But they caught up to us. They overwhelmed us, and dragged us away. They separated us - I never knew what happened to you afterwards."
Connor looked downwards. "And... they brought you back to the place I found you?"
"No. They brought me back to where we first met." Sparky explained. "But they were worried you might come back for me. So they moved me to the other place - the one where you and Ivan saved me."
Connor leaned back. "Did, um... did you ever see me leave on my own? The Commandant... he pulled a trick on me."
"No, never." Sparky shook his head.
Connor stroked his chin. "Makes sense. We escape together, then after they bring us both back, the Commandant stages another escape to crush my hopes of leaving. They must've somehow wiped my memories of us getting out, too."
Sparky looked away. "They were cruel. After meeting so many normal humans, I don't get it. Humans aren't so different to wolves. You're naturally decent. So what's wrong with them?"
Connor sighed. "I don't know, bud. They're cruel. Evil. Monsters..."
Sparky thought for a moment. "The word he used - Fenrir. That's what they called me, too."
"Project Fenrir... that explains the super-dogs earlier." Connor nodded, visibly joining the dots. "Whatever made me and the Super-Agents, Fenrir made you and the dogs."
"They're building an army." Sparky realised in horror.
"Still... it doesn't explain why we're different. I mean, you couldn't hear the dogs talking, could you?"
"No, nothing." Sparky shook his head. "This weird connection we have... I think it's just us."

Connor paused. He closed his eyes, hesitated, and reached out. He placed his hand on Sparky's head, and their minds touched.
Fear. Pain. Rage. All swirling through his mind, like he was pulling the depths of the ocean to the surface. He reached inwards, and Sparky let him in.
This 'mind-bond' they had was utterly alien to both. The flashes, the shared dreams... and now this. Connor stretched his mind's eye into Sparky's subconsciousness, and watched. He saw himself, bursting out of a steel door, drenched in blood and howling in pain. He felt Sparky's memories, his emotions, his senses... the link grew stronger. Sparky saw through Connor's eyes, and Connor through Sparky's. The line where one ended and the other began had blurred into oblivion.
Connor saw the torment Sparky had faced. Horrific surgeries. Agonising tests. He watched as the rest of Sparky's litter died, until only he was left.
Sparky saw Connor's own pain. The torments. And others, too. He saw the moment Vince was arrested. He saw the days in the Bogside where Connor's grandfather beat him. He saw a blazing inferno in a car, with his grandmother's burning corpse hanging out. And deeper still, he watched through Connor's eyes as drove a fire poker through an older man's head.
It was too much. The two pulled back, and rubbed their skulls, regaining their senses of self. After a short while, they both opened their eyes, and looked down - as though visibly checking which one they were.
"You okay?" Connor asked.
