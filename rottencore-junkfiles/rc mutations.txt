Every Gene Mod reduces Metabolic Efficiency, unless otherwise specified
Reproduction System genes do not affect efficiency unless you have more than 1

Combat Pheromones
	Increase Adrenaline of host and allies when killing an enemy
Circulatory Distribution
	Grows secondary mico-hearts throghout the body, can survive loss of a heart
Electrosensitive Skin
	Can sense enemies, even cloaked or invisible ones, by detecting electrical signatures.
Chameleonic Skin
	Can change skin colour and properties to blend in
Shapeshifting
	Can change physical appearance to resemble specific individuals, or with time, entirely separate species
Third Eye
	Special Eye in forehead, improves psychic abilities
Syanptic Evolution
	Neural modifications in brain improves psy power, focus generation, synaptic pressure, and learning speed, but reduces social skills
Advanced Organ Signalling
	Neural modifications across body means that medical information is always up-to-date and accurate
Muscle Fibre Density
	Extra dense muscles make flesh hardier and stronger
Twitch Reflex
	Improved reflexes and reaction times
Regeneration
	Heals scars, wounds, and even regrows body parts
Superclotting
	Bleeding wounds close very quickly
Wakeful
	Host needs to sleep half as much as a baseliner
Circadian Wakefulness
	Never needs to sleep. Twice as much metabolic efficiency loss as other mods
Streamlined Genetics
	Adds extra Metabolic Efficiency
Supermetabolism
	Halves all Metabolic Efficiency losses from gene mods
Metabolic Unlocking
	Halves all Metabolic Efficiency losses from gene mods (stacks with supermetabolism), but causes at least 1 of the following daily: Migraines, Seizures, Heart attacks, carcinoma. Character is also sterile.
Robust Digestion
	Can eat almost anything
Acid Blood
	Burns others on contact, also makes host immune to acid
Acid Spit
	Spits acid at target
Ironskin
	Half of all Sharp damage is converted to Blunt damage
Hardened Bones
	Bones are much harder to break
Sensory Enhancement
	Sight, Hearing, and Smell are all improved dramatically
Night Vision
	Can see clearly even in pitch blackness
Pheromones
	Can release Pheromones which induce desired mental states in others
Adaptable Biology
	Allows the host to adapt to any biome's conditions
Neural Chest Cavity
	Allows the host to survive decapitation
Independent Eyes
	Allows the host's eyes to point in different directions
Expansive Stomach
	Host can gorge on food and stretch the stomach to fit it in
Hyperoxygenated Blood
	Blood carries far more oxygen than usual, allowing host to perform astonishing feats of endurance
Melanic Skin
	Skin can turn pitch black to shield host from ionising radiation
Antioxident Hyperproduction
	Host is much more resistant to ionising radiation, and also lives longer than baseliners, but has reduced fertility
Breathable Skin
	Can breathe through skin - can survive loss of lungs
Anaerobic Optimisation
	Can survive off of anaerobic respiration - can live without oxygen, though endurance is reduced when this is active
Hyper Immunity
	Can fight off infections and tumours from twice as well to up to 10x as well as a baseliner
Smart Immunity
	Can manually target immune response against specific diseases and tumours
Spare Organ
	Grows a spare organ. Can be used in case of destruction of original. Does not apply to brain.
Geminitism
	Grows a second head. One head controls the body at a time.
Calcification
	Flesh slowly becomes calcified, making host much hardier but also slower
Exoskeleton
	Skin becomes chitinous, encasing host in armour
Xenophilia
	Host can reproduce with alien species
Venomous Fangs
	Can bite enemies to inject with venom, dealing tox damage or paralysing them
Perfect Memory
	Host almost never forgets a detail - both good and bad
Agelessness
	Host does not age past maturity - becoming biologically immortal
Long Lived
	Host ages past maturity at half the rate of a baseliner
Gills
	Host can breathe underwater
Chloroplastic Skin
	Host can perform photosynthesis
Fissile Reproduction
	Host can shed spores, which eventually grow into spawn
Incorporate Genes
	Host can incorporate the genetics of other creatures
Spawning Pool
	Host can interact with Spawning Pools, and can produce spawn from them
Seedling Reproduction
	Host can reproduce by producing Seedlings, which are planted into the ground
Vatgrown
	Host cannot reproduce normally, but has bonuses to being grown in a vat
Egglaying
	Host can reproduce by laying eggs
Viviparity
	Host can reproduce via live birth
Hermaphroditism
	Host can both fertilise and be fertilised
Parthenogenesis
	Host can produce spawn by themselves, without needing to be fertilised. (does not apply to pure fertilisers)
Rapid Development
	Host's spawn reach maturity faster than baseliners
Haplo-Diploidity
	Hosts that can be fertilised can produce fertiliser offspring parthenogenetically.
Tox Cloud
	Host can release toxic gas, poisoning those without immunity
Virus Maker
	Host can produce pathogens to infect enemies
Parasitic Injection
	Host can inject larval spawn into target. Birthing will kill target
Web weaver
	Host can weave webs, to be used for various purposes
Metamorphosis Cocoon
	Host can enter a cocoon where they can heal and reconfigure their body
Cryptobiosis
	Host can enter a coma where their biology is suspended, allowing them to survive prolonged periods without necessities of life
