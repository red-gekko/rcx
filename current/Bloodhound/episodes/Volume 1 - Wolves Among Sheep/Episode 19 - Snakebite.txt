	He rose, grabbed Snakebite's jacket, and dragged him into the tower block. There, an industrial lift stood ahead.
	His ears pricked up. Skinners. Disciples. The occasional ex-Grin.
	He recognised a few scents, even - these were his usual prey.
	"He's here!" one barked.
	"You're sure?"
	"Yeah! I heard from a little birdie that he's been poisoned. We can take him!"
	Connor cracked his neck, and threw Snakebite into the lift. Then he sent it down, and stumbled forwards.
	He stood at the head of the stairway, and rolled his shoulders. The first pair of Skinners had turned the corner.
	"COME ON, YOU BASTARDS! COME AND GET ME!"

	The first thug came around the corner with a crowbar raised.
	Connor met him head-on.
	His fist connected with the man's jaw - a solid hit, but not the bone-shattering blow he was used to. The man stumbled, more surprised than hurt, and swung the crowbar at Connor's ribs.
	It connected.
	Connor grunted, feeling the impact reverberate through his body. It hurt - really hurt, the way it would have before. Before the serum. Before the Commandant. Before he became something more than human.
	But he was still standing.
	He grabbed the crowbar before the Skinner could swing again, yanked it from his hands, and drove his knee into the man's gut. He doubled over, gasping, and Connor brought the crowbar down on the back of his head.
	The man dropped.
	Connor didn't wait. He stepped over the body and kept moving, his boots echoing in the stairwell. The lift had already descended - Snakebite was on his way down, paralysed and helpless. All Connor had to do was follow.
	Just keep moving. One step at a time.
	A Disciple appeared on the landing below, a machete in one hand. He charged up the stairs, screaming.
	Connor met him halfway.
	He ducked under the machete swing and drove his fist into the man's chest. The impact was harder this time - he felt it, felt the ribs give slightly under his knuckles. The Disciple gasped, stumbling back, and Connor followed with an uppercut that snapped his head back.
	That was stronger.
	The man collapsed, and Connor looked down at his hands. They were still shaking, still weak - but there was something there. A flicker of the power he'd lost.
	The antidote. It was working.
	He didn't have time to savour it. More footsteps below - a whole squad of them, by the sound of it.
	The third and fourth Skinners came together, one with a baseball bat, the other with a length of chain. They attacked in tandem, trying to flank him.
	Connor moved on instinct, ducking under the bat and catching the chain before it could wrap around his throat. He yanked hard, pulling the chain-wielder off balance, and drove his elbow into the man's temple.
	The Skinner went down. The bat-wielder swung again, and Connor took the hit on his shoulder - hard - but this time, he barely felt it.
	He turned, grabbed the man by the collar, and slammed him into the wall. Once. Twice. The third time, the man stopped moving.
	Connor released him and kept going.
	Stronger. Faster. It was coming back.
	A pair of Grins were waiting on the next landing, both armed with handguns.
	Connor didn't slow down. He raised his grapple gun, and fired.
	It made a direct hit, gripping onto the pistol. He yanked, disarming the Grin, before moving faster than he had any right to. The first bullet grazed his shoulder - painful, but shallow. The second missed entirely. By the time they'd adjusted their aim, he was already on them.
	He grabbed the first gunman's wrist and twisted, feeling the bones snap under his grip. The man screamed. Connor drove his fist into his face - hard - and felt the jaw give way.
	That was almost full strength.
	The second Grin fired again, and this time, Connor took the bullet in the chest. It should have dropped him. Should have at least winded him.
	Instead, he barely stumbled.
	He looked down at the wound - already closing, the flesh knitting itself back together.
	"There it is." he grinned.
	He grabbed the second gunman by the throat and lifted him off his feet. The man choked, clawing at Connor's arm, his eyes wide with terror.
	Connor dropped him, and kept moving.
	The ground floor was a wide, open space - some kind of reception area, now repurposed by the Disciples - it seemed that before Snakebite came to roost, this was one of their depots. Crates of weapons and supplies lined the walls, and a dozen thugs were waiting, armed with everything from pipes to pistols.
	In the centre of the room, the industrial lift sat motionless, its doors open. Snakebite was still inside, crumpled and twitching, his eyes open but unseeing.
	Connor stepped off the stairs and rolled his shoulders.
	"Come on, then."
	They came at him all at once.
	The first wave was easy - his strength was returning now, and each punch landed with the force of a sledgehammer. He grabbed a man by the leg and swung him into two others, sending all three sprawling. A pipe cracked against his face, and he barely felt it.
	His hands swept the floor, grabbing a dropped length of chain. It snapped out, blinding one man. A second swing wrapped around another man's neck, and choked him out. Connor yanked him forwards, and drove a fist into his head.
	The second wave was harder - they'd learned, hanging back, using their numbers to try and overwhelm him. A knife found its way into his side, and he grunted, pulling it out and snapping the blade in his fist.
	He could feel it building. The power. The Rage. The thing that made him what he was.
	Almost there.
	A rifle butt caught him in the face, and he staggered - but only for a moment. He caught the weapon before the man could swing again, tore it from his hands, and bent the barrel in half.
	The Skinner stared at him, frozen. A strike to the solar plexus sent him down and out.
	The last few thugs backed away, weapons raised and eyes wide.
	They'd seen what he could do. They knew what he was.
	He was *back.*
	"Stay away!" one of them shouted, his voice cracking. "I'll shoot! I swear to God, I'll shoot!"
	Connor took a step forward.
	The man fired. Three shots - chest, stomach, shoulder. Each one hit its mark.
	Nothing.
	He kept walking. The bullets clinked like coins as they were pushed out of his flesh.
	He grabbed the gunman by the head, and squeezed, just a little. The man's temples creaked under the pressure.
	"Let me make myself clear." he whispered. "No one can stop me. Nothing can kill me. Not men. Not weapons. Not armour. I am the Werewolf, and THIS IS *MY* CITY!"
	He released the man, who collapsed to the floor, sobbing.
	Connor turned away.
	And then - finally - he felt it.
	The shift. The ripple. Biomass surging through his veins, ready to be shaped.
	And - there. The right side of his chest thrummed, and one pair of lungs rose as another fell.
	His clothes began to tear.
	The hoodie split along the seams, fabric ripping away as chitin burst forth from his skin. The ski mask tore out as pressurised tar shot from his face. His hands curled into fists, and he sent out a flurry of hooks. His clawed wristblades shot out, carving through the air.
	He stood there for a moment, breathing hard. The tower block fell silent.
	Connor walked to the industrial lift, where Snakebite still lay paralysed. The assassin's eyes moved - following him, wide with fear - but the rest of his body was still.
	He pulled out his phone and dialled.
	"Doc? It's me."
	Judi's voice was tired, but relieved. "Are you okay?"
	"I'm... better." he began. "Listen, there's going to be a patient coming into A&E soon. Snakebite - the assassin. He's been dosed with his own neocurare. When he arrives, I need you to keep him under. Use NMBAs - keep him paralysed."
	Judi hesitated. "That's... that's not exactly ethical. I'm a doctor. I took an oath."
	"I know." Connor said quietly. "But this man - he's not going to hurt anyone else. Not if we can help it. And if he's awake, he'll find a way to escape. He's too dangerous."
	A long pause.
	"Please, doc. I'm not asking you to torture him. I'm asking you to keep him sedated. That's it."
	Another pause.
	"Fine." she said finally.
	"Thank you."
	He hung up, and turned back to Snakebite, who was still staring at him with wide, terrified eyes.
	"Someone will be here for you soon." Connor said. "Until then... enjoy the taste of your own medicine."
	He turned and walked away, his costume rippling with each step.
	The tower was taken.
	Now, the castle.

	Sparky whimpered softly, as he tried and failed to raise his head.
	The venom was strong. Stronger than anything he'd ever had. His body was weak, as his eyes watered.

	"That's... that's not my name..." he tried to whisper. "I'm Sparky. Connor's packmate."

	He wanted to scream. Wanted to cry.
	His chest heaved, as blood flushed out of his body, and into vials of fresh serum.
