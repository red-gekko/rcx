EPISODE - Judgement Day, Part 1 - Valley of the Shadow of Death
============================================================

It had been a chaotic few days - but days, nonetheless. The sun dragged itself up, whispering over the valley.
Chloe was sitting on a bench in the repair shop, sipping a latte. Amy approached.
"Hey, girl! How are you holding up?" she asked, hugging Chloe.
"Heya! Yeah, I'm doing okay." she replied, hugging back.
Trey and Shannon followed in after.
"Ladies, ladies!" Trey smiled. "Good to see you both."
"Hey, guys!" Shannon added, smiling.
"Hah, thanks." Chloe smiled back.
"So, um..." Trey began awkwardly. "Should probably clarify I know that you know."
She chuckled. "And you also know that I know that you know?"
"Something like that. My head hurts, now." he grinned.
"How, uh... how are you taking it?" Amy asked.
"Better than I thought I would. I mean, it's all so... much. But we're together, which means we'll deal with it together." Chloe shrugged.
"That's good... it sounds like you're coping well. Did he, um... did he mention Sparky?" Trey asked.
"You mean the talking dog?" Chloe quipped, smirking.
They laughed.
"Hah, that's the one. I, um... I know it's a lot to take in. We're here if you need to vent." he smiled softly.
"I just..." Chloe began. "I get why he didn't tell me. Hell, I can't judge him - I don't know for a fact I would have told him if our roles were reversed. But I... I just wish he had told me. Not even just for my sake - obviously I'm not thrilled that he lied to me, but I'm more upset at seeing how it's hurt him. I... I know how honest Connor naturally is. And I can see how much he hated lying to me. And... part of me worries if I can still trust him after all of this. I mean, I do trust him... but... I don't know. It's strange... I'm terrified for him, but I also feel closer to him than I ever have before. Like, despite all the danger, he's more real to me now." Chloe said, lost in thought.
"Oh..." Amy replied guiltily. "It, uh... it's my fault. I'm the one who told him not to tell you."
Chloe looked over at her, surprised. "You did?"
"Yeah. I was worried that telling you would force you to choose a side, and I knew that you'd choose his - at which point you'd have had an even bigger target on your back. Instead, I was wrong. Not telling you just meant you were less prepared when the time came. I'm sorry."
Chloe took a moment to process. "I... thank you. You were looking out for me, and hey, we don't know you were wrong. If I knew sooner, maybe the gunman would have just killed me - he prepared to before Connor made them both fall. I think he just wanted to taunt him." she mused. "Either way... I get why you told him not to. And... thank you for telling me. It helps clear things up a little."
"I'm so sorry, Chlo. If I had any idea something so big was going down, I'd never have told him that." Amy swore. Chloe smiled warmly and hugged her.
"I know." Chloe whispered. "It's okay."
Amy pulled back from the hug, giving Chloe a small, tentative smile.
"You're strong, you know that?" Trey said softly, a hint of admiration in his voice.
Chloe chuckled mirthlessly. "I don't feel strong. I feel... scared. Scared for my own safety, of course, after the incident at the lab. But mainly, I'm terrified about Connor. You guys... he fell 13 stories, snapped his spine in half, got filled with over 100 assault rifle bullets - all in one night. And he's acting like he's fine. I don't know what scares me more - the idea that he's lying to me again, or that he's telling the truth. Because... what kind of person would be fine with all that? How can he cope?"
"Connor's always been... resilient." Trey began, his tone soft, almost wistful. "Even before the powers, he'd been through a hell of a lot. And he was always good at compartmentalising."
Chloe looked up at him, and smiled sadly.
"Look, Chlo..." he continued. "When I found out, I was pissed off with him. Rightfully so, but still. I realised... Connor's a good man. So, even though he did the wrong thing by keeping this a secret... he had good intentions. And that's gotta count for something."
Chloe nodded. "I don't believe he was trying to deceive me or anything. I just... I'm worried is all. His body might be... whatever it is, but his mind is still human. How's he supposed to cope with this? I mean, he... he kills people. And I know, he has to, but..."
"He's a tough cookie." Amy began, smiling wistfully. "It sucks that Connor kills. But... he'd never kill someone who didn't need killing. And it's up to us - his friends - to help him cope when he does."
"Being a superhero ain't like what the movies say." Trey added. "It doesn't just mean saving good people... it means stopping bad people. And these bad people..." he shuddered, "They gotta be stopped. That's just what Connor's like. And hell, if any of us had his powers, we'd be the exact same."
"That's the thing." Amy nodded. "The Werewolf isn't a monster, or an anomaly. It's the natural extension of who Connor truly is. His powers might have been forced on him, but... he chose to be the Werewolf just as much as I chose to be Stunlocke. You..." she paused, searching for the words. "You know the real Connor. All those times you've seen him, they were real. That's who he is. The funny, kind, loving, annoying, endearingly awkward guy - that's the real Connor."
Chloe sat back, processing for a moment. "You mean... everything we had... every moment we shared..."
"Was real. As real as it gets." Trey nodded.
Chloe took a deep breath, and smiled.
"You know..." Shannon said, having kept quiet until now. "Connor saved my life. I was attacked by some prick, after my parents kicked me out. I was thinking about just lying down and letting it happen, and then out of nowhere, this superhero just shows up, beats the guy up, and sits with me. He was wearing his full costume, but... he was so, *so* human in that moment. He just sat there, spoke softly, and called Tommy to get me somewhere to stay. I know I've not known you guys for long, but... he's a good guy. And yeah, it's a shame he lied to you... but from what I can tell, he's no different with the mask on or off."
Chloe smiled softly. "Thank you, guys... thank you so much, for all of this."
Amy grinned. "Don't tell him I said anything nice about him."

Chloe laughed, and the group went to go get brunch at Raj's rebuilt cafe. Not long after, Bea joined them, fresh from university.
They had a catch-up about normal, everyday, human things. Chloe piped up.
"So, anyway... my birthday's in a couple of weeks - on the 6th. I'm gonna be going back up home for it, but I'd really appreciate it if you all came with me."
"You just got nearly murdered, and you're thinking about your birthday?" Bea asked, shocked.
"I, um..." Chloe began, looking down. Bea pulled her in for an embrace.
"I love you." she whispered. "You are the strongest person I know. Only you..." her voice cracked, "Only you could face death and be so strong."
"I'd love to come." Amy said, smiling warmly.
"You bet I'll be there - Doubt it'll be as drunken as mine, but that's okay." Trey quipped, causing Chloe to chuckle.
"And obviously I'll be there - I haven't missed a birthday of yours since we met." Bea smiled.
"Am I invited too?" Shannon asked timidly.
"Of course." Chloe smiled, before pausing. "My mum, she... she can be a horrible cow. I... it's up to you if you wanna come, I'd--I'd hate it if she said some bigoted shit and hurt you."
Shannon shrugged. "For me, that's a Tuesday."
Chloe smiled warmly. "Thank you all... I'm really happy that I'll have you there."
She lent into her chair. Despite everything, it was nice to have some normality.

That afternoon, Naismith stood alone in a briefing room, facing a wall with notes on it. Mayor Ward entered.
Naismith clicked his heels and stood to attention, turning to face her. He was wearing sunglasses - to conceal the black-veined eyes of the Super-Agent.
"As you were." Ward said. Naismith's posture relaxed slightly.
"Ma'am. It's good to be back."
"How are you feeling, Henry?" Ward asked, a hint of concern breaking through her professional, stoic demeanour.
"I... to tell you the truth, better than ever, ma'am." Naismith began. "Whatever the Werewolf gave me, it's invigorated me. I feel... well, to coin a phrase, superhuman."
"And... how is your mental state?"
"Ironically, better than before. I... I've been struggling with a lack of purpose lately, but this... it feels like a new lease on life."
"Any... any violent urges?" Ward questioned, getting closer to her point - Is he safe?
"None, ma'am. I definitely feel more *active,* but in no way am I like the men we've been fighting." Naismith replied firmly.
That was mostly true. Or rather - pain was such an old friend of his, he was skilled in forcing it down..
"Good to hear." Ward said, clearly relieved. "In that case, please attend my office at 12PM. I've called in Nina, too - we're having a briefing to assess our next steps."
"Very good, ma'am." Naismith nodded.

At the same time, Connor and Chloe were in a supermarket, buying food.
"Ah - these ones. They're Sparky's favourite." Connor said, picking up some processed meats.
"Hah - I never imagined you eating this much junk food." Chloe chuckled.
"It's good for calories - from what I've worked out, I need to eat at least twice as much as a normal person to maintain my edge. Sparky seems to be the same." Connor explained.
"Well, you definitely make it work. You must work out constantly to keep those muscles." she smiled.
"Actually..." he began, "I barely work out these days. I go for jogs with Sparky, but that's a mix of patrolling the city, and burning off our energy. I think my muscles just automatically build themselves."
"Sounds like steroids on steroids." Chloe quipped, eliciting chuckles from both.
"Come to think of it... I've been given leave from Uni since the incident at Vitalis - I'm going back after my birthday. When we come back, would you... would you like to come to the labs there with me?" she asked.
"Yeah, that'd be good - I was also thinking, can we see what happens when I burn myself? Like, there must be something that gets released there which counteracts the Paradrenaline." Connor nodded, curious himself.
"That's a very good idea." Chloe replied, eager to find a way to stop him from needing to harm himself. "Maybe there's some mutated endorphin-analogue that flushes the paradrenaline from your brain... hah, I'm rambling again." she caught herself.
Connor smiled brightly. "I love it when you talk nerdy." he grinned.
"Always a charmer, eh?" she winked, smirking.
The two paid, and walked to Connor's car. Connor was holding all the shopping in his hands.
Chloe put out her hand, offering to take some shopping. Oblivious to the offer, Connor shifted the shopping into one of his hands, and held her hand with his free one.
"That's not--nevermind." she smiled warmly.

Back in Ward's office, the two officers of TO-24 were assembled and waiting.
"How are you feeling, sir?" Nina asked hesitantly.
He took off his shades to reveal his eyes. "I'll never be the same man again, but... I'm not too cut up about that."
Nina gave him a caring glance. "It's... it's good to see you doing so well, sir." she said.
"If you hadn't covered me there, Nina, I'd be dead. So, thank you." he nodded. Nina smiled proudly.
"Um... any idea why Ward's called us in?"
"To give us all commendations and cushy desk jobs? Hey, a man can dream right?"
With that, Ward entered. The pair snapped to attention.
"As you were." she said, smiling. "Thank you all again for coming in on short notice. I won't sugar-coat it. It's a fucking mess in this city." she began. "The attack on the station - 17 officers lost their lives."
Ward's face looked more grim than ever. The team looked down at their feet.
"As if that's not bad enough, I've heard word from station chiefs. Over 40 letters of resignation have been sent in across all departments. Officers are terrified that they could be next. That's not even getting into the fact that so many officers either resigned or were arrested following the Dorsey fiasco." she continued.
Naismith winced at the news.
"So, as a result..." Ward sighed, "I have had to make the very, very difficult decision... to bring in outside help. Please come in, Mr. Sokolov."
With that, Ivan entered the office. They stood to attention, but Ivan raised his hand in an easing gesture.
"As you were." he said. "My name is Ivan Sokolov, codename Verdict. I am the KGB's specialist in all things... mutant." he began, removing his shades to reveal his own black-veined eyes.
"You..." Nina gasped. "You were there with the Giant, weren't you?"
"Well met, PS Chowdhury. Congratulations on your promotion. Speaking of... congratulation on your promotion from mankind to *this,* DCI Naismith." Ivan smirked, turning to face him.
"The KGB... what's the KGB doing here, Ma'am?" Nina asked.
"Mr. Sokolov is, as he said, a specialist in these matters. He has direct contact with the Werewolf, and important intel on the conspiracy we're facing." Ward answered, sitting down. "I... I have spoken with people at the Home Office, and confirmed that, in these exceptional circumstances, this collaboration is legitimate. Mr. Sokolov, please begin your report."
"Thank you, ma'am." Ivan began. "Here." he brought up a slide. "Our enemies. Plague. The Optimates."
"Who?" Ward asked.
"Fascist billionaires." Naismith replied nonchalantly.
"A cabal of the rich elites, dedicated to seizing power. They found something. Something... horrifying." Ivan explained. "Somehow, they discovered the means to change humanity into weaponry. This is where their 'Serum' comes from."
"The 'Serum' is derived from the Werewolf's blood." Naismith noted.
"Yes, I know - the same blood runs in both our veins." Ivan nodded. "Which is strange, as we believe that the Werewolf was created from the Serum. They must - they *must* have an original supply somewhere. One they used to make the Werewolf. According to our own studies on my blood, Super-Agents can be used to create serum, though our blood is weak and often causes minor or harmful mutations when used. The Werewolf's blood appears to be 'pure', for lack of a better term. We believe that sufficient injection of the proper serum can make one a 'purestrain' - a being that is to Agents what we are to humans. These 'Purestrains' can be used to create the proper serum." Ivan replied.
"That, or the Werewolf is the original source." Naismith shrugged.
"Nyet - the Werewolf became active after Paladin fielded its first Super-Agents." Ivan said, a flash over his eyes - a memory. For a split second, he was back in Sukhumi in 2009, fighting monster-men. He banished the memory.
"Hm." Nina replied, sceptical.
"Our knowledge is incomplete. Much of this is theory - hard-earned, but unproven. In truth, we are grasping at shadows, hoping the light reveals something useful. Scepticism is good. Trust nothing without verification. Hah, you'd be good in KGB, Nina." Ivan quipped.
"So, as you were saying, Mr. Sokolov." Ward interjected.
"Yes, ma'am. The attack at Vitalis and here was devastating - but Paladin also lost their top Agents. They'll likely start using increasingly unstable strains of mutant to fight us - in particular, unhinged, psychotic creatures, almost forever trapped in a Rage. Such things would be... highly problematic for the force to face on its own. Therefore, I and the Werewolf will be available to the police, to face these monsters for you."

In an extravagant country home, an old man stood at his balcony, looking out at stretches upon stretches of land.
The study room behind him had a coat of arms hanging on the walls - the coat of the House of de Vere - the Earls of Oxford.
A knock on the study door.
"Enter." he beckoned. A servant came in.
"Lord Oxford." he began, his voice trembling. "Your... guest is here."
"Bring him in." Oxford nodded.
Then entered a monster - grotesque, but thick with muscles. It was tall, and had dark grey skin, with blood-red eyes. Its face looked truly inhuman - at once too angular and too smooth, eyes too far apart for a human skull... the only word that could describe it was "alien."
It took a knee, placing its fist on the ground.
"Master." it said, with an ethereal tone.
The old man grinned.
"Judge. The Commandant's little toy has been wreaking havoc." he said.
"The youngblood?" the creature tilted its head.
"I'm tired of his vanity project." Lord Oxford shook his own. "It's high time he was reminded that he's not the only player in this game."
"What would you have me do, master?"
"Go to Drackhill. Get his attention. Draw him out. And... treat him the way your people do to heretic youngbloods."

It was late evening now, and Chloe was working on Connor's laptop in the break room, compiling the data they recovered into a single document.
As she worked, Connor and Sparky entered through the back. Both were in their costumes, and Connor's was noticeably singed. They walked in, grabbed the food Chloe left out for them, and ate.
Chloe looked up, and smiled. "How'd it go?" she asked.
"Ugh..." Connor groaned. "Word to the wise - petrol bombs sting."
Chloe smirked. "Damn, there goes my weekend plans."
Connor chuckled, and began making himself a protein shake.
Sparky un-morphed his costume, and trotted over.
"Hi Chloe! We did good today. We fought a bunch of bad guys, and we managed to smash all their guns - so no one will get hurt with them!"
Chloe smiled, and stroked him. "Well done, buddy."
Sparky barked happily, jumping up and licking her. Connor came into the room, now wearing his civilian clothes.
"Oh god!" Chloe gasped. "Your back!"
Connor turned his head. "What? What's wrong?"
Chloe moved him over to a mirror, and pointed out the knife sticking out of his back.
Connor chuckled. "Sparky? How come you never told me I've been running around with a big knife in my back?"
"I assumed you knew!" Sparky retorted.
"Hah! Fair enough." Connor nodded. He reached to grab it, but couldn't quite get to it.
"It's at an awkward angle..." Chloe shuddered. "I, um... want me to grab it?"
"Oh, please!" Connor smiled.
Chloe half-smiled back, and gripped the handle.
"Okay... 3, 2, 1..." she said, before trying to yank it out. It didn't budge.
"It's stuck in there..." she shuddered.
Connor un-morphed his shirt, and pulled his arm out to widen the wound. "Try gripping it as hard as you can, and I'll pull." he said.
She did this, and it came out.
"You alright?" she asked worriedly. Connor smiled.
"Yeah... that feels a lot better."
Chloe smiled a little more now. She looked at the wound.
"Oh my god... that's amazing!" she gasped. She pulled out her phone, and started filming.
"What is it?" Connor asked.
"The wound... I can see it healing!" she replied, awestruck.
Connor pulled his arm out to widen the wound. "That a better angle?" he asked.
"Yes. Yes!" she nodded, zooming in. "I can... this is amazing. I can physically see the flesh almost knitting itself back together."
"Really?" Connor tilted his head.
"That's... okay, future Chloe, see that stuff there? That's not blood, that's liquefied muscle - look at the fibres inside it. See how it's solidifying into flesh. And..." she noted to herself, before pointing. "Look at the skin re-forming."
The healing process finished up, leaving a reddish-pink scar.
"Okay, that'll take a bit longer. I've noticed your body leaves cosmetic damage 'til later." she finished, putting her phone away.
Connor turned, and chuckled. "I guess looking pretty is secondary to kicking arse, eh?"
Chloe giggled, then found herself looking closely at Connor's chest. His muscles were incredibly toned - like a marble sculpture. He had next to no real body fat - everything beneath the skin was muscle. The scientist in her was in marvel at this precision-engineered specimen. The human being, the girl in love, however...
"Like what you see?" Connor smirked, flexing slightly.
Chloe blushed and looked down. "Oh, uh... sorry, I didn't mean to stare..."
Connor laughed, and re-morphed his shirt. "Hah, it's okay. I used to be so scrawny, too - one advantage of the morphing is not having to buy a new wardrobe, haha."
Chloe chuckled shyly. "I guess it's the mundane things, huh? I've been meaning to ask... what the hell is that thing?"
"No clue." Connor grinned. "After I escaped the lab, I felt this... pressure, building up under my skin. Like I was going to explode. Then this weird, black, tarry gel erupted from my pores, and covered me in itself, before hardening into some kind of armour. After a little while, I learned how to control it - mimic clothing, or make up a costume, you know?"
Chloe stroked her chin, with a great deal of awe coming over her face. "That's incredible. I... when we get access to a lab, I'd love to take a look at that."
Connor smiled. "That reminds me - the hoodie I gave you? Do you still have it?"
"Oh, yeah I still do. I, uh... I wear it a lot." she blushed. "It's in the wash currently."
He chuckled. "It's still fine? Not... decomposed, or anything?"
"...why would it decompose?" she tilted her head.
"Well, I morphed it." he shrugged. "Just wondering how long my morphed stuff lasts, is all. If it's still good, then there's a chance someone's out there with a collection of my knives."
She paused. "...I'm not sure if I should be pleased or grossed out. I mean, on the one hand, I'll always have a part of you..."
"Yeah, but most of the time when people say that, they mean a memento. Not a bodily secretion." he grinned.
She shuddered, chuckling softly, before sitting down on the sofa.
"Now that you mention it, though - it has gotten darker since you gave it to me. Hah... I just thought of some more tests to run."

Connor joined her. As he sat, he groaned slightly.
"You okay?" she asked, slightly concerned.
"Yeah - spine's still hurting from the fall. It's mostly done healing, but some dick decided to offload a shotgun into it."
"Do you need to get the bullets out?" she asked, putting her hand on his shoulder.
"Nah, I'm good - none of the bullets went too far in. They've all been pushed out naturally. The real problem was the impact - there's still a couple of spinal fractures that got rocked by the blast." he explained, leaning his head on her shoulder and closing his eyes. Chloe stroked his back.
"The thing about healing this fast is, it doesn't mean the pain just goes away. It's like my body skips ahead, but my nerves take a while to catch up." he continued, stretching his arms out, before recoiling in pain as his back clicked. "And something as nerve-heavy as the back, well... I'm in no hurry to break that again."
"Do you want a back rub?" she asked shyly.
"Hah - that might actually be nice." Connor smiled. He rose, and laid down face-first on the sofa. Chloe stood over him.
"Okay, let me know when I'm hurting you." she said unsurely. She began to knead his lower back.
As she travelled closer to his spine, Connor's leg shook.
"Oh - did that hurt?" she asked, concerned.
"Nah, that's good actually. Think it's just the nerves tingling." he replied.
She kept kneading his back, and a loud cracking noise snapped out from his spine.
"Crap!" she gasped, startled.
"AaaAaAAh!" Connor yelped, before jumping to his feet. "That... THAT was the spot!" he laughed.
Chloe laughed too. "You feel better?"
"Way, *way* better now. I think you clicked something back into place there." he smiled, sitting down - this time much more smoothly.
Chloe sat down too, and snuggled up next to him. "Well, there's one bonus of me knowing about all this. If I didn't, who knows how long you'd have back-ache."
Connor nodded, smiling. "Having a sidekick is *sooo* underrated." he quipped. Chloe smirked, and brushed his side.
Connor squirmed. "Hah! Hey... that tickled." he laughed.
Chloe paused, eyebrows raised. "You get ticklish?"
Realisation dawned on his face. "No... don't you dare..." he began.
Too late. Chloe pounced, tickling him. He squirmed, cackling like a hyena.
"HAHAHA! Hey! Stop! HAHAHAA!" he laughed.
"What's that, Connor? You're ticklish, are you? The big, badass superhero's secret weakness is being tickled, eh?"
A devious smirk comes over his face, and he brought his hand to Chloe's sides - before tickling her back.
"HEY!" she screamed. "AAAH! That tickles too! HAHAHA!"

"HAH! Good boy, Sparky!" she shouted, pouncing on Connor again and tickling him back.
"HAHAHAHA! Okay, okay! Mercy! I surrender!" he faux-begged, tears of laughter forming in his eyes.
Chloe laughed, and released him. Sparky trotted over to her, and Chloe held out her fist. Sparky batted it with his paw, and nodded.
Chloe wiped her eyes. "So, how's it feel to lose a fight for the first time?"
Connor grinned. "Paladin *wishes* they could beat me like that."
Chloe laughed, and cuddled up. Sparky jumped up, and curled up on her legs. The three all smiled warmly.

It was early morning.

"But look at the way he's been strung up. No human could do that in such a short space of time. This was superhuman. But Super-Agents don't play with their kills like this." Ivan explained, tilting his head.

Ivan took out his burner phone, and called Connor.

And a lot of my advantage last time was reliant on you not knowing how my powers worked. Once you clocked them, the fight was over pretty quickly. Then again, Bubo might give me an advantage..."
