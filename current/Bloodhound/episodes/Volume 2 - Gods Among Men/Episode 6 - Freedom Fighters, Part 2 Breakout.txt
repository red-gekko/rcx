VOLUME - Gods Among Men
EPISODE - Freedom Fighters, Part 2: Breakout
============================================================

It was your average evening in Belson.
That is, the weather was grim, the mood was grimmer, and a young man was dragged into an alleyway by a mugger.
"You know how this goes! Give me your fucking money!" he snapped.
The young man trembled, and put his hands together. "I-I don't have any money! I swear!"
The mugger laughed, and drew a switchblade. "You sure about that?"
The man closed his eyes. "O-our father, who art in h-heaven... hallowed b-be thy name..."
A punch to the gut brought the man out of his prayer.
"Don't worry, mate. No God's gonna come and save you."
With that, the air trembled. The sound of a deep, low, bestial growl.
"Who's there?!" the thug turned, pointing out his knife. "Show yourself!"
With that, two bright yellow eyes glowed in the shadows. Wolf eyes.
"W--what the fuck..." the mugger whispered, and then--POUNCE.
Sparky's eyes flared red as he knocked the mugger flying, and barked right in his face.
At the sight of the chitin-covered, cowl-wearing bipedal wolf, the mugger soiled himself.
Sparky grimaced at the smell, and released him. As the mugger ran away, he turned to face the young man.
"J-Jesus?" he asked. "Is that you?"
Sparky tilted his head. "Who's that?"
The young man bowed his head, and ran away wordlessly.
Sparky sat there for a moment, letting the wind blow his fur. He sighed, and scaled a wall, watching the city from a rooftop.
His city. His home.

"Woo!" Bea cheered, bringing over a tray of shots.
Amy grinned halfheartedly, as did Chloe. They took their drinks, and downed them.
"Hey... thanks for this, Bea." Amy began. "I know things are tough, and me and Chloe are going through it..."
"I am, too." she smiled softly. "I miss them too. But I can't do anything to help them yet. I *can* try and help you guys feel a little better, so... that's what I'll do."
Amy blushed slightly, sipping her cola. Then, she felt her phone ring.
"Hang on, it's my boss." she said.
"Ooh, put it on speaker - I wanna hear all the mischief Sparky's been up to." Bea grinned.
"Me too!" Chloe smiled warmly.
Amy chuckled, answering. "Hey, Alison! Everything okay?"
"Amy - I'm so sorry." she began. "Sparky managed to climb the fence. I've never seen anything like it... I've been trying to find him, but I've had no luck."
"Oh, no..." Chloe shuddered.
Amy rubbed her temples.
Not yet... not now...

Sparky took a deep breath through his nose, letting the cold air fill his lungs. Two rose, two sank.
Then something shot into his ears - footsteps. He turned and snarled, only to change into a big puppy-smile.
"Privet, volchonok." Ivan grinned, walking towards him. "I've missed you."
"Ivan! How have you been?! Have you heard from Connor?! Is he okay?!" he asked between frantic licks.
"Haha, hello boy!" Ivan chuckled. "Before you ask - or, if you already did - Connor's in jail at the moment. They don't know he's one of us, which means he can't break out without revealing himself."
"Damn it!" Sparky curse-barked.
"Indeed, bratan. I'm working on a plan to get him out - but until then, I need your help with something." Ivan continued. "I've been keeping an eye on you - good work carrying the fire while he's been gone. This next job - it's Gambit. The Chief Constable suka has deputised them - so we need to go on the offensive. Raiding their armouries should go a long way, ah?"
"Where do we need to go?" Sparky barked affirmatively.
"I'll take that as a yes!" Ivan grinned. "Follow me - I'm sure you won't have trouble keeping up."
Sparky nodded, and the two sprinted away.

Meanwhile, in Redbridge, the girls were wandering the streets.
"Sparky!" they called out. "Where are you?!"
"Keep that noise down!" a grumpy old woman shouted out from her bedroom window.
"Fuck off!" Amy snapped back.
Chloe turned to face them all, looking forlorn. "Where the hell could he have gone?"
"He must have tried to find Connor." Amy shuddered. "Damn it, I should've known he could jump that fence..."
"He's gotta be around here somewhere." Bea pondered. "Maybe there's somewhere important to him?"
"Maybe he went back to your old place?" Chloe asked. "Maybe he thinks Connor's moved back in?"
"I doubt it." Amy shrugged. "I, uh... he'll probably come back to the garage, eventually. You guys go home, I'll wait for him there."
"You sure?" asked Bea.
"I'm sure."

Sparky and Ivan arrived outside a run-down warehouse in Belson, now converted into an arms depot for Gambit.
Sparky sniffed the air. "Super-Agents. Lots of them."
Ivan likewise peered through his binoculars, and grimaced. "Got your work cut out for you, volchonok."
He rose, rolling his shoulders.
"Okay, here's the plan. You go in loud - maul those bastards. I'll come in through the roof, and cover you with Isabella here." he grinned, motioning at his rifle. "She and I are going to have a nice romantic evening."
Sparky tilted his head. "Are... are you going to mate with that gun?"
Ivan smirked. "Bark twice if you like the plan."
Sparky barked twice, and Ivan nodded. He scaled the roof while Sparky barged in.
Immediately, a squadron of Super-Agents opened fire. Sparky was far, far too quick for them. He darted from wall to wall, diving in and biting with enough force to crush their torsos in his jaws.
*BANG!*
Ivan grinned as his bullet hit its mark. An Agent's head made a distinct *pop* sound, as the bullet sailed through.
Sparky leapt up, grabbed one Agent's head in his mouth, and twisted in mid-air, ripping off the head and sending the body flying into another Agent.
Then, a horrific screaming noise. An 8-foot-tall monster stomped out from a containment cell - another Jotunn.
"Nevozmozhno... another one?!" Ivan muttered. "Kill him, Spartacus!"

Amy took a deep breath, standing before the Riverside garage-motel. She opened the door, and headed down to the basement.
"All units - Werewolf confirmed active in Haliford Court, assaulting a Gambit depot." the police scanner began.
"There you are..." she sighed.
"All units, this is Oscar Bravo Five-Four. We've got a group of Skinners in Drury Lane, harassing people. Requesting officers, ASAP."
"Belay that order. Priority One is locating and eliminating the Werewolf. All units, converge on Haliford Court."
She scowled. A gang of skinheads were on a rampage, and these jackbooted thugs... the city needed its hero.
Then something else in the basement caught her eye.
Her gaze ran over and across the Crusader suit; its blackened cerametallic carapace catching the light, while its red LEDs flickered softly.
Next to it was Connor's sleek, black motorbike - useless to him ever since he discovered wall-crawling, let alone grapple-swinging and gliding.
She walked over to it - hesitant, unsure.
The city needed a hero.

Sparky lunged forwards, clamping his jaw on the Jotunn's arm. It responded by beating him against the ground, cracking the concrete floor.
Shockwaves tore through his bones, but he did his best to hold on. It wasn't enough.
The Jotunn spun around, backhanding him with enough force to launch him across the warehouse.
*BANG!*
Isabella roared as her bullet cut into the Jotunn's chest - to no effect.
"Yob tvoyu mat..." Ivan muttered, taking out the magazine and grabbing another.
Sparky didn't wait. He dashed from the wall he'd landed in, crashing into the Jotunn. His teeth sliced into the freak's abdomen, ripping out chunks of hot, red meat. His claws swept and raked across the monster's legs, and he dug his heels in, morphing up the chitinous armour to encase him.
The Jotunn wasn't gonna go down without a fight. It bellyflopped down, knocking the wind out of the wolf's chest. Then, it ripped a piece of rebar from the floor, and brought it down like a sledgehammer.
He howled in agony - and fury.
The Jotunn raised its boot, ready to begin stomping. But before it could manage...
*BANG--BOOM!*
Another bullet, this one an explosive, tore into the Jotunn's torso and detonated inside its ribcage. It spat out blood, reached out with its arm, and collapsed.
Sparky panted and huffed, exhausted. Ivan dropped down to the ground.
"Khorosho, volchonok. Very, very good." he praised.
Sparky just smiled, unable to speak. Finally, he caught his breath.

On the third swing, the Jotunn's arm came off. Sparky spat it onto the ground as the Jotunn clutched his arm-stump in agony.
"I need to go back to my friends. They'll be wondering where I was." he said.
Ivan looked at him. "Do you need to leave?"
Sparky barked affirmatively.
Ivan grinned. "Carry on then, tovarishch. And..." he paused. "We'll get Connor back soon. I promise."
Sparky nodded, nuzzled Ivan's hand, and tore off into the night.
"Tsentral'nyy, eto Verdikt." Ivan began into his earpiece. "Vragi unichtozheny."
He cracked open a nearby crate, and saw a number of syringes - this time though, not serum. Instead, they were all labelled "XJK5."
He stroked his beard, taking a photo.

"Please, take anything you want - just don't hurt us!" the terrified young man begged, shielding his friends.
"Aww! Look here, boys! Little man here fancies himself a martyr."
"Happy to oblige." another Skinner grinned, brandishing a club.
"Okay, fine - just let them go!" he pleaded.
"No! Javier, don't!" a girl behind him cried out.
"Her, next." the savage skinhead laughed.
At that, they were covered by a deep red light, and their ears were filled with a revving noise.
"HEY! Why don't you pick on someone your own size?!"
They turned their heads around, and cocked their eyebrows at the sight.
Amy stood firm in the powered exoskeleton. She stretched her arms out, as her heart beat fast. The motorbike purred next to her, illuminating the scene with its red headlights.
The thugs laughed.
"And you're meant to be?"
"He ain't the Werewolf, that's for sure."
"*He's* a *she.*" she corrected, readying her fists.
"Well, *she's* gonna get a beating." their leader snarled, brandishing his club.
He charged forward. Amy shifted to dodge - the suit moving faster than she expected. The club sailed through the air, slamming to the ground.
A roar punctuated the second swing. She went to block - too late. It smashed into her helmet, and she reflexively closed her eyes in anticipation.
The metal hummed slightly, undamaged. The club, meanwhile, had snapped in two.
She reached up, and gripped her helm with her gauntlets, as if checking her head was still attached.
Yep, it was. A grin of excitement cracked under her faceplate, as she sent a punch to her attacker's gut.
"GAH-HH-HH-HH-HH-HH--!" he jerked, as electricity coursed from her gauntlet into his flesh. He fell down, twitching and with now-wet trousers.
She gasped, pulling her hand back. Then she clenched her fists, and watched as brilliant light crackled and danced over them.
Shock gauntlets. Damn, this was gonna be fun.
"GET HER!" the others roared, charging in.
She threw up her stance, and tackled the first. As she squeezed him, he screamed and shook, before a throw to the floor took him out of commission.
A second wisely kept his distance. He had a metal bat, and was light on his feet. He quickly turned the circle, trying to outflank her.
She took a few more strikes. The kinetic force reverberated through her suit - she could almost feel it running harmlessly over her skin.
This was a new sensation. She was a good fighter - a very good fighter. But she was also human. Her training, her instincts - they taught her a fight with such a group was suicide.
The cerametallic carapace disagreed. She grinned, and turned. The bat swung out, and she stopped fearing it. A quick step forward as it clanged against her chest, and a flying fist sent the Skinner tumbling down.
Then her ears pricked up, and her blood ran cold.
"LOOK OUT!" Javier screamed in terror.
*Click! BANG!*
The bullet sailed through the air. She'd never been shot before, naturally - so she froze in fear, throwing up her arms as if she could block it. Instead, it slammed right into her forehead.
*Clang!*
It felt like hours, before she allowed herself to reopen her eyes. It was milliseconds. She saw the bullet bury into a wall, having harmlessly ricocheted off.
"What... what the fuck are you?!" the gunman asked in horror.
He was answered by a gauntlet strike, and the world went bright, then dark.
"I'm... working on a name." she grinned. She relaxed her fists, and watched as the electric hum died down. She walked over, offering her hand to the scared citizens.
They hesitated, but took it. To her relief, they didn't immediately convulse, but instead rose and thanked her.
Smiling warmly, she headed off into the night.

It turned late, now - very late. Amy rolled her way through the city, her eyes heavy and muscles aching as she tried to keep a low profile.
"Fuck..." she huffed. "Wish I could shapeshift like him..."
She saw something - amber eyes looking at her. They were perched on a street light.
An owl? Didn't think you'd find those in the city, but--
Before she could even finish the thought, she heard a cracking noise in the air. She turned to face it, and felt a whip wrap around her neck.
"Urk!" she gasped, grabbing for it. Too late. It folded and knotted itself into a noose, as she was hoisted in the air. The bike fell to its side, as she choked and coughed, dangling from the post. Her hooded attacker held the other end tight, as the owl flew to her shoulder.
"Oh, you bastards are getting brave, aren't you?" Gwen snarled. "Sending a Crusader unit, out in the open?"
She tried to say something - to beg, maybe - but could only spit and gag.
Gwen drew her knife, and used it to pin the rope to the ground.
"I'll leave you here. Let it be a lesson to your masters - just because Connor's not here, doesn't mean you can fuck with our city."
"*C-Connor!*" she pleaded, gripping the rope and doing all she could to get a little more oxygen. She felt the air rush out of her lungs, and struggle to come in, as Gwen's eyes flexed with power.
It was getting dark now. Blurry. Amy kicked the lightpost, hoping to break free.
*BANG!*
The bullet shot through the rope, and Amy slammed to the ground. She choked, spat, and caught her breath.
Gwen turned, her bowstring drawn with a gleaming arrow.
"Dobryy vecher, damy." Ivan grinned, slinging Isabella back around his shoulder.
"Verdict." Gwen nodded curtly. "You in the business of rescuing Crusaders, now?"
He sighed. "Show her."
Amy coughed again, and took off her helmet. She shook her hair free, and rose to her feet.
"Amy Zhao. Connor's sister." he chuckled.
"Oh." Gwen's eyes widened. "I, uh..."
"Amy." Ivan grinned. "I think you'll find that suit is private property."
"Hey, me and Connor share everything." she smirked, now breathing more steadily.
Gwen bowed her head. "I know Connor. He, uh--"
"Oh, you're the psychic dominatrix, right?" Amy gasped. "Uh, I--I mean..."
"*That's* how he described me? Oh, he's in so much trouble..." she snarked.
"He mentioned the owl, too. Can I stroke him?"
"Uh, sure." Gwen cocked an eyebrow. Amy took off her gauntlet, and gently held her hand out. Bubo tilted his head, but relaxed.
"Hey, buddy..." she cooed. "You're beautiful, aren't you?"
Gwen cleared her throat. "Sorry about that, I... I thought you were one of them."
"I'll get the suit customised a little, just for future." Ivan sighed. "As in, Connor's future. You need to put that suit back where you found it."
"But why? I can help out. Don't tell me the city doesn't need it."
"If Connor found out you were doing this, and you got hurt... he'd never forgive himself. Or us." Ivan shook his head.
Amy sighed. "Are you gonna tell him?"
Ivan looked away, stroking his beard.
"Tell you what. Put the suit back, and leave it there. We all go home tonight. Connor doesn't need to know his sister went out on vigilante duty - or that Whiplash nearly killed her."
"Yeah... definitely keep the last part schtum." Gwen nodded grimly.
"Deal." Amy chuckled. She put the helmet back on, and the three parted ways.

The midnight moon illuminated the city, as Amy - now in her civilian clothes - came in through the garage door. Immediately, she was pounced upon by Sparky.
"Amy! Where did you go?! Is everything okay? Why do you smell like an owl?" he asked between frantic licks.
"Hah, hey buddy..." she chuckled. "Please don't disappear like that again. Or at least next time... can you wait until you're not in daycare?"
He nodded, before going back to licking. She giggled, lying down. The two snuggled warmly, and fell asleep splayed out on the sofa.

Morning dared to come. Connor was laid on the top of the bed, as Stoker was timidly lying on his bunk, his mouth still bruised and his teeth still gone.
A knock at the door.
"Barrett." the officer began. "Visitor."
Connor's head perked up. "Who?"
"I don't fuckin' know. Go see them, or don't." the officer dismissively replied.
Connor sighed, and hopped from his bunk to the ground. He left the cell with the guard, and was seated in a white room.

"Privet, tovarishch." Ivan grinned as he walked in.
"Oh, thank god..." Connor breathed out in relief. "How did you manage..."
"MI5 still owe us for Hughes." Ivan grinned, taking a seat.
"I should kill child traffickers more often, then." Connor quipped.
"I think that's what got you into this mess." Ivan smirked. "Dorsey's launched his retaliation, it seems."
"The fuckin' monster." Connor growled.
"You're not wrong there. I managed to do some digging of my own."
"And what did you find?"
"His underling, DCC Branson, has taken direct charge of the investigation into the traffickers. And coincidentally, all of the evidence that could potentially implicate him has vanished. From victim testimony to frozen condoms the traffickers were keeping as blackmail. Normally, such a move would be a blatant giveaway - but the chaos of his crackdown has caused it to go unnoticed."
"So... you're saying he's done all this, to cover up..." Connor shuddered.
"Da. He's an *izvrashchennyy ublyudok,* as we say back home. And more - he's deputised Gambit. He's got them patrolling the streets, turning a blind eye to the Skinners - and to Paladin."
Connor's face creased. "Then he's a dead man."
"Agreed, bratan." Ivan nodded. "But you're not killing anyone locked up in here. And as I'm sure you figured, you can't use your powers to escape."
"And I'm guessing, if we could pull the same trick that MI5 used to get me off the hook for what happened at Vitalis, we would've?"
"You're a good guesser." Ivan sighed. "We can play it that way, but it'll take time. Time the city doesn't have."
Connor sighed, and looked down. "How have things been while I've been gone?"
"You're not the only superhero in town." Ivan chuckled. "Let's just say, there's been a drop in murders by blade - and a corresponding rise in murders by rifle, arrow, and claw."
Connor smirked. "I heard through the grapevine there was another one of those big fuckers. You and Sparky took it down, I'm guessing?"
"He's a very good boy." Ivan grinned. "But we could use you back. Lucky for you, your handsome Russian mentor has escaped a gulag in his time."
Connor chuckled. "You watch too many spy flicks."
Ivan laughed jovially. "It's true! Every KGB operative has to at least once."
"What, as part of your training?"
"Nyet, because we inevitably get accused of treason at least once and have to clear our names." Ivan giggled.
"When I get out of here, I'm gonna tie you to a chair, pin your eyelids open, and make you watch that movie until you hate it to your core." Connor snarked.
Ivan grinned. "No force on heaven or Earth could make me hate Red Rebellion. But to the point... I have a plan for you."
He revealed his prize - a schematic of the prison. Specifically highlighted were the access points to the roof. In his other hand was a spare, fully-charged earpiece.
"How the hell did you smuggle that in here?" Connor tilted his head.
"I didn't stuff it up my *zhopa,* if that's what you're asking." he grinned. "One small problem - this place has a lot more CCTV than I expected. You'll need to be wary."
Connor leant back, and stroked his chin. "Actually... I think I might have an answer to that."

A few hours had passed, and Connor sat in the prison yard, mind elsewhere.
Tommy and Vince came over.

"Fuck. They're in the women's block?" Connor asked.
"No, worse. They've created a 'Special Housing Unit' where anyone and everyone getting caught up in their sweeps is being tossed. 'Seditionists,' that's what they're calling them. Gives Dorsey a blank check."
"What about the mayor? Can't she do anything."
"Call me a cynic, but she's a politician." Vince scowled.

"I imagine the Optimates have used their media machine to pin the attacks on the Hoods, since pinning it on me is no longer viable. And they know I'm in here, so I can't prove it was them - only that it wasn't me. All they need is Dorsey to fabricate evidence, and their puppets in the Home Office can push the right buttons and get things moving."

"How did we get caught up in this, again?"

"So, um... who came to visit? Was it that girl you like, again?"

"I'm gonna find this Dorsey fella. I've got evidence that he's involved with the freaks I'm after. I don't think he knows who I am - I can use that. Once he's taken down, I'll do what I can to get them to let us go."

"You know..." Vince grinned. "I know it's my worst nightmare, having you in jail too... but I'm glad we got to spend this time together."

That night, Connor was pacing in his cell, thinking long and hard.
"Will you sit down or something?!" Stoker snapped. Connor turned and gripped Stoker's nose in his hand, breaking it.

He arrived outside Shannon's cell. He struggled with the lock, but the door opened.

Connor forced him to his feet, where a jab to the solar plexus evacuated the air from his lungs - his in-breath interrupted by a chop to the throat. "Let's go!" Connor barked. The trio sprinted onwards.

Almost there.
Almost.

Too late.

Morning finally came again, and Chloe began the miserable walk to university.

Fuck this city.
