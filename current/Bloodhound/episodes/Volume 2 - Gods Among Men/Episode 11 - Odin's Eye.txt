VOLUME - Gods Among Men
EPISODE - Odin's Eye
============================================================

Grozny, Chechen ASSR. 18th of February, 2009.
Separatist forces led by Islamist warlord Vadim Zamaldayev had captured the city. Recognising their position was untenable, Soviet Loyalists had organised a convoy to escape into Russia proper. But Zamaldayev's forces had spotted them, and mobbed nearby.
The evacuation would have been doomed - were it not for the 9th Motor Rifle Division's timely arrival.
This was going to be the Battle of Grozny.

The Division's commander, Major Iosef Tarkovsky - an older man, with a nervous disposition - was surveying some maps, alongside his aides.
"[It's a mess out there.]" Tarkovsky noted dryly.
"[Yes, sir.]" an aide nodded. "[Our current strategy is to have Makharadze and Lebedev reinforce Ahmadov - the de facto leader of the Chechen Loyalist forces. From there, general Trepov will come in from the north, and close in on the insurgents. Once they are put to rout, we will complete the evacuation.]"
"[Is there any hope of us taking the city?]" Tarkovsky asked wearily.
"[I'm afraid not, sir. Even if Zamaldayev died here, the city is effectively lost to us. Our only hope is to cross back over the mountains, and pray Moscow hasn't fallen when we get there.]"
"[So be it.]" Tarkovsky sighed. "[Begin the operation.]"

An hour had passed. In Lebedev's brigade, the fighting was intense. Separatist forces anticipated the manoeuvrer, and intercepted before Lebedev could reach the convoy.
In the 3rd Company, Svetlana Popova was staff nurse. She hurriedly tried to stop the bleeding of a young, pimply Lance Corporal. His eyes widened in terror, and she desperately closed his wound.
"[You'll be okay, dorogaya.]" she said softly, trying to calm him.
He tried to speak, but blood spat from his mouth.
She nearly finished stitching him, and then--BOOM!
A shell, or a missile, or something else - she wasn't quite sure. The explosion rocked the ground beneath her feet. She knocked into the ground, rolled over, and checked mechanically to make sure her arm was still there - it was, but her shoulder had been dislocated. She looked over at the young man, whose wound was torn open by the blast. He was dead.
She stood there for a moment, unable to think. Her brow furrowed, her pupils constricted, and then...
She marched over, and grabbed the man's rifle. She tore off her medic patch - not that it had stopped them already. She clicked her shoulder back into place, readied the gun, grabbed a drum magazine, and charged.

Meanwhile, just north of the AO, Trepov's forces were caught by separatists, and were stuck in a bloody battle with them, unable to support the convoy.
Trepov ducked as a bomb went off near him. He looked around, and sprinted to his armoured car.
Across the battlefield, Regimental Commissar Ivan Sokolov spotted him running.
"[TREPOV!]" he roared. "[GET BACK HERE, YOU COWARD!]"
"[The commander's leaving!]" one soldier shouted.
"[We're doomed! We have to retreat!]" another cried.
Ivan closed his eyes, tensed, and then opened. He looked through his rifle's iron sights, squinted, and fired a single bullet.
Trepov's car spun out, crashed, and caught fire.
Ivan leapt atop a tank hull, using the vantage point to take out separatist soldiers. He made himself a target, and got two bullets in the gut for his trouble. Undaunted, he kept firing.
"[NOT ONE STEP BACK!]" he barked to his men. "[ONWARDS! FOR SOCIALISM! FOR DEMOCRACY! FOR THE SOVIET UNION!]"
"[FOR THE UNION!]" his men roared back, renewed and wrathful. In moments, the separatists were the ones retreating.
Ivan clambered atop the lead APC, and raised his gun in the air.
"[Soldiers! Brothers and sisters of the USSR!]" he began. His comrades listened in to every word.
"[A few miles from here, thousands of our comrades are being slaughtered by these traitors. We are their last hope of seeing the Red Dawn. NOW WHO'S WITH ME?!]"
"[WE ARE!]" the soldiers barked back.
"[THEN FOLLOW ME!]" he cheered, entering the vehicle.
"[I--Commander!]" the driver began. "[You're bleeding - we need to get you to a medic.]"
"[I'm fine!]" Ivan grinned, patching it up himself. "[Just drive the truck, comrade.]"

Back in Lebedev's regiment, Sveta had 13 insurgent kills to her name. She was wrath incarnate - in a trance of blood.
"[Die! Die, you traitorous bastards!]" she howled. A bullet landed in her leg. She buckled, ducked for cover, and fired back. She heard a satisfying *thump,* and grinned.
Trepov's--well, Ivan's--regiment arrived, providing heavy artillery to scatter the rebels. Ivan ran out of the truck, and joined the fray. He spotted an enemy sniper, and sneaked around, before killing him. He grabbed the rifle, and saw the name 'Isabella' scratched into it.
"[Decent rifle... proper Dragunov, this...]" he mused, grinning. He looked through the scope, and was impressed by the quality. He pulled out his radio.
"[All forces, this is Commissar Sokolov. I have just liberated a sniper rifle, and will be using it. Please don't blow up the nest I'm using - if you can help it.]"
He chuckled, and spotted an enemy commander. He pulled the trigger, and he went down like paper.
He kept doing this - and scores of rebels had their heads pop like grapes.
Down in the carnage, Sveta spotted a rebel - he was a huge man, wearing bloodied kevlar. She shot at him, and he barely flinched.
Unfazed, she kept firing, only for him to charge and launch her in the air. She landed down, and wheezed.
"Poor girl." he said in English - he had an American accent.
She pulled out her Makarov, and fired at his head. It snapped back, but he didn't fall.
A Super-Agent. One of the first, in fact.
"Oh, I was gonna be quick. But now... now I'm gonna make you suffer..." he growled, approaching.
*BOOM!*
The Agent fell to the ground, headless. Through Isabella's scope, Ivan looked in horror.
"[How the fuck did she not kill him...]" he whispered, aghast. "[What kind of animals are these traitors?]"
Finally, the tide of battle turned. The separatist forces turned and ran back to fortified positions in Grozny, while the Soviet troops made it to the mountains. The evacuation had been a success.
Ivan clambered atop a wrecked vehicle - it wasn't clear if it was Soviet or rebel - and addressed his comrades-in-arms.
"[Comrades!]" he shouted. "[Today, we gave these traitors a bloody nose. I know - I *know* we have suffered, as well. We have lost too many. Now is the time to rest, and to heal. Tomorrow, we return to Moscow. And from there, we pay these rebel scum back, with interest! Long live the Soviet Union! On with the Red Dawn!]"
The soldiers cheered and roared for their Commissar-Commander. "[Long live the Soviet Union! On with the Red Dawn!]"

Sveta, despite her wounds, patched up each soldier as best as she could. She came to Ivan.
"[Commissar.]" she began. "[Or, should I say, Commander?]"

"[The pile of traitor cadavers behind you was just a coincidence, then?]" he grinned.

"[Go on, comrade.]" he said encouragingly.
"[I was called up to active duty.]" she began. "[I'm out of practice as a soldier, but I'm still a good nurse. But seeing that young man die... it lit a fire in my heart. It made me think of my own little boy, back home.]"

"[Comrades!]" he shouted. "[Look at the news!]"
Ivan raised an eyebrow, and put the radio to the military news channel.
"[All forces, this is Marshal Rykov. I have been forced to order a retreat from the Moscow district. Rebel forces have captured the city. Premier Kruglov has been killed.]"
All soldiers present turned pale.
"[Sir...]" Sveta whispered. "[My family, they--they live in Moscow. I... I'm sorry, sir, I have to see them.]"
"[You'll need leave of absence from your CO.]" Ivan shook his head.
"[Sir, please... you are the Commissar for the 9th. I am begging you...]"
Ivan sighed. He wanted to tell her she had to stay, that their mission was not yet done, but... there was no way the 9th could hope to recapture Moscow.
He relented. "[Alright, comrade Popova. Just be careful.]"
"[Thank you, comrade Sokolov. Be well.]" she saluted, before continuing to treat the other soldiers, and prepared to leave.
Ivan sighed, and leant back.
Then, a man approached him.
"[Commissar Sokolov?]" he asked.
"[Who's asking?]"
The man took out a badge. "[Vladimir Morozov, KGB.]"
The two went to a separate room. There, General Tarkovsky, along with colonels Lebedev and Makharadze, sat.
"[Comrade Sokolov.]" Tarkovsky began.
"[General.]" Ivan nodded.
"[We were saddened to learn of the heroic death-in-service of colonel Trepov.]" Lebedev noted sourly.
"[Perhaps you could enlighten us about the details of his sacrifice, comrade?]" Makharadze added.
"[Comrades.]" Morozov interrupted. The two colonels flared their eyes, but relented.
Tarkovsky continued. "[With Moscow's fall, returning home may be... dangerous. Comrade Morozov here has informed me that Tbilisi remains loyal. Therefore, we will march to Georgia instead, and seek reinforcements, before retaking the capital.]"
Ivan shook his head. "[General, with respect, the 9th cannot hope to take Moscow, even if the entire Transcaucasian Military District was at our side.]"
"[He's right, Tarkovsky.]" Morozov nodded. "[Get your men to Tbilisi, let them rest, and do what you can here in the Caucasus. As much as I weep for the Motherland... this will be a long, long war.]"
Tarkovsky looked like he wanted to say something, but instead merely saluted, and left. Makharadze and Lebedev rose and left, without saluting. Lebedev made sure to bump his shoulder against Ivan's as he walked past.
"[That was some very good shooting, comrade.]" Morozov began.
"[What can I say? The rifle did the work.]" Ivan shrugged.
Morozov chuckled. "[Listen... with Kruglov's death and Moscow's fall, things are going to get a lot worse before they get better.]"
"[Get to the point, comrade.]" Ivan muttered.
Morozov grinned. "[We can't win a conventional war against our own people - you know this. But what if we waged a more... secretive war?]"
"[Go on?]"
"[Our enemies appear to be backed by some... cabal. We believe they sponsored Kruglov's rise, and now have taken advantage of his idiocy. And they are also behind the creation of this... super-soldier you took down. Our mission... is to find these conspirators, and kill them all.]"
"[You want me to become an assassin?]" Ivan tilted his head.
"[I want you to help me save our country.]" Morozov replied. "[First things first - we will accompany the 9th to Georgia. From there, you will focus on eliminating rebel groups in Abkhazia and Ossetia. Soften them up, and the 9th should be able to roll on into Sukhumi.]"

"[Two reasons. First, you're clearly a better sniper than you let on - that's a rare talent. Second, you're a Commissar - that is, one of the few people we can count on to be absolutely loyal to the Socialist cause. Now, comrade, I ask you - are you in?]"
Ivan hesitated. He shook Morozov's hand.
"[I'm in, comrade.]"
