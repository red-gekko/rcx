"Boss. We've got a problem."
Antonio 'Tony' Casale sighed as he stood from his maple desk, and turned to face the glass panes that let him overlook his warehouse-turned-nightclub. He was a fairly portly man, with balding brown hair and a clean face - which emphasised a prominent grin cut into his left cheek, long since faded to a line.
"And that would be, Marcus?" he asked his imposing subordinate. His voice was that of a thick Cockney - and surprisingly high-pitched for a mob boss.
"Crane ain't happy about us pushing on his side of the river."
Casale smirked. On the left side of his mouth, his canine and its two adjacent teeth sparkled in the light - his golden grin was well known in Drackhill's underworld.
"Oh, don't tell me. He'll send 'Il Lupo Mannaro' after us. You don't seriously believe in that bullshit, do you?"

He looked down at his little slice of Belson, like a king at his castle.
"No one fucks with the Gold Tooth Grins." he said, almost to himself.

"Oi, chav. Move along." he barked.

"The... the fuck?!" the doorman gasped, pale as a ghost.
"I said..." Connor began, "I need to speak with Casale."

The door swung open, as the doorman flew through. Connor stomped in, as another man rushed over. He drew a knife, and slashed out. Connor effortlessly grabbed the knife, twisted, and knocked the man to the ground. Another drew a revolver, aiming it right at Connor's head.
"Stop right there... or... or..." he stammered.
Connor grinned under his mask, and in a heartbeat closed the distance, before snapping the revolver in two, and launching a haymaker at the man's face.
Panic erupted. Partygoers screamed as they tried to find an exit - unfortunately, sketchy downtown warehouses-turned-nightclubs aren't known for their strict adherence to safety regulations. Instead, they merely huddled in the dance floor, like panicked cattle.
Connor stormed up the metal stairs, and kicked open the door. Marcus launched a flurry of punches with his knuckledusters at his chest - rather than blocking, Connor placed his hands at his hips and looked up at the man.
Marcus was a head taller, but in that moment - he was little. A light push, and he launched backwards.
"DIE, YOU FUCKIN' FREAK!" Casale screamed, unloading his shotgun into Connor's chest. He flew back, smacking into the wall, and fell to the ground. He coughed, regaining his breath, and pounced forward.
Casale made an almost froglike noise as Connor raised him by the throat, before slamming him into his glass pane hard enough to shatter it. He held Casale over the edge for a second, letting the moment hang along with him.
"Please--Please, I--" Casale sputtered.
"Message received?" Connor asked.
"L-loud and clear."
Connor smiled, and placed Casale down, before brushing the dust off of him.
"Good. Pleasure doing business with you, Mr. Casale."
Casale looked at him, dumbstruck, before shaking his hand.
"Jesus Christ... you're really something, Lupo." he grinned.
"So I've been told." Connor chuckled, before melting into the night.

Now, in the days leading up to Halloween, 2015 - it seemed this beast, vengeful for the insolence of the cross-worshippers, was flapping his wings with vigour.
Most people in the valley didn't care for such flowery language, of course. To quote one:
"It is *fucking* pissing it down!" Connor spat, taking cover under a bus shelter.

The other, however, swung a sock loaded with a billiard ball into Connor's back. He felt it coming before the man even thought of it - but let it make impact. He twisted with it and faked a gasp of pain.

"No funny business, or we're makin' bacon." a guard jeered.

"Pig Man!" Troll smiled. "He look funny!"
Goblin chuckled, before crawling into a corner of the cell.
"Story time?" Troll asked, getting comfy on the bed.
Goblin fluffed the pillow that served as his own, and stretched his arms.
"Once upon a time... there was a knight in shining armour, called Troll; he travelled far and wide with his best friend, the Goblin Wizard..."
