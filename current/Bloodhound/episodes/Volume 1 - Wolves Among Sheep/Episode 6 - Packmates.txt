"Gentlemen." the Commandant began.
"What have the events in Drackhill cost us?!" one old man roared. His face, like the others present, was partially obscured by the room's shadows.
The Commandant sighed, and rubbed his temples. He looked down the massive oakwood table, and try as he might, he could not shake the image of murdering everyone present. The urge was strong.
"Commandant!" the man at the head of the table barked.
"Apologies, sir." he said. "The loss of Subject Six--"
"Due to YOUR incompetence!" another old rich man screamed.
"Daniel..." the chairman said softly, raising his hand. His fellow cabalite relaxed slightly.
"Proceed, Commandant."
"Thank you, Lord Oxford." the Commandant nodded. "As I was saying, the loss of Subject Six has negatively impacted serum production. However, the amount harvested from Barrett has allowed us to create a meaningful stockpile."
"How are you planning on killing Barrett?" one man asked.
"We have a number of--"
"Answer the question!"
The Commandant snarled animalistically in response, making the man go pale.
"Do that again, George." Oxford said softly.
"S-sir, I--"
"I SAID *DO THAT AGAIN!*" he screamed. His eyes flashed a brilliant violet.
The Commandant quickly cleared his throat. "My apologies, sir. If I may continue?"
Oxford sat back down, satisfied. "Proceed. What are your plans on dealing with Barrett?"
"We have a number of active plans we are developing, sir. Project Crusader is currently underway, and we will test it as soon as possible. We also hope to use what we still have of Project Fenrir to engage in psychological warfare."
"Why don't you just kill him yourself?" another man chimed in.
"That is certainly an option." the Commandant nodded grimly.
"No." Oxford cut in. "It is not. A fight between you two, in the middle of the city... it hardly bears thinking about. You will keep out of Drackhill. Ideally, we can keep him distracted while our other plans go ahead unimpeded."
"Sir, with respect..." the Commandant began. "We're bleeding operatives by doing that--"
Oxford's eyes flashed violet again, and the Commandant began to choke, as though an invisible hand was squeezing his throat.
"The last time I checked, George - you were under orders to leave him and the other children alone. This is *your* fuck-up. So *your* department will pay the price."
The Commandant desperately nodded, and Oxford released the intangible grip.
"Well then, that will be all. Good night, everybody." Oxford said chipperly.
The board members left, as the Commandant caught his breath.

It was like another world.
Connor and Sparky were rushing through fields together. Sparky leapt into the air and caught a tennis ball. Connor laughed, and cheered. Luna, his old, long-dead Staffy, was rushing around with them, as though she was a puppy again. Vince, Amy, and Trey all stood nearby, cheering. A number of young wolves revealed themselves from the long grass, playing with each other. Sparky's adoptive mother - a brown mutt - plodded over, and licked him warmly.
Then, a horrid crashing noise pierced their minds, which rapidly turned into a gentle fumbling. Connor opened his eyes, and through his super-vision saw Amy struggling with the lock, still drunk. It was the early hours of the morning.
Sparky shot up, confused.
"Who's that?" he asked, trembling.
"Oh, sorry. That's Amy, she's... part of our pack."
"Oh, okay! Can I say hi?"
"...let me do the talking first, buddy." Connor smiled. "And... make sure you don't use your powers, okay? She doesn't know about... any of that stuff. Not yet."
He heard further fumbling, and grinned.
"That is, whenever she manages to open that door."
"I had a good dream." Sparky smiled warmly.
"Hah, me too! Me and you were rushing through some fields together. My old dog Luna was there--"
"--and so were some other humans, too! And my mum!" Sparky finished.
Connor pulled his head back. "You... did we have the exact same dream?"
"Is that not normal?" Sparky asked.
"Not to my knowledge. Though I must say, Luna couldn't tell me." Connor grinned, before pausing. "When I stroked your head in the labs, I saw some flashes... that must be why I can understand you, too. Some kind of... I dunno, telepathy? That's why we shared a dream - we were touching in our sleep. That's why your siblings were there - they came from your memories, while Luna and the others came from mine."
"Me and 217 had the same thing." Sparky nodded. "Whenever we were curled up together, we would have the same dream."
Connor stroked his chin, deep in thought. His pondering was interrupted by an excitable yip.
"I've never been to a field like that before! I guess it must have come from your memories."
Connor smiled. "Yeah, I recognised the field. It's not far from here."
Finally, Amy managed to get the door open.
"Ugh... fuckin... fuckin door..." she slurred.
"Hey, sis." he chuckled. She turned, and spotted him - and immediately ignored him.
"Oh my GOD! Who's this fella?!" she squeed, rushing over.
"Hi!" he barked. "I'm Sparky! You're part of my pack, right?"
"Hey, buddy!" she cooed. "What are you doing here?"
"I, uh... I found him earlier tonight." Connor cut in awkwardly. "While I was out. The, uh... the Skinners, they were abusing him."
"Skinners?" Sparky tilted his head.
"Poor baby!" Amy said, hugging him. "He's so well behaved, though! Aww... what's his name?"
Connor smiled. "I've named him Sparky. Uh... short for Spartacus."
"Fuckin' nerd." Amy grinned. She looked down, and beamed. Hi, Sparky! I'm Amy."
"Hi!" he yipped back.
"Aww... chatty pup, isn't he? Anyway, what are you gonna do with him?"
"I, uh..." Connor paused, trying to figure out the words. "I'm gonna keep him here."
She looked up, and cocked an eyebrow. "Here? In the flat?"
"Yeah. Well, I... he's not got anywhere else to go."
Amy sighed, and looked down at him.
"Connor... he, um... he's not just a husky - you can tell by the shape of the snout. I think he might be a wolf. Or at least, wolfdog."
"Alright, little miss 'I did zoology in college.'" Connor snarked. She chuckled, and they both crouched down to Sparky's level.
"Amy..." he began. "He's been badly treated. And you said it yourself, he's part wolf. Which means no shelter would take him. Most zoos would be hesitant. Come on, we can't send him away."
She rubbed her eyes, conflicted. "I know, but... he's gonna have... 'needs,' Connor. Doubly so if he's been abused."
"I'll deal with that." he said hurriedly. "I'll take him on walks, I'll feed him, all of it. Besides, you have no idea how obedient he is."
"He's a wolf. He's not gonna listen to you on a walk. He'd probably end up eating a chihuahua." she sighed.
Connor grinned, and looked Sparky in the eye, giving him a knowing wink. "Sparky - roll over, then bark, then lie down, then jump."
Sparky immediately ran through the tricks, to Amy's amazement.
"Now, come to my heel." he nodded.
Sparky walked over obediently, sitting by Connor's side.
"Holy shit..." she gasped.
"See? He's clever. And he listens. And if anything goes wrong, I'll take full responsibility. Please, can we just keep him?"
Sparky's pupils dilated as he bore his gaze into Amy's eyes. She tried to refuse, but fell apart in an instant.
"...Okay, fine." she finally nodded, grinning.
"Thank you, thank you! You're the best sister ever!" Connor beamed, hugging her.
"Yeah, yeah, fuck off with the mushy shit." she laughed, pushing him off. "It'd be nice to have a dog again... even if said 'dog' is a wolf. Just... keep an eye on him."
Connor smiled, and Sparky began licking her in the face. She stroked his mane happily, and nuzzled him.
"So I get to stay?" he asked.
"Yeah, you're gonna stay with us." Connor answered, trying to avoid sounding like he was *actually* talking to him. Oblivious, Amy smiled warmly.
"I'm only agreeing because I'm drunk." she laughed. "Anyway, I'm gonna go to bed. I've got work in... fuck. Four hours."
Connor chuckled. "See you later."
"Oh, and it's my turn to cuddle him." she grinned.
Sparky shrugged. "Don't humans all sleep together?"
Connor covertly shook his head. Sparky smiled, and trotted into her room with her.

Morning came, and Connor awoke to a knocking on his door. He stretched, feeling his joints click, before he opened it.
"Morning, dipshit." Amy grinned.
"'Good morning, dearest brother. I hope you slept well.'" Connor snarked. "That's how it's supposed to go."
"Choke on a dick." she laughed. "I'm going off to work now. You're not busy today?"
"Nah, I'm free until the evening." he shrugged. "I'll take Sparky for a walk."
"Nice one." she smiled. "He's so warm... I call dibs on him at night."
"We take turns." Connor said, faux-firmly. She laughed.
"He's still sleeping on my bed, the little lazy-bones." she shrugged. "Anyway, no time for chit-chat. What time you getting back tonight?"
"Not too late, I imagine." he smiled. "Still, don't wait up."
She nodded, somewhat sadly. "I'm on a split shift tonight, anyway. Raj's in the morning, tattoos in the evening."
"God, the tattoo parlour being open at night is downright immoral." Connor grinned.
"I'm just glad I'm not working Halloween..." she smirked. "The amount of drunkards who'll be coming in, asking for skulls..."
Connor laughed, heading to the kitchen, as Amy headed out. He grabbed a drink from the fridge, sat down, and checked his phone. He saw a text from Chloe.

"Hey! Hope you had a good weekend! Sorry I didn't message sooner, uni's been hectic, aha. How are you?"
He smiled, and replied "Heya, i'm good!! That's okay, work's been busy for me too haha. Do you wanna meet up today if you're free? If not that's cool :) Quick question - how are you with dogs?"
Chloe replied quickly - betraying how keen she was. "Omg, I love dogs! Where is this going?"
Connor chuckled and replied "I may or may not have got a dog recently - wanna go for a dog walk?"
Before Connor could even walk from the sofa to the bathroom, she replied with "Oh my god, yes please!"
...followed up by "Where did you want to go?"
He could almost hear Chloe clearing her throat, and almost see her blushing there. He chuckled, and replied "Meet us in Wyrmrock Park at 11 AM?"
"Sure! Where's best to meet there? Sorry, never been!" she texted back.
Connor smiled. "If you get the bus from Caldermouth, it'll drop you off right by the lake centre - we'll meet you there :)"
He went into Amy's room, and woke Sparky up. He was greeted by several licks.
"Morning, Connor!" he smiled. "Where did Amy go?"
"Haha, morning buddy." he beamed. "She's gone to work."
"What's 'work'?"
"Something humans do - it's very boring, but it's how we get money."
"What's 'money'?"
Connor smiled. "It's how we get food."
"Oh!" Sparky barked excitedly. "We'd better go to 'work' then! I want food!"
Connor laughed. "I have a feeling Ivan will have plenty of work for us soon. Anyway - remember the park you saw in my--our--dreams?"
He nodded.
"Shall we go there? A friend of mine wants to meet us there. Plus, you can smell it in real life, too!"
"Oooh, yes yes yes! I want to chase squirrels, and bark at ducks, and swim, and play fetch, and--"
"Haha, good to hear! Before we go, you're going to have to wear a collar and lead - are you okay with that?"
"Oh... I don't know... they used to put things on me that hurt me when I was bad..." Sparky said, his ears and tail flopping down.
"Oh no, no shock collars! Just a regular collar so no one thinks you're a wild wolf - I promise, I'll let you off lead as soon as we're at the park!"
"Oooh, okay good! I'm ready!" Sparky said, his cheerful demeanour returning.
"Just one thing - and this is very important. Luna's recall was awful - if I let you off lead, you have to come back to me when I call you, okay? I promise I'll only call you back if it's for the best."
"Okay, that's okay!" Sparky replied, clearly happy to be given any freedom at all.
"And remember." Connor began. "I know you're... well, you're probably used to other people being bad to you. But don't worry - there is no one bad at this place, and if anyone bad arrives, I will tell you."
"Got it." Sparky smiled. "Don't maul random people."
Connor laughed. "Exactly."

The pair headed to the park, and Connor saw Chloe sat on a bench. She was wearing a padded jacket and thermal leggings.
He self-consciously zipped up his red hoodie, realising that he simply didn't feel the cold as much anymore. He wondered if that was another mutation of his - but banished the thought as he tugged at his red hood.
"Oh my god, hi!" Chloe said, running over to them. She gave Connor a hug, and he awkwardly put his arm around her.
"Oh, sorry, are you not--" she began.
"It's okay." he smiled. "I just never know where to put my arms."
She seemed relieved, then looked at Sparky.
"And who's this handsome man?! Hello!" she said, and Sparky smiled.
"Hi! I'm Sparky! Do you want to be my friend?"
"Aww, he's a vocal one, eh?" she smiled, clearly unfazed by his barking.
"Haha, this is Sparky!" he smiled. "Sparky, this is Chloe."
Chloe knelt down, and stroked Sparky's ears. "Hi, Sparky!"
Sparky sniffed her face, and licked her. "Hello! You smell nice!"
"Haha, hi!" she replied, giggling.
The trio headed on for their walk.
"By the way, I've been meaning to ask - how's uni going?" Connor inquired.
"Really well!" Chloe smiled. "I'm settling in nicely. But... well, I'm getting a little impostor syndrome."
"How so?"
"Well, everyone keeps telling me I'm doing really well, and I just... I don't feel like I'm good enough. So, I'm worried that eventually they're gonna find out that I'm not good enough, and, well..." she trailed off, feeling shy.
"Hey." Connor replied, his voice caring but firm. "You're definitely good at what you do. You managed to break down the most complicated stuff I've ever heard, and explain it in terms that I, an eejit, could grasp."
Chloe smiled shyly. "You mean that?"
"I rave about lipids all the time now." Connor grinned.
Chloe chuckled. "Well, thanks for the vote of confidence."
"I'm serious, Chloe. You know your stuff."
"Thank you... that means a lot." she smiled.
"So, what kind of stuff do you get up to?" he asked.
"Oh, this and that. Mainly, I've been working on medical studies. We're looking at these things called Hox genes. They're..." she trailed off. "Ah, I'm rambling."
"I'd like to hear it?" Connor asked, smiling comfortingly.
Chloe relaxed, smiling. "Well, there are these genes called Homeobox genes, or Hox genes for short. They tell the body where organs and limbs should grow. They're really old, and haven't changed much over the years - you put the Hox gene for 'put leg here' from a human into the genes of a fruit fly, and it'll work just fine."
"Whoa..." Connor replied, fascinated.
"Yeah! And they're dormant in adult humans, but active in some animals like salamanders and axolotls. We believe they're used to allow these creatures to regrow their limbs."
Connor's eyes widened. "So, if you reactivate them, you can regrow limbs?"
"Kind of!" she smiled. "It's not as simple as just 'switching on' a gene, though. The biological pathways are incredibly complex. There are mechanisms that would need to be unlocked at the cellular level - growth factors, stem cell activation, and even reprogramming cells to take on functions they don't typically perform in adulthood. But... we're starting to make headway in understanding how we might trigger those pathways."
"Sounds amazing. I mean... regrowing limbs..." Connor pondered. He wondered if he could do that, but banished the thought.
"I know! It's really cool. I, um... I made a bit of a breakthrough." she admitted.
"Do tell?" Connor asked, intrigued.
"Well, I... I found a way to reactivate them in ants. It's only a small step, and it's really hard to implement long-term - the poor things started growing more limbs than the usual 6. But it's still a step forward!" she explained, beaming.
"Damn! I mean, switching it on at all sounds impressive. And, you said the Hox genes are old - so what switches them on in ants probably works for us too, right?"
"Exactly!" Chloe nodded. "The tricky part is figuring out how to control it. The genes need to switch off again once the limb's grown, or we'll end up with, I don't know, extra fingers or something."
"Yeah, that'd be... a problem." Connor laughed, but there was a slight edge to his voice. Note to self - try not to lose any limbs.
"Right? And then there's the bigger question of... well, what if we reactivate them in humans? We don't know what else they could do - maybe it's just limbs, maybe it's organs. The body does switch them off once we reach adulthood, so there's got to be a way to just get it to do that again, without it getting out of hand. But... it's tricky."
As they were walking, Sparky spotted a squirrel, and began to chase it.
"Hey! Sparky! Don't do that!" Connor shouted, chasing after him.
"It's food!" Sparky shouted back.
"No! No food!" Connor replied, panicked and embarrassed.
Sparky paused, and turned to face Connor, head tilted. The squirrel used the opportunity to rush up the tree.
"No food. Not for eating." Connor shook his head, trying to avoid looking like he was *actually* communicating with his dog. Chloe giggled, bemused.
Sparky turned back, looking disappointed, but accepted and headed to the pair.
"Good boy!" Connor praised. With that, Sparky regained his cheerful demeanour.
"Oh, okay! I thought I was in trouble!"
"Oh no, you're not in trouble!" Connor beamed. "Good boy for coming back there."
"You're really good with him, Connor. I'm impressed." Chloe smiled. She paused, watching them, a soft warmth in her eyes.
"I have a dog at home - Molly. She's not as big as Sparky, but I remember how protective she was over me. She used to bark at every delivery guy. You'd think they were coming to rob us or something."
She smiled at the memory, a soft chuckle escaping her. "I guess I always liked that about dogs. They have this loyalty that's kind of... magical."
"Hah, too right. My old staffy, Luna - she was a real one. One time, I remember she-"
As he said this, a dachshund came running over to Sparky, barking and snarling. Connor ran over.
"Sparky, it's okay. Hey, can you get your dog please?" he said to the owner.
"It's fine, he's only little!" the owner replied, carelessly.
Sparky let out a ferocious roar, causing the dachshund to run crying to the owner.
"Get that thing on a muzzle!" the owner screamed, running away.
Sparky huffed, and ran off.
"Sparky, come back!" Connor called out.
Sparky didn't listen. He rushed away into the woods, his eyes watering.
