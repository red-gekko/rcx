VOLUME - Age of Heroes
EPISODE - The Last Judgement
============================================================

The Kestrel finished its rip through the skies, touching down atop the KGB field labs of Karelia. Its wings twisted, spinning the craft in a circle, before its landing legs popped out, and the engines powered down.
The gang stepped down, out of the passenger section. There, they were greeted by Morozov - flanked by two Jotunns.
Connor immediately assumed a fighting stance.
"Connor, wait!" Ivan barked. "They're friendlies."
He hesitated, then looked the Jotunns in the eyes.
"Who were you? What happened to you?"
"They chose this, Mr. Barrett." Morozov replied. "Critically wounded in defence of the Motherland. And reborn, to serve once more."
"You." Connor growled. "You're the bastard who kept the Judge alive."
"Connor." Chloe cut in, putting her hand on his shoulder. "Let's save this for later, yeah?"
Connor hesitated, but nodded. The snowfall began to pick up.
He turned back to the helipad. The Kestrel's pilot hopped out, and he and Bevan began arranging its refuelling.
Connor nodded, and the group headed inside, as the snowfall began to pick up.

Chloe made herself comfortable in the laboratories, going over the research the KGB had conducted on the Judge.
Connor, Trey, and Sparky sat down outside, letting the cold air fill their lungs. Sparky laid down, his head hurting from the travel. Ivan approached.
"Hell of a craft, isn't she?" he began. "Drackhill to Karelia in just over an hour."
"Flying in that thing was torture." Trey sighed. "Super-senses don't mesh well with going faster than sound. Not to mention the pigeon-sense."
"Pigeon... sense?" Ivan tilted his head.
"Yeah, you know? We can sense electromagnetic fields - and part of that means sensing our own location." Trey shrugged. He looked over at Connor.
"You're awfully quiet. It's normally you who explains this shit."
"I'm watching my words around the KGB." he muttered.
"Tovarishch, I swear..." Ivan began, but Connor cut in.
"Don't call me that. Comrades don't lie to each other like this. Comrades don't keep monsters like the Judge alive."
"I'm sorry. It wasn't my call." Ivan begged. "I told them this was a bad idea."
"I went after Murillo because he was using serum. I threatened the FBI because they *thought* about using it. Do I need to start thinking about the KGB?"
"We're fighting a war, Connor. We need all the power we can--"
"I trusted you, Ivan. I trusted you, and you let me down!"
Ivan looked away, ashamed.
Connor sighed. "What is this place, exactly?"
"Officially, it's a decommissioning site for chemical weapons seized from rebel forces." Ivan began. "Unofficially, it's where we experiment on superhumans."
"It reminds me of where I grew up." Sparky noted. "Are there any animals here?"
Ivan shook his head.
"No, volchonok. I promise, we're not doing anything like what happened to you."
"You promised the Judge was dead, too." Sparky replied, his top lip curling.
Bevan walked over. "I hope the KGB has learned their lesson."
"Yes, sir." Ivan nodded. "You can tell your people this won't happen again."
"Good." Bevan sighed. "Anyway... Chloe has just finished catching up on your research notes. Thank God she's fluent in Russian."
"Is that why you lot never sent them over?" Connor hissed. "So you could keep this all to yourselves?"
"One thing at a time, Mr. Barrett." Bevan shook his head. "Briefing room, now."

Assembling in the briefing room, the Werewolves, Ivan, Bevan, and a squad of Jotunns took their positions, while Chloe and Morozov stood in front of them.
"Okay, so, I've finished skimming the reports on the Judge." she began. "I confirmed they're of a similar type of mutant to the Werewolves. They have 15 additional, non-paired chromosomes, which match perfectly onto yours."
"So why'd he look so different?" asked Ivan.
"That's the thing... their paired chromosomes." she shuddered. "17 pairs. Humans have 23, wolves have 39, rats have 21. The 17 have no analogue to any known species on Earth. We've only been calling them 'he' out of convenience - I have no idea based on the genetics whether they're male or female. The autopsies done by the KGB didn't look into gametes or genitalia, but... that's not important, anyway."
"So... the Judge is an alien?" Trey gasped.
"I... it's looking that way, yeah." she hesitantly nodded. "Presumably, whatever was done to you three in-utero was done to them, too. I couldn't establish any common markers between the fifteen unpaired and their 17 pairs - implying the 'Werewolf' DNA is as foreign to their species as it is to humans."
"Great. *Two* different alien species then." Trey groaned.
"He--they--definitely spoke like they were from another planet." Connor noted. "Kept calling me 'heretic' and 'youngblood' - mentioned 'the Lord.' Must be their religion."
"Confirms our suspicions - our mutations aren't from Earth." Trey sighed. "One existential crisis at a time."
"Boo hoo, you're not human. Humans aren't that great." Sparky quipped. The boys chuckled, allowing the moment.
"Anatomy? Physiology?" asked Bevan.
"Similar enough to the team." she nodded. "I can't tell how much is normal for their species, versus how much is caused by the Werewolf mutations - but the Judge has two hearts, and a pretty standard humanoid body plan. Neural growths in the torso means they can survive decapitation, though without a brain, they'll be incapacitated. Destroying the head should kill them permanently - then it's just a matter of the body."
"How about location?" Trey cocked an eyebrow. "We're fighting in the snow?"
"We have tracked the Judge to this location here." Morozov answered, pointing to a map. "We don't have an exact location, but we know it'll have a vendetta. As it escaped, it made reference to 'a divine reckoning.'"
"So, we head to the area, and wait for them to come, right?" Connor tilted his head.
"Exactly." Morozov nodded, before turning to the Jotunns. "[Egorov, Kinnunen - you will attend. The rest of you will remain on guard here.]"
"The target location is a small valley - there's an outcrop that overlooks it." Ivan began.
"You should stay here." Connor cut in. "I can *hear* the way your back creaks when you walk. You're still messed up from Belson."
Chloe looked at him, giving him a soft smile. Despite the tension, he was still Connor - and he still cared for his comrades.
"Good thing I'll be lying down with Isabella then." Ivan shrugged. "I'll be fine."
"Major Bevan - any chance that craft outside could help?" asked Morozov.
"The Kestrel's a transport craft - her guns are rather lacklustre, I'm afraid." Bevan shrugged. "Besides, she's refuelling - she won't be ready for at least an hour."
Chloe piped up. "I've synthesised these syringes." she said, revealing them. "Red ones are paradrenaline, blue ones are endorphins. Use the red ones to trigger a Rage if you need the boost - but be careful. You overdo it, you'll burn yourself out. Don't let it be a fight of attrition if you're using them."
"As for the endorphins, they worked a treat on the Judge the last time." Connor added, now turning to face the others. "They can cancel out a Rage, for starters. An overdose might even put them into a coma."
"But that involves getting close enough to deliver the syringe - and the Judge'll be wise to that trick by now." Chloe added.
Connor morphed up his costume. "Remember. The Judge is not an easy target. They're a highly experienced, superhumanly-strong killing machine. After reviving, they're most likely even stronger than they were last time, too. That means we can't hesitate, we can't hold back. With five of us in the melee and sniper cover, it's gonna be pure chaos - so make sure you're only hitting the enemy. But above all - don't ever fight like you're not one step away from death. Because you are."
With that, the group stood. Chloe pulled Connor to the side, as the rest filtered out.
"I'm so sorry about this..." Connor began.
"Don't. I chose to come here, and I wanna help." she replied, before hugging him tightly.
"I love you." he whispered.
"I love you too..." she said, tears in her eyes.
They kissed, and rested their heads on each other.
"Come back, okay?"
"I will." he vowed.

The Commandant stepped out of his passenger plane, and took a deep breath through the nose. He grinned, flexing his arms.
"This'll be good..." he growled.

Across the valley, the Judge sat in a small, makeshift den, meditating.
"Retribution." they whispered. "Vengeance."
They carved a sigil into the ground.
"Malai-akhun." they said, like a vow. "Nezh-dakh palantis, virian Horax."
The sigil glowed faintly, before fading.
"Keyah kashal?" they asked, almost begging.
The sigil didn't respond. The Judge sighed, and rose.

The sun was rising now in Karelia, glancing and glistening off of the snowfall. The three Werewolves and their Jotunn attendees had taken positions in the base of the valley, while Ivan had set up a sniper's nest overlooking them.
To begin, Connor let out a low call - a vibration from his throat, similar to their echolocation skills.
Trey joined in, and then Sparky did, too - a haunting alien chorus filling the air.
A figure appeared on the rim of the valley, that then slid itself down the slope into the base. It rose to its feet, and looked up.
"Hello, boys." the Commandant grinned.
"YOU!" Connor screamed, morphing his armour.
"BASTARD!" Sparky roared.
"Who the fuck is that?" asked Trey, morphing up likewise.
"You can call me 'Commandant.'"
"Chyort!" Ivan gasped, before switching Isabella to semi-auto, and firing wildly.
Immediately, the Jotunns rushed in, preparing their haymakers. The Commandant replied by delivering a jab to the gut that disembowelled one, before effortlessly pulling the head off of the other. Ivan's exploding bullets detonated on his skin, leaving... bruises. Nothing more than bruises.
The boys morphed their claws, and dug their heels into the snow.
"What is this guy?!" Trey asked frantically.
"The leader of Paladin." Connor growled. "What are you doing here?"
"That'd be telling." the Commandant laughed, standing up straight. "Listen, Connor. I'm gonna offer this once. Surrender, and come with me. Or I'll kill you and your friends, and then I'll head to that little KGB lab, find that lovely Chloe of yours, and--"
Before he could finish, Connor roared and charged in, landing his strongest hit. It knocked the Commandant back a pace.
"Insolent wretch..." the Commandant snarled, before sending a punch in response.
Connor threw up an armoured guard, while Trey and Sparky launched themselves overhead. Trey kicked downwards, pushing his boot across the Commandant's face. Meanwhile, Sparky locked his jaw down on the Commandant's wrist, while clawing at the chest.
The Commandant's clothes tore, and he was marked and bruised.
Not bloodied. Not cut.
He slammed Sparky onto the ground, while Connor sent a bladed fist into the gut. The blade bent, but finally broke the skin. Nothing past it - just the skin.
Roaring, the Commandant brought a hammer strike down on Connor's head, before a spinning backhand sent Trey flying sideways. He knelt down, catching his breath, while the boys scrambled to their feet.
Connor looked down at his comrades. He looked up at Ivan, who was desperately firing useless volleys. His mind turned to everything he held dear, and he gripped his friends tightly, pressing his mind into theirs.
Three became one. Their eyes all snapped open, glowing blood-red.
And then, they charged again.

"Verdict, confirm - this is the Commandant?" Bevan asked.
"CONFIRM! COMMANDANT PRESENT!" Ivan barked back, his rifle providing a hum of blasts.
"Shit..." Bevan muttered.
"There is something." Morozov began cautiously. "A not-so-nuclear option."
"What do you mean?" Bevan tilted his head.
"I have your word, this will not leave us?"
Bevan sighed. "Show me."
Morozov summoned Chloe, and led both to another wing of the facility. Yellow barrels filled this room.
"Nerve gas. Chlorine. Sedatives. Irritants. Blood toxins. Cyanide. Hallucinogens. Neocurare."
"Chemical weapons?!" Bevan gasped.
"Unconventional weapons for unconventional warfare." Morozov shrugged. "Mostly seized from Chechnya. That's our cover."
"You want to gas them? That'll kill our operatives, too!" Bevan said, disgusted.
Chloe stroked her chin. "Bevan's right. At best, it'll incapacitate them, while the Commandant - still fighting-fit - can either finish them off, or escape."
"But if they manage to wound him?" Morozov pressed.
Chloe pulled at her hair. "I don't know. Such a high concentration, such a varied mixture..."

The snow had started to turn red, as fresh blood splattered across it.
The Commandant had several scratches across his body now. Nothing serious, but he *was* hurt.
Meanwhile, the boys were on the edge. Their mind-merge had allowed them to coordinate their attacks, but the Commandant was simply too strong.
Connor and Trey sprinted ahead, allowing themselves to be punched in order to catch the Commandant's arms. Then, Sparky dived in, sinking his teeth into the Commandant's clavicle.
The Commandant roared in fury, as Sparky pulled out a chunk of meat. He replied by headbutting the wolf hard enough to knock him into the ground, leaving a small crater in the snow.
"Oh, Six." the Commandant chuckled. "What a shame."
Connor turned, delivering a stab to the hamstring. Trey morphed up an air pistol on his wrist, and used the momentum of a punch to increase the impact force - burying the chitinous round in the Commandant's abdomen.
It would've been too much. Even Junior wouldn't've taken that well.
The Commandant simply grabbed both boys, and slammed their heads together with enough force to blow away the snow around them. Then, he jabbed both in their throats. They collapsed, and reached out for each other.
The three Werewolves were nearly beaten, while the Commandant of Paladin stood menacingly.
"Last chance, Connor!" he roared. "Join, or die!"
Connor forced himself to his feet. "Never." he spat.
The Commandant stopped fighting for a second, and growled.
"Why won't you just GIVE IN?!" he screamed.
"BECAUSE I DON'T WANT TO!" Connor barked, charging in. The Commandant grabbed him by the throat, and raised him up.
"Everything that happens after this... it's all *your* fault." he snarled. "Remember that, while you rot in hell."
Connor raised his fist, morphing his own crossbow. He shot the Commandant straight in the eye.
"AAARGH!" the Commandant roared, throwing him to the ground. He ripped the bolt out, and... spotted a blue syringe attached to it.
"You think that'll work, traitor?" the Commandant spat. "You think I can't kill you?"
A bullet from Ivan - one of his last - slammed into the Commandant's head, hitting him on the temple. The explosion snapped his head sideways, and he staggered slightly - like a human might after a light whack to the head. Nothing more than that.
"See..." he grinned. "Still got it."
Connor snarled, his claws growing sharper. His eyes flared once more, and he let something wash over him.
A thing he'd suppressed.
Pain.
Hatred.
Vengeance.
He charged in. His fist made contact with the Commandant's, and both felt their knuckles crack.
"YOU TORTURED ME!" Connor roared, delivering a second blow. "YOU CUT ME!"
"I MADE YOU STRONG, BOY!" the Commandant barked back, throwing Connor into a rock.
Connor cracked his head against the stone, but rose anyway.
"I WAS A *HUMAN!*" he screamed, pouncing into the air and slashing wildly. "I WAS A HUMAN BOY, AND YOU *STOLE THAT FROM ME!* AND THEN YOU *LEFT* ME! BEATEN! BROKEN! *BLEEDING!* YOU TURNED ME INTO A *FUCKING _MONSTER!_*"
The Commandant's flesh tore under Connor's assault, while Sparky and Trey forced themselves to rise. The hate - the pain that Connor felt - flashed through their skulls too.
The Commandant was hurt, sure - but he had more than enough left. He slammed his boot into Connor's head, before picking him up and slamming him down against his knee. The crunching sound of his back filled the air, before he tossed the limp, bleeding berserker towards his friends.
"ABOMINATION!" a voice boomed from the edge of the valley.
The fighters looked up, and saw it.
The Judge.
"Oh, hey buddy." the Commandant grinned. "Seems I've had all the fun, I'm afraid."
"Oh, the fun has only just begun." the Judge smiled, sliding down.
"Connor's mine. You can have his friends." the Commandant snarled.
The Judge approached the pile of broken Werewolves, then turned to the Commandant.
"If you want to kill *them...*" they growled, drawing their blades, "Then you'll have to go through *me.*"

"I always knew I couldn't trust you, alien." the Commandant growled. "You and our boss."
"Likewise, abomination." the Judge snarled back. The two monsters circled each other, like beasts fighting over a kill.
Connor rose, readying his claws.
"Down, youngblood." the Judge barked. "This one's head belongs to me."
"Lucky you, boy." the Commandant hissed. "Take a break while I kill the freak."
The Judge was done playing. They rushed in, their blade clashing against the Commandant's skin.
Every cut on the Commandant's body had been delivered by the boys sending their hardest strikes, their strongest slashes.
This was an opening slice, and red droplets splashed onto the snow.
The Commandant replied by sending a punch into the Judge's gut. A punch so hard, it shook the air and sent them an inch upwards. Undeterred, the Judge spun in a whirlwind motion, tearing flesh from the Commandant's hide. The two fiends were locked in a fight to the bitter end.

"Tsentral'nyy, eto Verdikt." Ivan whispered into the radio.
"Verdict, this is Bevan. What's your sitrep?"
"The Judge has arrived... and it's fighting the Commandant."
A pause.
"How about our team?" Bevan finally asked.

"Derrington is right." Morozov agreed. "Maintain obs, but do not engage. Keep us in the loop on the fight."
"Prinyal." Ivan nodded, observing the battle from his scope.

"DIE, ABOMINATION!" the Judge screamed. Their swords were shattered now - replaced with morphed claws. The Commandant had likewise opted for wrist-blades. Each monster took out chunks of the other.
The Commandant sent a stab-punch to the Judge's chest, puncturing into their upper-right lung. The Judge, unbothered, struck out at the Commandant's neck, aiming for the jugular.
Blood sprayed out from both, as they fell backwards, clutching and closing their wounds.
"I'm gonna kill you, alien." the Commandant spat. "There'll be no room for your kind in my new world."
"I should've realised it sooner." the Judge hissed back. "I should've noticed the lies of the False Lord when he made the likes of *you.*"
The two charged forward, and slashed. The air stood still for a moment, as both tore past the other. After a second that felt like an hour, a disgusting slipping noise emanated from both.
The Commandant's hand fell off. As did the Judge's top half.

"Chloe, I must protest." Bevan pressed, rushing after her.
"No offence, Major, but you'll need two hands for this." she shook her head, zipping up an NBC suit.
"None taken. But we have the Jotunns!" he begged.
"There's no NBC suits for them. They won't fit in the Kestrel. And we need them here, in case things go sour, to hold off the Commandant while you evacuate." she replied clinically, boarding the VTOL.
"Ready to deploy?" the pilot asked, seating in the cockpit.
"Almost." Chloe nodded, strapping herself in. A pair of Jotunns rushed over, carrying barrels which they loaded into the craft.
"Chloe..." Bevan began.
"Major, the man I love is out there. As are two of my best friends. And they could all die if I don't do this." she cut in.
Bevan sighed deeply. "Be careful."

The Commandant howled, gripping his arm-stump. His eyes flashed a bright red, the fury cutting through the xeno-endorphins.
At this, the three Werewolves rose. Catching their breaths, they injected each other with Paradrenaline, and charged to meet the monster.
The force of their impact shook the snow in the valley, echoing out into the forests above.
But it just wasn't enough.
Trey gladly broke his fists against the Commandant's hide, his pain fading as he felt flesh rip around his shattered knuckles. Sparky tore out chunks of meat with such ferocity, his own teeth came loose. Connor drove his claws deep into the Commandant's thigh, hitting an artery and being showered in blood.
The Commandant didn't break.
First, he grabbed Trey, and wrapped his hand around the man's neck. Squeezing and shaking, he felt the windpipe crush, before the neck-bones made a sickening snap. Trey's limp body fell down, his face contorting and his teeth gnashing - trying, in vain, to get back up and kill.
Sparky was the next victim. The Commandant's hand plunged into Sparky's bleeding mouth, and ripped out his tongue. Then, a wristblade shot out, popping out of Sparky's throat. The wolf fell to the ground, hot blood pouring out.
Finally, he turned his attention to Connor. Slamming him to the ground, he drove his boot into his face.

The first stomp was almost merciful. By rocking Connor's brain against his skull walls, he lost a little bit of consciousness.
That made the next hurt a tiny bit less, as his jaw snapped in two.
*HAWK-ptoo-FWOOSH!* he spat weakly, burning a mark into the Commandant's arm-stump.
"ARGH! Little shit!" he roared, raising his boot again.
Then another stomp. And another. And another. Before long, Connor's entire lower face was a puddle of red mush and bone fragments. His glowing red eyes stared up, ever defiant.
The Commandant knelt down, and sighed.
"Such a waste." he spat, before rising his boot up.
Blood shot out from Connor's lower face - as his lungs desperately tried to make the noise "Fuck you."
"What was that?" the Commandant gloated. "I can't hear you."
And then, a noise boomed from the sky. The Kestrel hovered over the reddened valley.
The Commandant looked up, and chuckled. "Oh no, not a VTOL! However shall I cope?"
The doors opened, and Chloe pushed down the barrels. The Commandant punched one before it could hit him, and... he screamed.
A horrific yellowish gas filled his lungs, while clear liquid burned into his skin. More barrels rained down.
Ivan looked on, and grinned. "Umnaya devochka."
He fired Isabella at the barrels, and the exploding bullets ripped them open, spewing out their contents into the valley.
The blood-soaked snowpit became a yellow, misty crater, and the Commandant hacked and spat bile. He stumbled, and fell to his knees.
His eyes flashed red, he roared, and sprinted off with superhuman speed, uprooting trees as he pushed into the forest.
"GO! GO! GO!" Chloe barked, as the Kestrel touched down into the valley. She hopped out, as Connor went deathly still.
"Come on... come on..." she begged, hoisting him into the craft. She ran over, and did the same for Sparky and Trey.
Then, she looked down.
The Judge passed out from the gas and blood loss, but their face was still contorted in hate. The Commandant's severed hand was a pace away.
She hesitated, but hauled their top half into the craft, and dosed them with xeno-endorphins, ensuring they stayed unconscious. She threw the hand in, and pulled the door shut. The Kestrel tore off into the sky, while Ivan rose up, and began the slow walk back to the labs.
