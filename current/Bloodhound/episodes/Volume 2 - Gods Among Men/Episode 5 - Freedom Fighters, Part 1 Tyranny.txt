VOLUME - Gods Among Men
EPISODE - Freedom Fighters, Part 1: Tyranny
============================================================

It was a warm summer's evening in 1995. Vince Barrett sat on a barstool in the White Hawk pub in Redbridge, sipping a pint of Guinness. He was younger - 23 years old - but looked the part of a rough mobster. His hair was cropped short, and a thick red beard hung from his face.
Across the bar, a 19-year-old man, with dyed black hair and clad in punk clothes, sipped his lager.
The bar was noisy, as expected. And then, all fell silent, as a group of men in leathers stormed in.
"Patriot Kings" - the last of the old guard, before they would become the Disciples and the Skinners.
"Everyone out!" the leader barked. Vince rolled his eyes.
Most of the patrons left. The black-haired man stayed, sipping his drink defiantly. One of the men nudged his shoulder.
"We said 'out', gay boy." he snarled.
"Alright, Stoker." the leader beckoned. "Maybe these gentlemen here didn't hear us right."
He walked up to Vince.
"We said OUT!" he barked loudly.
Vince barely flinched. He gripped his pint glass.
The barman hid away.
Vince slammed the glass into the leader's face, covering him with beer and embedding shards of glass in his skin.
"How about I drink where I FUCKIN' WELL PLEASE?!" Vince barked back.
With that, all hell broke loose. The punk immediately slammed his fist into Stoker's gut, winding him. He then delivered a knee to the face, knocking him down.
Vince delivered blow after blow to one man, but his guard was unbreakable. Before Vince had to switch tactics, the punk grabbed the man from behind, leaving him open.
"Now!" he barked.
Vince grinned, and delivered a devastating haymaker, knocking the man out flat. The punk nodded.
One man drew a hammer, and swung it at Vince. He was met with a volley of punches, before Vince disarmed him, looked at the hammer, and grinned viciously.
Soon enough, every King had taken a swing of the hammer to the head.
The punk delivered a high kick to one thug, knocking his front teeth out.
Finally, Vince kneecapped the leader, filling the club with screams.
"Fuckin' shit!" the barman trembled. "Get the fuck out, before the coppers get here!"
"There are no fuckin' coppers out here!" Vince barked back. "There's only these fuckers."
The punk grinned. "Come on, let's bounce."
Vince smiled, and the pair left.
"That was some class swingin' there, lad." Vince began. "You a fighter?"
"I train every so often. I go to Dynamite Fitness." he replied.
"No fuckin' way! I go there every Tuesday and Thursday!" Vince exclaimed.
The punk laughed. "Well, I go every Monday and Wednesday."
Vince chuckled. "About time someone put those bastards in their place."
"Maybe..." the punk paused. "Maybe we could do that more often."
"How d'you mean?" Vince tilted his head.
"Maybe we can take the fight to them. We work well as a team, you saw it. 'Cause I don't know about you, but I'm sick of them lot running the show."
Vince thought, then smiled warmly. He extended his hand. "Vince Barrett."
"Tommy." the punk smiled, shaking his hand. "Tommy Crane."
Vince chuckled. "That a pornstar name?"
"God given." Tommy grinned. "But speaking of... I heard through a friend that the Vixen is looking for bouncers to keep 'em safe from the Kings' extortion. Maybe we start there?"
Vince nodded, cracking a smile. "Viva la revolution."

It was cold now, with icy rain pattering on the ground - as though the heavens had decided the valley wasn't miserable enough. About an hour had passed since the purge of Redbridge, and a big, bulky transport vehicle pulled up outside HMP Northgate, Wickermast. Prison guards stood by, as the back doors of the van opened up. The captured Hoods were shoved out of the vehicle by officers.
The urge to kill humans hadn't been this strong in a while, now. He suppressed it with all his willpower, and rose to his feet. Despite being cuffed, he helped Trey stand.
With that, they looked up at the prison complex. Connor had been here to visit Vince before, but the place never looked so... imposing. Tall wire-fence walls with barbs at the top. A guard tower overlooking the complex from across the car park.
They were separated. Connor was taken to a room, where a prison officer stood.
The officer pat Connor down. While doing so, the violent temptations returned.
It would be so easy.
Connor bit his tongue. Visions of snapping the officer's neck flooded his mind.
The officer finished, and handed Connor a set of prison clothes.
"Get changed." he ordered, before leaving the room.
Connor un-morphed his clothes, and stood naked for a moment, feeling his muscles tense in anger. He sighed, and put on the prison uniform, before exiting. It felt like sandpaper on his skin - he couldn't wait to ditch it and morph a comfier replica.
The officer marched Connor to his cell.
"You'll remain in this cell. You'll be bunking with another inmate. As you know, you're being held under the new Terrorism Act, which means certain liberties will be denied to you. You do not have the right to visitation. You do not have an unconditional right to legal counsel. Your case will be heard by a judge under the Diplock System in no more than 60 days."
Connor's face creased in anger. "I grew up in the fuckin' Bogside, mate. I know what this bullshit entails."
The officer shrugged and raised his palms, as if to say "Fair enough," before opening the cell door, and walking away.
In the cell stood Stoker. He turned, spotted Connor, and grinned viciously.
"Well, well, well..." he chuckled. "If it ain't Vince's brat. Oh, I'm gonna enjoy making you my bitch--"
Before he could finish, Connor delivered a punch to his face at super-speed. Stoker's teeth flew backwards into his throat, and blood poured from his mouth as he choked. Connor grabbed his neck, and stared into his eyes.
"You so much as snore too loudly, I will fucking kill you." he growled in Stoker's ear. Stoker nodded hurriedly, and Connor released his grip, before heading off to the prison yard.

Before long, practically every business in Redbridge started paying them."

The girls all sighed in unison.

"Not yet." Tommy nodded, getting the hint. "He's not going to break us out, that much is sure."
Connor cursed under his breath - it was an order. "Don't reveal yourself over this."

"I can't say it's good circumstances, but... it's good to see you again, mate."
Tommy smiled. "You too, boss."

"Oh, by the way." Trey began. "This is Shannon."
"Hi..." she said meekly. "I, um... I do computer stuff."

"What?" she tilted her head.

"Oh, um..." Trey scratched the back of his head.
"Trey's been moving product for me. Not much, but enough for them to make the case in court." Tommy explained.
"They're throwing you under Diplock for *delivering weed?!* Connor tilted his head incredulously.
"I just... dad's been worrying about the mortgage, so I tried to make a little more money." Trey began.
"It's not that. Why the fuck would they lock up small fry like you?" Pawel asked.
Trey shrugged.
Connor hugged him. "It's gonna be okay. We're... we're gonna get out of here. I swear."
Tears ran down Trey's face. "Are we?"
