VOLUME - Wolves Among Sheep
EPISODE - Reanimation
============================================================

SCENE - Torture - Part 1

	Springtime, 2006. Warm wind washed over the field, and a young boy was riding his little bike. His uncle pushed him along, as a young girl and a small Staffy pup stood to the sidelines.
	"Okay, Connor!" the man cheered. "3, 2, 1, GO!"
	With that, he let go. Connor beamed in happiness.
	"I'm doing it, uncle Vince! I'm doing it!" he cheered. His accent was different - much more Irish. It hadn't been long since he'd moved to England - only 4 years.
	"Go on, Connor!" the girl - Amy - shouted happily. The pup likewise barked in excitement.
	"You're doing great, lad! Keep going!" Vince cheered. He spoke with a native Derry accent - but somewhat muted, as though he was used to toning it down so English people could understand him.
	Connor continued on for a moment, before he wobbled and fell.
	"Shite!" Vince cursed as he ran over. "You okay, my lad?"
	Amy and the puppy also rushed over. "Are you okay, Connor?" she asked.
	Connor rose, his face scuffed, and his nose bloody. Despite the pain, he didn't cry. He just looked on, saddened and frustrated.
	The Staffy ran over and licked his face clean. Connor smiled softly, before frowning again.
	"Buddy..." Vince said, kneeling in front of him.
	"I can't do it." he lamented. Amy gave him a hug.
	Vince smiled softly, and brushed Connor's hair out of his face.
	"Listen, lad... you've got this. You're made of strong stuff, you know? You've survived worse than this."
	Connor looked up, and smiled.
	His consciousness returned. He was bloody and bruised, still. But not from a bike.
	Blood and cerebrospinal fluid leaked from the gunshot wound in his skull.

	"Where the fuck is he?!" Trey demanded.
	"For the last fucking time, I have no idea." Tommy snapped back. He leaned back in his office chair, rubbing his temples.
	"Then find him!" Amy roared. "Because I swear to god, if he's hurt, I'll..."
	"You'll what?!" Tommy barked. "You think I don't wanna find him? You think I don't give a shit?! Connor isn't just an employee. I swore an oath to your uncle. I *want* to find him. And if I knew where he was, I'd tell you."
	Amy looked into his eyes, a fire burning in hers. She relented, and slammed the door as she left.
	Trey sighed, and looked over at Tommy, forlorn.
	"You really don't know where he is, do you?" he asked.
	Tommy's own eyes were wet, though he rubbed them before Trey could see.
	He couldn't speak. Only shake his head.

	Connor's vision was blurry. A man was walking upside down--no, Connor was hanging upside down.
	He closed his eyes, and listened. He was breathing. He was alive.
	The Commandant approached, and smirked.
	"Welcome home."
	He turned to an old man in an orange jumpsuit, with several small cuts on the side of his head.
	"Doctor Zhao. Is everything ready?" he asked.
	"Yes sir, Commandant, sir..." Zhao nodded timidly.
	The Commandant's smirk was cold, as if he'd done this a thousand times.
	"Begin."
	The doctor pulled a lever, and electric shocks coursed through Connor's body. He screamed, cried, thrashed about.
	His body spasmed with pain.
	Before he could even register what was happening, the world slipped away again, leaving only darkness.
	He had a few brief flashes of consciousness - none pleasant. Surgeries. Drills. Saws on his flesh. Injections. Pain. And Zhao.
	The pain felt like fire coursing through his veins.
	Connor had been no stranger to pain. He'd suffered more than most in his 18 years on this world. But this... this was new.
	Ice cold. Lightning hot. Every neuron in his body was alight with the purest agony. He screamed as loud as he could, and felt his throat rip and tear from the strain of his howl.

	He woke up - an unknowable amount of time had passed. Now, he was nailed - literally nailed - to a wall. The Commandant looked at him, shaking his head.
	Connor sobbed. "Pl--please... please..." he begged, blood and bile spitting out of his mouth.
	"Pathetic." the Commandant folded his arms. "You need to accept this new reality, my boy... you're mine."
	The Commandant approached Connor's broken, bleeding body. He launched a punch to the face, and Connor blacked out again. Only flashes jolted throughout his consciousness for a length of time.

SCENE - Torture - Part 2

	Connor screamed, convulsing as electricity burned through his veins. The surge finally ended, and he collapsed down. His torturer spat, and left.
	Connor curled into a ball, sobbing in pain. His vision faded, and he lapsed into something vaguely resembling sleep.
	Some time had passed. Could've been seconds, could've been a lifetime. He awoke to the door swinging open.
	"Pl...please..." Connor spat. "No... no more..."
	"It's okay." the voice said gently, lifting him to his feet. "I'm here to get you out."
	Connor's vision was blurry. His saviour's features were completely indistinguishable. But he nodded anyway, and shambled alongside him.
	The pair made it to a hallway, where Connor threw up blood and bile. The man patted his back, and sat him down.
	"It's alright. You're gonna be okay." he said softly.
	"Who... who are you?"
	"I'm with Interpol." the man answered. "What's your name? Can you remember?"
	"C--Connor. Connor Barrett."
	"Nice to meet you, Connor." he said. Connor couldn't see, but he imagined the man smiling. He pasted his uncle Vince's face over the white blur.
	"It's okay," he thought to himself. "Vince has come to rescue me."
	The mystery man pressed a small bottle to Connor's lips. Water.
	He greedily drank it, and savoured the moment. He washed away the blood and bile in his throat.
	"Come on, let's go." the man said again. Connor chose to pretend it was Vince's voice.
	The pair made their way out of the facility.
	"Where are all the guards?" Connor asked.
	"No idea. Think they all left." the man shrugged.
	He turned, opening the facility's main door.
	Connor stepped outside into the night, and took a deep breath of the cold air. Then, he felt the hairs on his neck tingle.
	*phit-WHACK!*
	*phit-WHACK!*
	His legs gave way, as bullets slammed into the backs of his knees.
	He howled in pain, falling into the mud.
	The man approached, grinning devilishly. He pulled at his hair, revealing a latex mask that had hidden his true face.
	The Commandant.
	"Now do you see?" he asked, using his real voice. "You're never gonna escape. So stop resisting, and just give in."
	Connor looked up, tears of pain streaming down his face. Before he could think, his eyes spotted something in the distance.
	The Wyrmrock peak. The core mountain of the Drackhill valley. Judging by the distance and the smell of peat, he was in Blackmarsh. He spat.
	"Fuck you..." he snarled.
	The Commandant drew a claw hammer, and started smacking Connor's head with it. His consciousness faded once again.

	Tommy pressed his bruised knuckles into a bowl of ice.
	"He's still not talking." Pawel sighed. "Want me to get the battery?"
	"No, I've got this." Tommy growled, walking back into the room.
	There, a beaten, bloodied man sat tied to a chair. One of the men who had jumped Connor outside the Horse.
	"You... you've gotta believe me, man." he begged, spitting blood and teeth.
	"Where's Connor?" Tommy asked grimly.
	"I already told you... I don't know..."
	Tommy picked up a power drill.
	"I learned this from our founder, Vince." he began. "He learned it from some provos back home. They'd normally mete it out on traitors, but... I think it'll work just fine on you."
	"Please! Please!"
	The drill whirred. "TELL ME WHERE HE IS!"
	"I DON'T KNOW!"
	The man's screams filled the room, as his kneecaps became porous.

SCENE - Reanimation

	Once again, Connor felt his consciousness flash back in. He was chained to the wall in a stress position.
	One part of him wanted to give in.
	Another part dreamed of resistance.
	Kill, a third demanded. Kill them all.
	The other two urges receded, like a pair of jackals at a dire wolf's approach.
	Kill. Them. All.
	He looked down at his emaciated body, and closed his eyes.
	A torturer approached, along with the Commandant.
	"He just won't give in." the torturer snarled. "His system's processing the drugs faster than we can cook 'em. As for the conditioning, we're not getting any results... he still clings to his old identity. We've tried memory deletion techniques, but those only seem to stop him from fully forming *new* memories, not erasing old ones."
	"Hah... you've gotta give him some credit, I suppose. Kid's got heart." the Commandant grinned. "I have to go back... the alien is a damn snitch. Give him your worst - I want him broken and reformed when I get back."
	"What about Six?"
	"We've arranged transfer." the Commandant nodded grimly. "His little canine companion won't be pulling any tricks like that again... you have your orders. Do to him as you please."
	"Yes, sir."

	He stepped forward, approaching Connor's body.
	Beaten. Broken. Bleeding.
	The dark urge only strengthened.
	"Still fighting, are we?" he audibly grinned. "You know... this can all end right now. Say the word, and I'll put you out of your misery."
	Only one word came out of his mouth.
	"Why?"
	Why, indeed.
	Why did they take him?
	Why were they doing this?
	And more importantly...
	"Why am I still alive?" he whispered, through shaky breaths.
	Not just alive, either. He was shot, but... everything was still fine. His knees were fine. His *head* was fine.
	Why?

	Give in.
	Resist.
	Kill.

	"Say the word."
	Connor let out a ragged breath.
	"I can't hear you..." the man taunted, leaning in. "Say it."

	Give in to your rage.
	Resist these monsters.
	KILL THEM ALL!

	The unmistakable sound of a helicopter taking off flashed in Connor's ears. He let out another whisper.
	"I'm listening..." the man grinned.
	At that, Connor's head turned. He clamped his mouth around the man's throat, and bit down.
	The man screamed, then gurgled, as he fell back, sans throat. He spasmed for a moment, before going still.
	Connor swallowed the red chunk. He felt his stomach twist, but he forced it to obey.
	The desire for blood was overwhelming. He felt a pounding in his head, and a warmth in his belly.
	He let it spread, radiating out into his muscles. He felt his body ripple, like a demon had reshaped it.
	And then, he yanked his left hand, ripping it out of its manacles. He turned, and peeled the second cuff off of his right.
	He fell to his knees next to the still, warm corpse.
	He looked down at his hands, and saw blood spattered over them.

	GIVE IN!
	RESIST!
	*KILL!*

	He closed his eyes, and reopened them--no he didn't.
	Something else opened them. And they were a glowing blood-red.
	Someone else was driving. Connor was in the back seats, watching in helpless horror.
	Beating. Breaking. Killing.

	"COMMAND! THIS IS BRAVO FOUR!" a uniformed man screamed into his radio. "SUBJECT ONE HAS BREACHED CONTAINMENT!"
	Across the hall, Connor - no, not Connor - was savagely devouring a freshly-slaughtered soldier.
	The base commander looked down at his radio from his desk, and shuddered. He pressed a button, and hightailed out of there.
	"WARNING!" the speakers roared. "CONTAINMENT BREACH! SELF DESTRUCT SEQUENCE INITIALISED!"
	"BASTARD!" Bravo Four screamed. "YOU BASTARD, SIR!"
	Not-Connor pounced on Four, his teeth sinking into the man's neck. A spray of blood coated the walls, and another chomp filled his belly.
	Already, he seemed healthier. His muscles more prominent, his skin more coloured.
	As Bravo Four collapsed to the ground, he wondered if this whole thing had been worth it. If making a monster like this was ever a good idea.
	"And even if it were," he thought, "We should've at least fed him."
	Then, the blood finished draining from his brain, and he entered oblivion.
	Before 'Connor' could finish eating his kill, a horrific crashing noise filled his ears. He was rocked side-to-side by sequential explosions, as flames erupted and the building started to shudder and break.
	And then, he felt the impact of a volley of bullets crash into his abdomen.
	The assailant finished firing, and grinned. Connor's eyes flashed bright, as he stumbled down to his knees.
	"Goodbye, Connor." he sighed, before drawing his pistol.
	Connor, undeterred, gripped the barrel and squeezed. The barrel bent around his fingers. The gunman pulled the trigger, and it exploded in their hands.
	"FUCK!" the man barked, clutching his hand as it bled. The crumbling pillars holding the barn finally gave way, and the whole burning building came crashing down.

	Emergency sirens blared from the roof of the fire engine. As it pulled up outside the blazing inferno formerly known as a dairy barn, its crew donned protective gear.
	"Alright, team - given the intensity of the fire, we can safely assume some high-grade accelerant was in place here. Thankfully, its isolation and the recent bloody rainstorm means we can keep it under control."
	One man raised his hand - the leader gestured affirmatively.
	"Cops been called yet?"
	"They said they'll get here soon enough. I assume this is either arson or a drug den, so they'll wanna do a look-over. Caller was a dog-walker who heard the explosion - according to the plods, this lot is owned by some random holding company, so we're suspecting criminal involvement. Tread carefully, and get this fire out."
	The firemen started hosing down the wreckage. After clearing the perimeter, they moved inwards.
	One, a young recruit, approached a heap of ash, dirt, and debris, hosing it down. Then, he nearly jumped out of his skin, as a horrifically burned hand pushed out of the detritus.
	"Shit!" he gasped. "Sir! Got a body here!"
	The hand pushed out further, revealing the full arm, before pulling the rest of the body out.
	A sight none could forget.
	A horribly burned man. Emaciated, almost skeletal. Hairless, skin charred and bloody, wearing nothing more than blackened tattered rags.
	"He's alive! Holy fuck!" the recruit shrieked.
	The leader rushed over.
	"Hey! It's okay! We're here to help!" he said, his face pale as a ghost.
	The burned man stepped forward, and gripped the leader, raising him up by the neck. He choked and spat, while his men screamed and cowered.
	The urge to kill resurfaced, burning through his brain.
	His eyes flashed red once more, before fading. Connor dropped the fireman, and instinctively sprinted away.
	"What the fuck was that?!" the recruit asked, horrified.
	The leader choked, catching his breath.

SCENE - Headless

	Another eon - or a minute - passed. Flashes once again rushed through Connor's mind.
	Finally, he awoke in a dark alleyway, in the pouring rain. He had just finished eating a live rat with his bare hands.
	He puked up blood, fur, meat, and bile.
	Connor rose to his feet. He stumbled around, trying to find his bearings.
	He didn't know who he was.
	Yes he did.
	No, he didn't.
	...too much. Too much.
	He looked into a puddle.
	He was gaunt, bruised, and bald. The horrific burns had... disappeared. He looked less skeletal, too - God only knows how many *things* he'd eaten.
	But at least one victory - that was definitely *his* face.
	"I'm still me." he sighed in relief.
	The hairs on his neck itched. He looked around, and spotted in the distance - a long way away, and in the pouring rain, but yet, he could see it as if it were up close - it was a man in Skinner leathers. One of Stoker's goons - one of the thugs who had jumped him outside the Horse.
	Belson. He was in the Belson district. Skinner territory. It wasn't far from home - an hour's walk, give-or-take. He could tell his location like he was a homing pigeon.
	He tried to sneak away, but - no. The man confidently sauntered towards him.
	"Oi, oi! The fuck are you doin' in our patch, boy?!" he spat.
	The urges, again. The dark, killing Rage.
	Connor prepared a stance, despite his aches and weakness. The man swung for Connor, and he ducked, then prepared an uppercut to the chin.
	He felt a surge of energy coursing through his limbs, a heat building in his chest, and his fist connected with the man's jaw.
	As he hit, he closed his eyes. He heard a massive, deep, booming sound, as though an explosive force ripped through the air.

	Holy shit.
	What the fuck.
	What the FUCK?!

	Blood.
	Blood everywhere.
	What?
	What?!
	WHAT?!

	The man stumbled backwards, as blood and brain shot out of the top of his head, punctuated by shards of skull. His hands reached towards his newly-folded neck for a split second, before he fell down to the ground. Gory paste rained down behind him.
	For a moment, Connor stood frozen, unable to process what he'd just done. Then, the act hit him like a wave of nausea.
	His stomach turned, and bile rose up his throat as he stared at the lifeless body. His hands trembled, slick with blood, and he felt a cold dread seep into his bones.
	He stumbled into alleyway, vomiting bright yellow.

	"Fuck..." he groaned, feeling pressure mounting under his skin - like something was going to burst out of it.
	His vision blurred and twisted. He looked back into the water, and saw the world go greyscale. And his eyes - they were almost glowing.
	Not almost. Bright enough to cast streaks across the water, like a lens flare. And the veins around his eyes - normally imperceptible - turned ink-black.
	"Gah..." he spat, blood and bile launching from his mouth. He clutched his head, and groaned in agony.
	The pressure was unbearable. It was like something inside him was trying to break free. His pulse quickened as the sensation intensified. He couldn't breathe, couldn't think - he only felt the agony of his own body betraying him.
	"Fuck... FUUUCK!" he screamed.
	For a moment, every nerve in his body seared in agony. But then... a black, tarry gel forced itself out from his skin, encasing him in it. And then it hardened, forming an exoskeletal bodysuit that covered him from head to toe. It hardened, then calcified, becoming a chitinous exoskeleton. Claws cut out from his hands, as he fell down to his knees.
	He looked back into the water, and under the exoskeleton, went even paler than before. His eyes blazed crimson.
	He felt a pounding in his chest.
	No.
	Not *a* pounding. *Two* poundings.
	He felt his chest, and quickly confirmed - two heartbeats. Twin hearts.
	He tried to pant - the way one does in such a panic attack.
	He wasn't panting. To his horror - as one lung filled, the other emptied.
	And so did the other. And so did the other.
	Four lungs. Two windpipes, even.
	As quickly as it had formed, the chitin liquefied once more, and forced itself back down through his pores.
	What was left on his skin quickly shifted and turned, slithering around him like worms on a corpse.
	He looked back into the water, and saw that he was wearing the same outfit from when he...
	When he died.
	Even the motorbike helmet had been perfectly imitated. He felt his stomach heave once more, and he desperately tore at it until it dissolved.
	That wasn't him, no. *He* wasn't *him.*
	Can't be.
	He was dreaming, of course.
	Humans don't have two hearts. Humans don't have four lungs.
	Humans aren't strong enough to kill like that.
	And he was human, right?
	Right?
	Finally, mercifully, he collapsed.

SCENE - Car toss

	Connor grunted as his conscious mind flickered like a faulty light.
	His ears pricked up, and he forced himself to his feet.
	A low, mechanical humming noise whispered above him. His eyes crept upwards, and he saw it.
	A drone - small, with a bright headlight - hovered in the air.
	Its light moved away from the headless corpse across the street, and fell on him.
	He looked into the light, his hearts quickening.
	"THERE HE IS!" a voice roared. The drone's light dimmed, and a squadron of armed men raised their assault rifles. They were kitted out in black uniforms, and dark veins covered their eyesockets.
	"TAKE AIM!"
	"Nonono, w-w--wait, wait wait..." Connor frantically gasped, holding up his hands.
	"FIRE!"
	Connor screamed in agony. The bullets slammed into his hide, and he felt his skin perforate.
	He fell down, blood pouring from his chest.
	He was dead.
	Right?
	Living people don't lie there like that, in a pool of their own blood, do they?
	So why... why in God's name were his twin hearts still beating?
	"GRRAAAAGH!" he roared, his eyes aflame. "WHAT DID YOU PEOPLE DO TO ME?!"
	The squadron ceased fire, and their eye-veins pulsed as they looked at him in horror.
	He looked around quickly, and spotted a wireframe fence further down the alleyway.
	Were he in his right mind, he'd know full well any attempt to scale it would be futile.
	But this had been a very strange evening, after all.
	He turned, and ran. He planted his foot on the wall opposite the fence, and pushed himself off. The wall cracked as he launched like a bullet, firing over it and rolling as he hit the ground.
	"Target's on the move! Go! Go! Go!" one gunman barked.
	"Sh-shit." he spat. He took a breath - through both pairs of lungs - before sprinting as fast as he could.

	Amy fumbled at the flat door's lock, before drunkenly stumbling in.
	She took a deep breath, sitting down at the sofa. Then, another breath, before she pulled out her phone.
	Trembling, she pressed call.
	"Hey, this is Connor. Sorry I missed your call. Leave a... actually, just text me, I never listen to these things." he said, audibly grinning.
	*Beep!*
	"Hey, bro..." she slurred, her eyes watering. "Look, I... please, just come back home, okay? I won't be mad, I promise, I just... I need my brother back. Please..."
	She started sobbing, choking the word again and again.
	Please.

	"Status report! Status report!" a voice barked over the radio.
	The black-veined gunman in the front seat of the car looked out, his lip curling.
	Ahead was Connor, sprinting away. They reached a crossroads, and he turned - catching himself by his hand as he skidded by a corner. The black, armoured SUV made an audible screech as it struggled to follow. Behind them, a similar car roared through.
	"Subject was able to survive the initial strike." he reported. "We are currently in pursuit. Bravo team have arrived to provide backup."
	"Received. What is the subject's vehicle?"
	"It, um... the subject is running on foot, sir."
	The radio went silent for a moment.
	"Alpha team, new orders. Run the target down."
	"Copy that."

	Connor ran as fast as he could, feeling the air roar past him. His hood fluttered in the wind, and he took a moment to dare to look down.
	"Holy fuck..." he whispered through one breath, as another came in.
	He was running about as fast as his motorbike could drive.
	He felt the air beside him distort, and turned his head.
	"Oh SHIT!" he gasped, as the black SUV slammed into him.
	Instinctively, he stomped his foot down hard, cracking into the road. Then, he swung his arm out, crashing it into the bonnet. He felt the metal bend, as the speeding car swept him off his feet.
	The men in the car frantically gripped as they made impact - it was less like hitting a human, and more like hitting a reinforced concrete wall.
	Airbags inflated. Seatbelts locked hard enough to choke. Glass shattered, and fixings snapped.
	Connor heaved as blood dripped from him - but without any conscious thought, he turned mid-air. His foot hooked under the bumper, denting it as his kick launched it upwards.
	"Control, this is Bravo One!" the other car's driver barked in horror, as he slammed on the brakes. "Alpha vehicle down!"
	"Go! Go! Go!" Bravo team's leader roared, as the men poured out.
	The car, and its quarry, flipped forwards. It smashed down into the ground, cracking its roof. It carried on tumbling, snapping its passenger's bones. Finally, it shot down, scraped along the road, and... it didn't fall. It stayed hoisted at an angle.
	"LIGHT IT UP!"
	"But sir, our men are still in there!" one gunman protested.
	"I SAID OPEN FIRE!"
	Bravo team stopped hesitating, and their muzzles blazed.
	And then, the car raised in the air.
	Connor's eyes burned crimson, as he screamed in animalistic fury. Sparks from the gunfire fell by his side, and searing petrol glowed in preparation.
	"OH FUCK!" Bravo One gasped. "HIT THE DECK!"
	Connor pushed the car in the air, spinning it, and grabbed the back axel, before launching it forward.
	"NO!" another gunman howled, as the car landed atop his.
	*FWOOM!*
	The concussive force of the explosion knocked Connor back. He slammed the back of his head against the road, leaving another pothole in the gravel.
	His ears rang. Every cell ached.
	He forced himself to roll onto his front, and looked.
	Burning men screamed, as they desperately rolled on the ground, trying hard to not die.
	Still, they did.
	Clinging onto the waking world like a man on a cliff, Connor slipped. The world went black once more.

SCENE - Enter, Naismith

	It had been a long night.
	But nights still end, and give way to dawn. The Earth didn't stop spinning, even if it felt like it had.
	Back in Belson, a man in a creased trenchcoat stood at the scene, rubbing tired eyes beneath short, receding brown hair. He had a detective's badge, a beer belly under his shirt, and stubble from too many late nights. He looked up, and let a spatter of rain fall onto his face. He closed his eyes, sighed, and looked back at the scene.
	"DI Henry Naismith, Serious Crime." he introduced himself. He wasn't a local - he spoke with a tame, but definitive Leeds accent. "What's the story?"
	"We're still not sure, sir." the FI shrugged. "Catastrophic trauma at the base of the skull. At first glance, it looks like a contact shotgun blast under the jaw, the kind where gases blow the skull apart, but..."
	"But?"
	"There's nothing. No pellets, no casing, no gunpowder residue, not even soot. The pattern of fractures and tissue disruption doesn't quite match a firearm, either. Honestly... it's more consistent with massive blunt-force trauma. Almost as if..." he hesitated, glancing back at the corpse. "As if someone punched him."
	"Punched? No, that's not possible." Naismith shook his head incredulously.
	A young South Asian woman in a PC uniform approached the pair.
	"Sorry to bother you, sirs." she said. "PC Nina Chowdhury."
	"Not at all." Naismith smiled. "DI Naismith."
	She shook his hand. "Um, I've just had the super on the radio, asking if we're any closer to identifying the vic?"
	"From the state of the head, we've done a bloody good job identifying him as human." the FI snarked. "If your super's giving you grief, tell him to take it up with the FC."
	She nervously nodded.
	"New to the force, are you?" Naismith asked.
	"Yes, sir. Just finished a placement in Mispers, now I'm on Serious Crime."
	"And as per usual, no one on SC was informed?" he raised an eyebrow.
	"Certainly seems that way, sir." she smiled.
	"Well, this case'll be good experience." Naismith grinned.
	"You're welcome to take a look, sir." the FI shrugged. "I can't believe it either, but... all the evidence says it wasn't a gunshot."
	Naismith paused, then put on some gloves, and entered the forensic tent. Nina followed suit.

	Naismith crouched beside the corpse, squinting under the harsh white tent lights.
	"As you can see, sir - no soot, no thermal damage. Not a firearm." the FI began.
	"Any connection to the gangs here?" Naismith tilted his head.
	"None that we can see. We haven't stripped him down yet - once we do, we'll check for tattoos."
	"No need." Naismith cut in. "Look here."
	He pointed to the cadaver's knuckles, where "1 4 8 8" was scrawled in ink.
	"So?" the FI asked, confused.
	"1488 is a Nazi number." Naismith said nonchalantly.
	"Nazi number?" the FI pulled his head back. "How?"
	"14 for the Fourteen Words - a racist slogan. And the 8th letter of the alphabet is H. 88 - HH. Heil Hitler."
	"The Skinners do have a lot of outright neo-fascist members - especially Stoker's faction." the FI nodded. "We found a knuckleduster on his person too. We're checking for prints, see if we can ID this guy."
	Nina, standing a little awkwardly, shifted forward. She leaned to look at the ruined jawline. "Sir... may I?"
	Naismith gestured for her to continue.
	She pointed delicately with a gloved hand. "These fractures - see how they radiate from under the chin? There are four clear breaks, evenly spaced. Almost the same distance as knuckles."
	For a moment the tent was silent, rain pattering on the canvas above.
	"Sorry..." she said shyly. "Looking at the numbering on his knuckles... made me notice the similarity."
	"Well spotted." Naismith nodded, kneeling closer.
	"That's why we thought it was a gunshot at first." he realised. "Fragments of jawbone broke off at such a high velocity, they cut through the brain like bullets. That's why the exit wounds look unusual."
	The FI sighed. "It makes no sense. You can't just punch someone's head off."
