This is a fanfic, Young Man Logan. It is a western-style Wolverine fic; its purpose is to assist with writer's block by providing a stress-free story to work on. Like Bloodhound, it is an episodic narrative; unlike Bloodhound, it is much more loner-y; the only constant is Logan himself.
Don't be too worried about marvel canon - it's a fanfic. the point is to get a good feel for a story that'll never be commercially published - and therefore, it doesn't matter if you make a dogshit episode, and importantly, you don't have to keep redoing older scenes like in Bloodhound. Just write it one episode at a time, helping to establish a feel.
If it's really good, pull a Fifty Shades and make him a wolverine wannabe; replace his claws with actual cat claws, for instance, and change him from Logan to someone else. Remember, this is pre-superheroes, so there won't be too much to worry about
