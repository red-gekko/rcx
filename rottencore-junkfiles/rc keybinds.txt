rotten core key bindings

wasd - movement keys
right click - aim weapon
left click - fire weapon
F - melee attack/heavy attack (melee weapon equipped)
Hold F - Grapple
G - Block
H - Holster
I - Inventory
M - Map
Q - Cover
E - Interact
R - Reload
T - Order Squad to Inspect
Y - Order Squad to Attack
U - Command Menu
P - Player Menu
Space - Jump/Scale
L Shift - Spring
L Ctrl - Walk
C - Crouch
Hold C - Prone
V - Change View

Shift + [Num] - Dual Wield (must reload weapons individually)
0 - Unarmed
1 - Left Sidearm
2 - Right Sidearm
3 - Shoulder Weapon
4 - Back Weapon
5 - Hip Weapon
6 - Throwables (tap to cycle)
7 - Tools (tap to cycle)
Tab - Weapon Wheel & Abilities
