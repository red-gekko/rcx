Summary of Powers:
	Regeneration:
		Heals from minor injuries in minutes and major trauma in hours or days.
		Extreme, near-fatal trauma triggers an Enhancement Coma, a longer Healing Coma where their body adapts to be more able to resist that damage in future
			[example - after Connor was mortally wounded by the Judge and lost his lungs and primary heart, grew several tertiary hearts and lungs became modular]
		Can adapt to specific conditions over time - if they regularly go prolongued periods without oxygen, they will gradually be able to hold their breath for longer - essentially, can train themselves to break beyond even their own biology. There is no theoretical limit to this ability - through sufficient training, their body can defy basic reason.
		Regerative feats:
			Recovered from severe smoke inhalation and multiple 3rd-degree burns in the space of 9 hours.
			Regrew an entire hand in 16 hours.
			Regrew an entire arm, as well as 60% of its intestinal tract, in 27 hours.
			Regrew its entire lung lining after a chlorine gas attack in 3 hours. Was observed breathing and speaking normally after only 40 minutes of the attack.
			Recovered from an attack which led to its lungs, heart, and ribcage being punched out in 3 days. Especially impressive as said attack meant all healing was performed via anaerobic respiration


	Biological Enhancements:
		Circulatory/Respiratory System:
			Multiple backup hearts and hyperoxygenated blood enhance resilience and physical performance. Two primary hearts, and multiple small secondary hearts.
			Capable of breathing toxic air or water temporarily; can survive without oxygen through anaerobic respiration.
				[side effect - anaerobic respiration will eventually cause cryptobiosis, a coma-state. Cannot use full strength without oxygen either]
			Blood vessels are capable of closing rapidly, massively reducing blood loss
			Multilpe windpipes and chambered lungs allow constant breath - one lung breathes out while another breathes in
		Digestive/Urological System:
			Can digest just about any organic matter. Multi-chambered stomach allows ruminant behaviour and easy toxin removal.
			Super-strong stomach muscles allow for weaponised stomach acid spit
			Liver and kidneys hyper-optimised. As long as healing factor is active, toxins are largely harmless in the bloodstream.
				Side effect: Physically incabable of getting drunk. As in, can consume straight ethanol without intoxication. Having ethanol literally injected into their veins continously will at most make them feel "buzzed"
				Anti-intoxication can however be disabled by lemon juice - somehow. Downing a cup of concentrated lemon juice, or eating a whole lemon, can allow them to get tipsy. They can't get past that, though.
			Toxin resistance is related to the Smart immune system - essentially, the body recognises substances as having foreign origins, and implements measures to neutralise and then either digest or expel.
				The flipside is, synthesised hormones (like paradrenaline or xenoendorphins) still affect them, as the body recognises them as coming from itself. This can be used to induce certain effects through hormonal cocktails (i.e., a dose of dopamine to mimic stimulants).
				Also, isolated markers can be added to substances to trick the immune system
		Metabolism: Efficient energy usage enables prolonged survival in hostile conditions, including starvation.
			[corollary - going without food restricts power - cannot heal without repurposing existing flesh, and is unable to use super-strength. Toughness remains as-is.]
			Requires a daily caloric intake of 9,000 calories to operate at peak efficiency. Can operate at near-peak efficiency at 6,000 calories. Can operate at high efficiency at 3,000 calories. Can operate at the same levels as a human at 1,000 calories. Maintains toughness regardless of calorie consumption, unless Ketosis has begun.
			Metabolism is extremely efficient all things considered. Minimal heat waste means basal body temperatrue is 1C higher than a baseliner.
		Immune system: Smart immune system can target specific problems. Completely immune to viruses due to unique genetic makeup (no viruses exist that could infect their cells). Bacteria and parastites are systematically targeted by the immune system, as are cancers.
		Telomerase: Ageless due to persistent telomerase production. Likewise able to recover from DNA damage over time.
		Evolution: Has a unique organ containing a fetus-like mass. Here, the body experiments with new mutations, seeing which ones would be beneficial to be applied to the whole body. Said 'fetus' has no frontal cortex, and is consumed when food is scarce.

	Physical enhancements:	
		Tissue Enhancements: Superior strength due to extreme muscle density. Can lift armoured vehicles, deliver punches with the force of 10 jackhammers, and leap 10 metres vertically. Flesh can receive gunshots from small arms fire and only result in bruising. Armour-piercing rounds like explosive rounds or CNT-tipped bullets can cut through flesh. Like bones, muscles are reinforced with CNT fibres.
		Bone Enhancements: Bones are increased in density and reinforced with chitin and CNT fibres - like rebar in concrete. Breaking these bones requires extreme force - up to 10x that of a regular human. Bones are also optimised to direct impact force to the joints - most 'breaks' are in fact dislocations. Due to reinforcement, despite major increase in density, bones are actually lighter per volume than traditional human bones.
		Physical feats:
			With heavy exertion, lifted the front of a BMP-3 over its head, flipping it over after considerable effort. Exertion was equivalent to a human lifting an extreme weight. A BMP-3 typically weighs 18-22 tonnes.
			With moderate exertion, lifting and holding an entire Jeep above its head. Exertion was equivalent to a human lifting a relatively heavy set of weights.
			Leaping vertically in excess of 10 meters.
			Overall, feats of strength appear to be reliably in excess of 10x that of the world record for a baseliner. When under the influece of a paradrenaline surge (see below), these feats become even more impressive.
			Delivering 'light jabs' that were able to crack concrete as though it were a jackhammer. Said feat was performed without 'breaking a sweat' so to speak - exhibiting a similar amount of exertion a baseliner would by using a punching bag.
				A jackhammer strike is typically ~5,000 N. The strongest recorded human punch is ~8,000 N. The strongest recorded punch by these superhumans reached ~120,000 N - see the below explosive killing.
			Punching a human opponent with enough force to practically disintegrate them - Forensic Investigators initially assumed the opponent had been killed by high explosives due to the extent of gorification.
			Punched a human opponent in the chin, ripping off the head and destroying it. FIs initially believed the killing had been performed with a shotgun fired at close-contact range.
			Dragging several cars out of a fast-running stream from underwater away to safety.
			Withstand small arms fire with only bruising.
			Survive armour-piercing rounds with injury comparable to subsonic pistol rounds on a baseliner.
			Fell from a 13-story drop, dislocating its spine. Upon being laid down flat, regained consciousness and was able to hobble away. In less than 2 hours, was engaging in brutal combat again.
			Carrying on from above: was shot at with over 150 assault rifle rounds while exhausted and injured from aforementioned fall. Was able to win ensuing melee fight.
			Has reached terminal velocity during a freefall and survived.
			Has survived exposure to the vacuum of space with no protective gear
			Survived a mission in a meltdowning nuclear reactor - was critically weakened by radiation, but recovered in a few days.

	Neurological & Sensory Systems:
		Enhanced reflexes, acute senses (including low-light vision and thermal detection), and the ability to multitask visually with chameleon-like eyes.
			Specifically, super-senses include, but not limited to:
				Vocal cord enhancements working in tandem with audiosensory capacity, permitting echolocation
				Deployable tapetum lucidum - allows for night vision, while being pulled back when in higher-light conditions, or when image sharpness is more important
				Able to shift visual detection to include infrared (aka, thermal vision)
				Can detect electromagnetic waves, such as those coming from electronic items or ionizing radiation
				Can activate a synesthetic "Sense Mode", an Active Power that allows them to use all their super-senses at once, while cleanly formatting the input as visual
		Backup neural network in the torso can restore basic function after head trauma - can theoretically survive decapitation.
		Natural telepathic abilities; while minor without training, can perform tactile telepathic powers like mind-merging, mental probing, emotional reading, lie detecting, and more
		'Paradrenaline' allows bursts of superhuman feats but induces catatonia afterward. Extreme stress can trigger "Berserker Rage", mindless rampages of unstoppable force
		Nervous system able to communicate with itself to an unprecendented degree in biology - the body has a 'blueprint' that allows it to direct its healing without becoming overly tumour-ridden or creating malformed organs. While in Enhancement, the body can tell based on subconscious thoughts what improvements would be useful.
		Senses are massively fine-tuned.
			Can clearly hear frequencies up to 40kHz.
			Can see small amounts of Infrared and Ultraviolet radiation.
			Can actively sense electromagnetic effects, including bioelectricity and radiation.
			Can use echolocation, though this requires concentration due to the frequencies messing with their senses.
			Inside the eyes, a small bioluminescent organ emits low-frequency lights, which are used like a radar system to assist depth perception and provide night vision.
				When in Berserker Rage, this organ goes haywire and produces visible red light, resulting in the iconic glowing red eyes.

	Vulnerabilities:
		Regular migraines are a constant hindrance, exacerbated under stress.
		Healing is limited by food supply and can be overwhelmed by sustained or multi-faceted damage.
		Use of so-called "Active Powers" (armour, wall-crawling, super-vision, Rage) will exhaust them quickly
		Reliant on specialist diet of bodybuilding shakes and high-protein meals. When faced with starvation, loses access to super-strength and healing.
		Susceptible to chemical, thermal, or radiological attacks if not mitigated
			[corollary - they are still more resistant to these than humans - they're just more vulnerable to say fire damage than they are other kinds of damage]
			Exposure to heavy radiation disables the healing factor and slowly saps away at their health. However, can develop rad-resistance with regular exposure. DNA repair mechanisms mean the damage is rarely permanent. Experiences Radiation hormesis much more reliably than baseliners
			While able to survive and ignore most drugs and toxins, they can still distract the healing factor, leaving them vulnerable to injury
			Fire and acid do a lot more damage than most attacks - they take longer to heal from burns.
		Neural overload is a major risk - sonic weapons can be highly disruptive, as can electrical disruptions
		Overexertion can lead to a breakdown in thermoregulation, causing a "Death Flush" - an episode where their bodies become unable to maintain homeostasis, and instead heat up to the point of death
		Berserker Rage leads to constant violent urges that must be surpressed via self-harm
		Requires a high caloric intake and may enter a "healing coma" for extreme damage.
		Extreme healing factor means massive cancer risk - if anti-cancer systems are disabled, will quickly suffer from rapid cancer growth

	Skills and Equipment:
		Able to telepathically communicate with other superhumans at short range - in practical terms, no language barrier between supers. They can also use this to 'mind-merge' with each other, sharing tactical and intimate details
		In posession of "Morphing Suit" - an organic substance they can eject from their skin to form clothes, armour, and weapons out of the substance - allowing them to change between costume and civillian clothes easily, and also means they always have a knife handy.
			list of morphing powers:
				clothing and costume
					can quickly form new clothes, repair damage, clean, or change into costume
				blades
					several options - throwing knives, wrist-mounted swords, or handheld weapons. can use more exotic forms, like chain-tied knives, axes, or spears
				adhesion
					several micro-hooks form at the contact surface, allowing super-grip
				armour
					can expel more substance into the costume to thicken it
				gauntlets
					can thicken his gloves to make blunt weapons for nonlethal takedowns of superhumans
				crossbows
					can morph wrist-mounted crossbows, allowing for ranged attacks. can use blunter bolts for incapacitation, or sharp ones for kills
				grappling/rope
					can morph rope from the body; can be used with crossbows to form a grapple gun, swing from buildings, or restrain enemies
						specifically, grappling hooks are formed with applying the adhesion power to a suction cup at the end of the rope, then firing this rope from either the crossbow, or an air/gas powered contraption (see below)
				shields
					use these when both defense and agility are needed - should typically be forearm bucklers rather than full ballistic shields unless he's covering someone else
				gliding
					morphs a cape that can rigidify on-command, and uses super-senses to detect updrafts and wind tunnels. can also use grappling to gain extra velocity (through pendulum swings or rapid reeling); can also use wall-running to gain altitude, or push self off of walls for extra speed
					can also use air pressure (see below) to provide thrust, allowing indefinite flight as long as stamina is high. can also use for rapid air braking, and parachutes
				caltrops
					area denial weapons, as well as anti-vehicle traps
				shock absorbers
					handwave his ability to land on his feet from a glide by saying he morphs honeycomb pads on his boots, which dissolves after landing. this also goes in with the armour
				bolas
					use these for non-lethal capture - can morph them into hogties or cuffs once he catches them
				net launchers
					use these for wide-area non-lethal ranged capture
				restraints
					gags, blindfolds, handcuffs, hogties, or even hanging people by their ankles from the ceiling
				water-walking
					pads that keep him at surface level as long as he's sprinting
				trip-lines
					clotheslines used as traps or perches
				prosthetic makeup
					change unmasked appearance
				first aid kit
					tourniquets, splints, bandages, wound-covering sprayfoam (delivered from air power, see below), and more
				multitools
					lockpicks, screwdrivers, spanners, crowbars
				pseudomorph
					thin mannequin shaped like him to act as a decoy
				air pressure maneuvers
					can rapidly pressurise substance for various uses - see below for special ones
				kinetic bursts
					can use air pressure bursts to increase force in movement - making punches faster if a burst happens at the elbow, or make them deliver more force by bursting air from the knuckles on contact
				smoke bombs
					release a burst of morphing substance as an aerosol, creating a short-lived cloud
				frag bombs
					hardens the substance before bursting, creating chitinous frags at high velocity
				sound funnel
					don't use too often as it's a little goofy, but use it when he's trying to catch distant sounds
				airsoft guns
					compressed air firearms that can shoot pellets of biomass, with different variants
						smoke rounds, which create a mini smoke bomb effect (if we allow pheremone manipulation, can also be used to disperse these)
						dart rounds (can either be used as simple flechettes, or if PM is allowed as above)
						acid rounds (rounds coated with stomach acid - supply acid to rounds by creating a running mouthguard tube to the shooting arm)
						baton rounds - think rubber bullets
						concussive rounds - above, but explodes on impact, delivering a wave of supercompressed air
						explosive rounds - above, but uses the chitin frag power for lethal options
						chitin rounds - baton rounds, but harder, sharper, and delivered at higher velocity, making them lethal
