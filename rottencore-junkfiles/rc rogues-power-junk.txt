
Werewolf
	Powers:
		Strength: Can lift >20t
		Durability: Can take direct hits from assault rifles with minimal damage
		Speed: Can reach speeds of >100mph
		Senses: Superior senses - twice as good as anything on Earth, plus Super-Vision allowing for synesthesia and echolocation
		Reflexes: Can slow down perception of time to a crawl
		Healing: can recover from serious injuries in hours; can regrow any body part so long as the brain (or backup chest-brain) is working

Super-Agents (generic)
	Strength: Can lift >3t
	Durability: Can withstand pistol shots with only minor damage
	Speed: Can reach speeds of >30mph
	Senses: Slightly superhuman - can smell as good as a dog, hear as good as a bat, and see as good as a hawk
	Reflexes: Can react twice as fast as a human
	Healing: Can recover from serious injuries in days; can regrow small parts like teeth, ears, or other extremities

Ranger
	Strength: Can lift >10t
	Durability: Can withstand rifle shots with only minor damage
	Speed: Can reach speeds of >60mph
	Senses: Slightly superhuman - can smell as good as a dog, hear as good as a bat, and see as good as a hawk
	Reflexes: Can react thrice as fast as a human
	Healing: Can recover from serious injuries in days; can regrow body parts after a while. When cancerous, healing factor is disabled

Stunlocke suit
	Strength: Can lift >5t (>10t when worn by Super-Agents)
	Durability: Suit can take assault rifle fire for short bursts; when upgraded with CNT armour, can even tank hand grenades
	Speed: Can enhance user to speeds of >30mph (>60 for supers)
	Senses: suit has thermal and sonar built into helmet
	Reflexes: Suit can administer adrenaline to highten reflexes and combat awareness
	Healing: Suit can adminsiter healing serum; armour cannot repair itself

Whiplash
	Super-Agent physical ability
	Can manipulate trajectory of objects in motion

Whisper
	Peak human physical ability; later Super-Agent
	Can turn invisible by bending light; later upgraded into perception filter covering noise, smell, and presence too

Roach
	Nigh-invulnerable skeleton

Harpy
	Fragile flier, with sonic scream attack

Changeling/Terror Toma/Malignant
	Sentient cancer blob; able to shapeshift but less strong and tough without a true host

Goliath
	Massive, cybernetically-enhanced giant. Can lift >100t

Mastermind
	Genius-level intellect. Self-made power armour capable of burst flight, and can match Werewolves for physicality

Sentinel
	AI system designed by Mastermind. Superior physical stats for main bodyform, but zero healing factor

Edge
	Cyborg ninja. Stunlocke-level capabilities

Bug Boy/Myrmex
	Super-agent physicals. Can psycically command a swarm of insects bonded to him

Twitch
	Super-agent physicals, but with speeds of up to 140mph. Can generate electrical attakcs

Blitz
	Super-agent physicals, made redundant by cybernetic enhancements. Ranger/Stunlocke-level physicals afterwards. Jetpack for improved mobility. Variety of tricks and flunky mechanics, like drones and grenades

Jinx
	Stunlocke-type suit, modified with extra cyberware and sacrificing some armour for a drone backpack, allowing for flunky deployment

Hellfire
	former fireman turned pyromaniac; flamethrower, molotovs, fire axe, flare gun, etc. Following Psychic Awakening, gains pyrokinesis, becoming even more dangerous and unhinged

Frank Ryder
	human physicals, but with superhuman short-range aim

Kemetar
	Resurrective immortality

Masters Shao
	Vampires - one a vampire mage, the other a Dhampir warrior

Countess
	Vampiress; powerful sorceror. Has Ranger-level physicals due to drinking Connor's blood

Mr. Magpie
	Vampire; excellent detective. Can shift into gargoyle form. Can perform psychometry in normal form, and precognition in monster form

Agent Khan
	Peak human; highly skilled martial artist, with litany of gadgets

Impact
	Super-Agent physicals. Can make telekinetic blasts and force fields

Blink
	Super-Agent physicals. Can teleport short distances

Dominus
	Compelling voice and telekinesis. Later refined into outright magic through daemonology

Frostbite
	Super-Agent physicals
