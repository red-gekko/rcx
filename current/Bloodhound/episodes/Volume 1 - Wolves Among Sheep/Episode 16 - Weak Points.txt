SCENE - Connor in Chloe's Flat

	Hated pretending that he was a human, and that it hurt.
	As his flesh twisted and turned, regenerating alveoli and closing perforations, he forced it to hide away.
	Don't let her know. Don't let her see.
	"What happened?!" Chloe asked, the concern palpable.
	"Nothing, nothing... just some Skinners." Connor said, swallowing his shame.
	"Was this because of how you rescued me last night?" she asked, trembling.
	"No, not at all." he said firmly. "They're just a bunch of pricks. Besides, Sparky and I fended them off. I just took a few licks on the way."

	"No, we can't... if we go to the hospital, it'll become a whole thing."

	"You can take the bed!" she cut in.
	"You sure? I don't wanna get in your way..." Connor said, shyly.
	"Connor." she replied. "You're hurt, and you need rest."

	"But nothing. I'm wide awake anyway - you go get some sleep."

	"I had noticed that." she nodded. "The skyscrapers, the underground, the big island just off the coast..."

SCENE - Chloe studies the hand

	Chloe walked into the lab, and saw a middle-aged man standing there, reading on a laptop.
	"Morning, Jones!" she smiled cheerfully. "Coffee?"
	"I'd love one, ta." Jones nodded. She grabbed a pair of coffees from the machine.
	"So, what are you working on?" she asked, putting their coffees down.
	Jones took a gulp of his coffee, and smiled, enjoying the taste.
	"We've got a weird one." he began.
	"What's that?" Chloe replied, sipping her own coffee.
	Jones stood, and opened up a freezer box.
	Chloe walked over, looked in the box, and recoiled in disgust.
	"A hand?!" she asked, horrified.
	"Belonging to the 'Werewolf'." Jones nodded. "Dug out of some rubble on the 25th. Cops have sent it over. We've had a look at it, but it might be good for you to take a crack before we incinerate it."
	Chloe grimaced. "Alright, let's have a look..."
	She lifted the hand out of the container and placed it on the workbench.
	She began by taking a scalpel, and tried to make an incision. It was a difficult cut, but after considerable effort, she managed to break the surface.
	"Damn." Jones muttered. "It's been over a week since it was severed, and the skin's still that tough?"
	"How did it get severed?" Chloe asked, curious.
	"Not sure. But look at the wrist - it's a clean cut. Working theory is, it got pinned under the rubble, and the Werewolf amputated it himself."
	"Jesus..." Chloe gasped. "What kind of person would just cut off their own hand like that?"
	"The kind that can grow a new one." Jones replied. "Apparently, he's been active since, and seen with 2 hands."
	Chloe paused for a moment. "I'm half-tempted to steal that for the Hox project."
	Jones chuckled. "It's not stealing if you do it in a lab. Then, it's science."
	Chloe laughed, before continuing to dissect the hand.
	"Initial findings?"
	"The skin... it's strange. Aside from the toughness... it seems more like kevlar than natural skin. Hell, I'm not sure if this layer here is meant to be the real skin. It's... it's a glove. But bonded to his flesh."
	Jones cocked an eyebrow. "That... that makes a lot of sense, actually. Hence why we couldn't get fingerprints or anything. But testing it confirmed it was an organic substance, with matching DNA to the rest of the hand... anyway, what else?"
	"Look at the muscles." she replied. "They're not just denser than normal muscles, they're... I mean, look at the structure of them."
	Jones leant over and looked at the muscle sample Chloe had extracted. While to the untrained eye it looked normal, there was an uncanny quality to it.
	"It's not a normal muscle. It looks like it's been..."
	"Engineered." Jones nodded.
	Chloe thought for a moment.
	"How bloody was it when they found it?"
	Jones paused. "Good question..."
	He pulled up a photo on his laptop.
	"Minimal bleeding... of course." Chloe said, upon seeing the photo.
	"Oh?" Jones asked, curious.
	"Look at the blood vessels - here." she motioned. "Tiny compared to normal. And numerous. He must have angiogenesis levels off-the-charts. It's a wonder he's not a lump of cancer already." she explained. "Such a massive number of vessels would allow minimal bleeding - since, as soon as the hand was cut off, the blood vessels would constrict to prevent exsanguination. Having loads of small ones makes that process easier."
	Jones stroked his chin, impressed. "Good find." he nodded. "I guess you could say you're rather 'handy', eh?"
	Chloe rolled her eyes, but couldn't help but laugh.
	"Okay... let's take a look at the bones--Agh!" she gasped.
	The hand gripped hold of hers, tightly. Not as tight as it used to be able to, but tight enough to hold on.
	"Um... uh..." she stammered. Then, thankfully, it went limp once more.
	"...Jesus." Jones shuddered.
	Chloe breathed out. "It's still alive... barely, but still. It must be respirating anaerobically."
	"But how the hell is it moving without a brain?" Jones cocked a brow.
	"...octopus." she realised.
	"...what?"
	"An octopus's arms have additional neurons, which allows them to act independently." she explained. "I can't think of a better explanation - though I'd like to get a closer look inside. Of course, if it's defrosted enough to spasm like that... we'd better hurry up and refreeze it before it starts to decompose."
	"Rather you than me." Jones grinned. "We've got a cold box for that?"
	She tilted her head. Jones stood up, and grabbed a metal box - it looked a little bit like a microwave.
	"Cold box. It's basically a blast chiller you can dissect in." he explained. "There's these little ports at the back with gloves, and the tools are already in there."

	"When are they planning on incinerating it?" she asked.

	"Sure thing - any reason?" Jones tilted his head.
	"I think I can do some maths. Figure out specific things - punch force, impact resilience, et cetera..." she shrugged.
