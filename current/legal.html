Last updated: 2025

Bloodhound is a work of fiction. Nothing in this work should be read as a factual statement about any real person, organisation, institution, or event. The following disclaimer applies to the entirety of the work, including all volumes, episodes, scenes, in-universe documents, and supplementary materials.

1. Work of Fiction

Bloodhound is an original work of fiction set in an alternate-history world. Its characters, settings, organisations, and events are the product of the author's imagination or are used fictitiously. The narrative timeline and any in-universe documents, primers, or fictional media referenced within the work form part of that fictional universe and should be understood in that context only.

2. Historical Figures, Organisations, and Events

Where the work references real historical figures, organisations, institutions, agencies, companies, or events, those references are purely fictional. They are not assertions of fact, and they do not represent the author's views about those subjects or about the individuals or groups concerned.

Great effort has been made to ensure that any real person referenced is deceased both in-universe and in reality. References to such persons are not statements of fact and should not be relied upon as historical or biographical information. For example, the depiction of a historical figure being killed by a fictional intelligence agency is not a factual claim that such an event occurred.

3. Living Persons

Any resemblance between the fictional characters in this work and real persons, living or dead, is entirely coincidental. No character is intended to depict, represent, or refer to any specific living individual, and no inference should be drawn from any apparent similarity.

4. Real-World Organisations and Media

References to real-life organisations, companies, agencies, or existing media should never be taken as factual statements about those organisations or properties. Such references are used fictitiously, often satirically, and do not reflect the author's actual beliefs about, or characterisation of, those organisations.

5. Satire and Social Commentary

Satirical elements and social commentary are integral to the narrative. They are not factual assertions by the author. Where the work depicts extremism, authoritarianism, corruption, or other objectionable conduct, that depiction is a critique of such conduct — not an endorsement of it. The author does not advocate for, support, or condone any of the ideologies, policies, or actions that the work depicts critically.

6. Sensitive Content

This work contains the discussion and depiction of themes and events that many readers will, and arguably should, find disturbing. These include, but are not limited to:

  • Physical, emotional, and sexual abuse
  • Racial violence and hate crime
  • Bigotry of many forms, including racism, homophobia, transphobia, and ableism
  • The mistreatment and exploitation of children
  • Torture, body horror, and graphic injury
  • Self-harm, suicidal ideation, and depictions of suicide
  • War, ethnic cleansing, and mass violence
  • Addiction

A full content warning, including a per-episode breakdown, is available on the Content Warning page. Readers who may be affected by any of the above are strongly encouraged to consult it before reading.
