EPISODE - Warzones

Wednesday, 20th of January 2016.
Maybe, just maybe, a date that would be remembered fondly by students of history.
Liberation Day.
The day a tyrant died.
The day that the Chechen National Legion fell, and the Chechen ASSR was reborn.
All that was certain, in the early hours of the morning though, as a covert plane rode over the skies of the Caucasus, was that two Werewolves were hungry.
Connor and Sparky sat on their seats, furiously scarfing down some pre-battle snacks. Ivan had a whiteboard with several notes on it. He stood and addressed the pair.
"Another briefing?" Connor snarked.
"Blah blah, Chechnya, blah blah, Russians, blah blah." Ivan jested back. "To summarise, Chechnya was conquered by the Timurids - a Turco-Mongol state in Iran - before Ivan the Terrible--"
"You're not that bad!" Sparky barked, confused.
"Thanks, tovarishch." Ivan grinned. "Anyway, the region wasn't fully annexed by the Russians until the rule of Peter the Great - that's when we expanded into the Caucasus. Typical colonial practices ensued - most notably, a mass deportation of over 80% of the Chechen and Ingush population during the 1860s and 70s. Anyway, skipping ahead a bit, following the October Revolution, Chechnya along with the rest of the Caucasus was re-integrated as part of the Greater Caucasian SFSR, which included Transcaucasia - places like Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan - and Ciscaucasia, where you'll find Ossetia, Dagestan, and relevant for us - Chechnya. And its current de facto leader - Vadim Zamaldayev."
He pointed to a photograph.
"Zamaldayev was an influential general in the Ciscaucasian branch of the Red Army, having carved out his own petty fiefdom under Kruglov's idiocy. When things started getting unstable, he threw his lot in with the rebels, presenting himself as an Islamist militant in opposition to Soviet imperialism."
"Imperialism? Any truth to the matter?" Connor cocked an eyebrow.
"Before Kruglov? Not even an iota. After?" Ivan sighed heavily. "It hurts to say it, but we were acting like Capitalists under him. Not that Zamaldayev was a better man, but... that's not good enough."
Connor smiled softly. Ivan took a deep breath, and continued.
"I've told you a little about my run-in with him."
"The battle of Grozny?" Connor tilted his head.
"That's the one. We managed to evacuate most Soviet Loyalists that day, but we lost the SFSR. We had to retreat to the Georgian SSR, and regroup. That's when I joined the KGB."
"How have things been in Chechnya since then?"
"There's a Turkic group called the Kumyks who live here - they and ethnic Russians make up the vast majority of non-Chechens here. Zamaldayev has taken to ethnically cleansing them. Currently, he's personally overseeing massacres."
"Silver lining - he's exposed." Connor growled excitedly.
"Damn right. Okay, so - this is the plan. Anti-Zamaldayev forces have already started their attack on his position, but he's deployed Super-Agents. We go in, grab Zamaldayev. You kill his guards while I take him away. Sound good?"
"Sounds good." Connor nodded. "Let's put this monster down."
Sparky sat up. "I'm ready." he barked.
"Okay... see you both on the ground!" Ivan shouted, running out the door. Connor and Sparky followed.
As they fell, Connor took a moment to take in his surroundings. He could see almost the entire Caucasus from there.
"This is crazy!" Sparky yapped excitedly.
Connor gripped Sparky hard, nuzzling him. They enjoyed the view.
He regained his composure, and they morphed up their gliders as Ivan pulled his parachute chord. The trio landed in a small, abandoned village.
"Okay, let's go. Do not kill Zamaldayev - he's ours." Ivan growled. With that, the trio descended.

In the distance, the tyrant stood atop a tank, barking orders to his soldiers as they mowed down Kumyk fighters in cold blood. The massacre was brutal, the screams of the dying echoing across the open field. Zamaldayev's eyes gleamed with sadistic pleasure.
"[Kill them all!]" he roared in Chechen, the command fuelling his soldiers' bloodlust as they reloaded their guns, preparing for more carnage.
Connor's blood boiled at the sight. His fists clenched, his jaw tightened, and before anyone could react, he leapt into the air, his body propelled by raw superhuman strength.
He landed with a ground-shaking thud right in front of Zamaldayev's tank, his eyes glowing red.
The Super-Agents around him snapped to attention, their guns drawn in a flash. Bullets rained down on Connor, the air around him crackling with the force of the barrage.
He moved like a blur, his enhanced reflexes making him nearly untouchable, but some of the bullets managed to strike his body. The impact was heavy, but his healing factor kicked in almost instantly, the wounds knitting back together. Shrapnel from explosions embedded in his gut, tearing into his flesh, but he didn't even flinch. His Rage drove him forward, and with a growl, he pulled the shards free, throwing them at the enemy like deadly projectiles.
Sparky, a blur of fury and muscle, launched into the fray alongside him. He tore through the soldiers with ferocity, his jaws snapping shut around their throats, tearing their heads clean off with horrifying ease. He howled, the sound of it striking terror into the hearts of Zamaldayev's men.
An armoured carrier drove into the fray, and its machine gun tore through the battlefield, rattling through their ears. Connor ducked to cover, but saw bullets slam into Sparky, who was knocked backwards.
"SPARTACUS! NO!" Connor howled. Sparky raised his head, and crawled over into cover.
"I'm alright... I can still fight... just give me a minute..." he panted.
Connor nodded, then looked at the APC.
"I'm gonna take it down." he vowed.
He morphed up his full armour, and rushed out from the cover, darting in a zig-zag to avoid the incoming gunfire. Arriving at the front of it, he placed his hands underneath it, and lifted as hard as he could.
It was agonising. He felt like his arms were on fire, but he pushed on. He raised the APC up vertically, and roared. He pushed it backwards, and the once-proud vehicle had been flipped upside-down. The crew scrambled to escape, only for Ivan's sniper rounds to kill them to a man.
"Excellent work, tovarishch." Ivan congratulated over the radio.
Connor panted, and took a moment. The reprieve didn't last long.
A tank round collided into his abdomen, sending him sprawling to the ground, his body contorted in agony as the blast ripped into his flesh. Blood poured from the gaping wound, and his body shuddered and shook. But even as his intestines spilled from the cavity, Connor's resolve remained unbroken.
With a guttural roar, Connor grabbed the mass of his entrails, now hanging freely from the wound, and with a brutal motion, he used them as a garrotte. He wrapped them around the neck of a nearby Super-Agent, pulling tight with inhuman strength. The Agent struggled and clawed, and Connor tightened the gut-garrotte, before jumping down, yanking and snapping the Agent's neck.
Another tank shell ripped through the air, slamming into his arm. The explosion was deafening, and his arm was mutilated at the elbow, blood spraying across the battlefield. He barely acknowledged the pain, his expression grim and unwavering.
The battlefield was now littered with the corpses of Zamaldayev's soldiers, the once-proud fighting force reduced to bloodied, broken bodies in the wake of Connor's rampage. His body was covered in blood - his own, theirs - it didn't matter. The Rage was still burning in him.
Ivan, positioned from a distance, picked off the remaining soldiers with deadly precision. Isabella sang with each shot, and Zamaldayev's forces fell one by one, unable to stand against the combined might of the trio.
Connor clambered up to the top of Zamaldayev's tank. He gripped the main gun, and with all his might, lifted.
He felt the metal bend along with his bones. His ruptured arm tore as blood spat from it, and he screamed and howled in pain.
His eyes burned brighter, as his stomach made a familiar heave.
*HAWK-ptoo!*
The base of the turret hissed as the acid burned through its fixings.
Then--
*CRACK!*
The entire turret ripped away from the tank's hull, swinging back and crashing into the ground behind him. Connor looked down, and saw Zamaldayev rushing out of the chassis. He was tempted, but remembered Ivan's orders. Instead, he gripped the barrel again, and brought the turret swinging down on the hull. The explosion ripped through the air, knocking Zamaldayev to the ground, and sending Connor flying away.
As Connor landed, his arm finally detached, falling from him with a sickening *plop* sound. Connor stumbled to his feet, his breath ragged.
"Fuck you, tank." he spat, sending teeth, blood, and bile onto the snow.
Zamaldayev's forces were no more. The only sound left was the distant cries of the innocent civilians, still caught in the chaos.
Ivan descended, and clapped Zamaldayev in irons.
"[You will suffer for this, traitor.]" he whispered in the tyrant's ear.
Down below, Connor's expression turned vacant. The red in his eyes faded, but his pupils were almost pinpoint-small. With no signs of a human mind, he dragged himself over to a nearby corpse, and sank his teeth into it.
Sparky cringed, recognising the same vile hunger that tore within him. He wanted to suppress it, like he had been for so long, but... if Connor was doing it?
Ivan looked down, and turned pale at the sight of his comrades consuming the dead. After Connor took in a couple of mouthfuls, he tried to rise.
"Obaldet! Connor! Are you alright?"
Connor collapsed, his face turning white. Ivan and Sparky caught him, and dragged him and Zamaldayev to the plane.
A Kumyk soldier rushed to the group. "[Thank you, thank you... you saved us.]"
Ivan looked at the soldier, nodded, and stood atop the flipped APC. He began to shout.
"[Let it be known! That on this day, the monster Zamaldayev fell! That from this day on, the Chechens and Kumyks will live side by side! That tomorrow's dawn will be Red! Long live the Soviet Union!]"
The present villagers, using their understanding of Russian, erupted into cheers. Soldiers fired their guns into the sky, and "[Long live the Soviet Union! On with the Red Dawn!]" was heard chanting through the village.

Connor woke up, his head heavy and woozy. He looked around - he was on the plane again. Sparky ran up and licked him.
"Welcome back, tovarishch." Ivan said, warmly.
"What... what happened?" Connor asked, his voice hoarse.
"You're okay." Sparky smiled. "You got hurt badly by the explosions, but you're okay now."
Connor looked down at his gut. It was red and mangled, still bleeding slightly - but he was alive.
"Wow... is there anything I can't heal?" he chuckled, before coughing up blood.
Ivan laughed. "I hope we never find out." With that, Connor rose.
"Here. Drink this." Ivan said, passing Connor a vodka. Connor took it and smiled.
Ivan raised his glass. "Na zdorovye." he smiled, and they drank. Even Sparky had a shot, causing him to gag.
"Ew! That tastes like soap!" he complained, causing Connor to laugh, and then clutch his abdomen - it still hurt.
"To Mother Russia." Connor toasted, smirking.
"Hah! Indeed." Ivan laughed.
"Where's Zamaldayev?"
"In some hell-hole run by the KGB." Ivan smirked. "We did good today. Anti-Zamaldayev forces have already started fighting harder. And most of them have sworn loyalty to the Soviet Union. We're..." Ivan paused, smiling happily. "We're so close."
"Glad I could help." Connor smiled, before recoiling in pain.
"You okay, tovarishch?"
"I'm fine... for a man whose guts were just made into set dressing, and whose arm is currently regrowing." Connor quipped through the pain.
"How does it feel? To grow a whole arm." Ivan asked, curious.
"Not great." Connor chuckled, clutching his abdomen.
"Can you feel any pain?"
"My fingertips. My *left* fingertips." Connor said, motioning to his left arm-stump.
"Phantom pains. I can't imagine how bad that feels, tovarishch. Still, at least you can heal. We thought we'd lost you there."
Sparky whimpered. "Don't ever die. I'd be lost without you."
Connor knelt down, and went to stroke him, before realising he was using the wrong hand. He chuckled, and stroked Sparky's ears with his other hand.
"I'll be here forever, buddy. You and me - til the end of the Earth."
"You remember what happened?" Ivan asked cautiously.
Connor thought for a moment, before turning pale again. "I... that hasn't happened before. Sparky, was I--"
"Just like when I ate that guy ages ago." Sparky nodded. "You've been suppressing the hunger for a long time - but in situations like that, when the fighting is over, you just need to eat."
"Eat. Eat. Eat." the word echoed through his subconscious. Connor shuddered.
Ivan cleared his throat. "Anyway, I've just heard back from our pals in the KGB."
"So soon?" Connor asked.
"We don't mess around." Ivan grinned. "We found something - the reason why Chloe was put on your case."
Connor leant in, intent on listening. "It's my fault, isn't it? They put her there to get to me?"
"Hell of an ego there, bratan." Ivan smirked. "Well, it's partly that. Having her there means they think you won't go into those labs to snoop around. The main reason, though - she's a genius. Honestly. It looks like she's managed to figure out some insane shit."
"Like what?"
"How to get limbs to regrow - topical, hah." Ivan began. "She also found this weird adrenaline thing - it's what gives you that Rage you have. And, she's made breakthroughs on some antibiotic-resistant bacteria treatments. She's a prodigy."
Connor smiled warmly. "She sure is. So... you're telling me, they'd have assigned her anyway? It's... it's not my fault?"
"That's how it looks. And also... I've been thinking. You should snoop around in those labs."
"But that's putting her in danger!" Connor protested.
"Bratan - there's something there they don't want you to look at - so you need to take a look." Ivan countered.
Sparky nodded. "That flesh-monster would have only been the beginning."
Connor stroked Sparky's ears. "I... okay, I'll take a look. But I'm not going to let it risk hurting Chloe, understood?"
"Good man." Ivan nodded.

Back in Drackhill, Shannon let out a smile of relief as a pair of heroines came down the basement stairs.
"Good, you're both here." she said, gesturing vaguely at the chairs. "I've got something."
Amy dropped into a chair, stretching her legs out, while Gwen stood to attention.
Shannon pulled up a map of the city, several red dots pulsing across it. "Those thefts Doctor Rosenbaum flagged - the ones I've been tracing through the supply chain. I've finally got a pattern."
Gwen leaned in, Bubo perched on her shoulder. "Go on."
"The stolen goods aren't going to the Skinners' usual fences. They're being funnelled through a distribution network in Belson. Medical supplies, mostly - painkillers, anaesthetics, that sort of thing."
Her expression darkened. "The kind of stuff you'd need if you were running a lab. Or a factory."
Amy's jaw tightened. "Paladin."
"Maybe. Or maybe just Skinners looking to move in on the pharmaceutical trade." Shannon shrugged. "Either way, I've just picked up chatter on their frequencies. They're moving a shipment today that's our window."
Gwen's eyes lit up. "So we hit them."

"Two girls, one owl, and a gaggle of Skinner." Amy grinned. "I like those odds."
Gwen leaned forward. "What's the plan?"
"Follow the shipment. I've traced it to a warehouse near the old industrial canal. You go in, you find out where the goods are going. But don't just hit the first truck you see - if we can follow it to the source, we can take out the whole operation, not just tonight's delivery."
Amy rose, her gauntlets already humming with charge. Gwen stood too, Bubo flapping his wings.
"Let's roll." Amy nodded.
"One more thing." Shannon cut in. "I've got a present for you, Gwen."
She handed over a box, which Gwen opened.
"Ah... this is good." she grinned. It was a smooth ball, drilled down the middle and attached to a length of bungee cord.

Then she passed over another box.
"This bad boy, I call the 'Ka-boomerang!' Um... just 'boomerang' is probably better."

"Thanks." Gwen smiled. "You're the best armourer I've ever had."
Shannon chuckled. "Good luck, guys. Stay safe."

The rain had returned, as it always did in Drackhill. A mist coated the valley in a slick, grey sheen.
Amy and Gwen moved through the Belson backstreets, Bubo gliding silently above. The hum of traffic was distant here, muffled by the towering warehouses and shuttered shops.
"Alright, team, you're close." Shannon began over the comms.
Gwen stopped, pointing upward. "Bubo's seen something."
The owl swooped down, landing on a nearby fire escape. He turned his head, and let out a low hoot.
Gwen's eyes dimmed slightly as she linked with him. "Three guards on the perimeter, another five inside. All human, no Super-Agents."
"Good." Amy flexed her gauntlets. "I'll take point."
"Not yet. Let's see if we can't do this quietly first."
Gwen pulled up her cowl, and drew an arrow. "Bubo, watch the back."
The owl flapped away silently.

Amy crept forward, her suit's servos whining softly. She spotted the first guard leaning against a wall, cigarette in hand, staring at his phone. He didn't even hear her coming.
Her gauntlet clamped over his mouth, and before he could struggle, a controlled burst of electricity sent him slumping to the ground. She lowered him gently, and moved on.
The second guard was patrolling the perimeter fence. He turned at the last moment, eyes widening.
"Wha--"
*THUMP!*
Gwen grinned as the guard's eyes rolled back, and she caught the boomerang on its return.
Two down.
The third guard was near the main entrance. He was bigger than the others, built like a brick wall, with a shaved head that reflected the daylight. He didn't look surprised when Amy stepped out of the shadows.
"Oi." he grunted. "You're that Stunlocke bird, ain't ya?"
"Maybe." Amy grinned under her visor. "You gonna come quietly?"
The guard laughed. "I'm gonna enjoy this."
He charged, moving faster than his bulk suggested. Amy sidestepped, driving a gauntlet into his ribs. The shock should have dropped him - instead, he grunted, twisted, and caught her arm.
"Nice trick." he snarled, yanking her forward. "But I've had worse from a toaster."
Amy headbutted him, her helm connecting with his nose. He staggered back, blood streaming, and she followed with a kick to the knee that buckled his leg.
Then, the Swingshot came out from the dark, smacking into the back of his head. He stumbled forwards - right into a cerametallic knuckle.
Amy rushed over, and ripped off his jacket - there. Some kind of skintight bodysuit, with web-like seams running over it.
"Ah." Gwen nodded, pressing her fingers to her ear. "Jinx, this is Whiplash. We've found something strange - I'm sending you a picture now."

"Where's the shipment?"
He spat blood at her feet. "You ain't the Werewolf, love. I'm not telling you nuffin'."
Amy tightened her grip, the electricity humming. "I can keep doing this until your heart gives out. Then, I'll resuscitate you, and do it again. Start. Talking."
The colour drained from the guard's face. "Okay, okay!" he gasped. "It's at the old depot on Mercer Street! They're moving it tonight, to a lab somewhere! I don't know where!"
Amy released him, letting him slump to the ground. A strike to the head knocked him out cold.
"That was... effective." Gwen noted.
Amy grinned. "Let's move."

Amy and Gwen crouched on a rooftop, watching as a group of Skinners loaded crates into a truck.
