📖 Interactive Readers

Read along with narration, save your place, and pick up where you left off.

This site hosts interactive readers for original stories. Each reader parses the story into volumes, episodes, and scenes; lets you read at your own pace; and narrates the text aloud using the voices installed on your device.

Your reading position, speed, voice, and other preferences are stored locally in your browser — per story — so switching between readers won't lose your place in either one.

📖 Bloodhound

A World Half Full

Connor Barrett was just a boy from the wrong side of Drackhill when they shot him in the head.

He woke up in a torture chamber, rebuilt into something no longer human.

Stronger.

Faster.

Deadlier.

Now he's the city's most feared vigilante - a red-eyed beast, tearing through fascist gangs, corrupt cops, and the shadowy cabal pulling their strings. He's all that stands between Drackhill's demons and the people he loves - with a talking wolf, a crew of misfits, and brutal, blood-soaked salvation.

As the bodies pile up and the city burns, Connor must grapple with the cost of his crusade: the friends he's lying to, the people he can't stop hurting, and the men who would be gods.

A visceral, profane, and surprisingly tender superhero epic for adults - where the action is brutal, the philosophy is sharp, and the found family is everything.

The old world is dying.

The new world struggles to be born.

Now is the time…
