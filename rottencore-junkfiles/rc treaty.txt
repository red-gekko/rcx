
Protectorate terms

1. Humanity ceases all military action against both the aliens and each other. All militaries are to submit themselves to the control of alien-appointed representatives
2. Humanity commits to building no independent space fleet, weapons of mass destruction, or other independent military assets that could pose a threat to the alien government, its people, or to humanity. Any such weapons in existence must be surrendered to the aliens.
3. Humanity commits to peaceful engagement within the wider galactic community. We renounce war as a sovereign right of the government. In other words, the right of belligerency of the state will not be recognised. 
4. Humanity commits to active engagement within the wider galactic community. We shall adopt the alien legal code globally, normalise relations with the aliens, and commit to a free trade agreement within their bloc.
5. All political and military leaders who have called for, engaged in, or are otherwise involved with military action against the aliens are to submit themselves to arrest and trial within the alien legal system.
6. To ensure humanity does not threaten the alien races in this way again, orbital weapons platforms will be placed in low Earth orbit carrying sufficient firepower to suppress any possible human resistance. These weapons platforms will be placed under the control of the aliens or their appointed human representatives. These weapons have been described to us as carrier stations containing thousands of combat drones apiece, which can deploy to any combat theatre on Earth in less than half an hour. Such weapons will either be decomissioned or relinquished to humanity's control if humanity proves itself to be trustworthy.
7. All Earth nations relenquish their authority to the United Nations, which will be reorganised as a global administration. The specifics of this governance is to be determined by the people of the United Nations, but we have been informed that this government's democratic character is non-negotiable
8. The aliens commit to protection of humanity, recognising them as a "Council-compliant species" and entitled to the full protection of alien law.
9. Humanity will make no law or otherwise restrict or prohibit immigration of Council-compliant species. Likewise, humanity will make no law or otherwise restrict or prohibit emigration of humans to Council-compliant planets.
10. The aliens will provide us with advanced technology, and begin medical research to cure our greatest woes.
11. All mineral and other resource rights in the Solar System, including but not limited to mining of the asteroid belts and the Oort cloud, are hereby recognised as under the sovereignty of the United Nations of Earth's federal government. Likewise, any colonies, habitats, or other settlements established on non-Earth locations within the Solar System are recognised as under the sovereignty of the UNE.
12. Upon full integration into the galactic community, the UNE will commit to paying its taxes and dues proportional to its economic output. It confirms it will support the alien's foreign policy, and will not support any rival governments within the galactic community, nor shall it seek to make war upon or incite rebellion against the aliens.

In effect, the United Nations of Earth rules the planet as a quasi-independent vassal of the aliens.

Supremacy terms

1. Immediate cessation of hostilities between aliens and humans.
2. Mutual diplomatic relations established. Humanity recognised as "Council-compliant species."
3. Sovereignty and resource rights of the Solar System and nearby systems recognised as belonging to humanity.
4. The aliens surrender all material assets within the Solar System. They make no attempt to sabotage, damage, destroy, decomission, or otherwise inhibit the study and reverse-engineering of these assets.
5. The aliens recognise humanity as an independant, sovereign race, and commit to make no efforts to challenge this.

In effect, the aliens must capitulate and allow Earth to ascend to near-equal status with them.
