SCENE - Project Crusader

	Autumnal evenings in Drackhill were often grim, and tonight's, it seemed, would be no different.
	The sky gods had clearly decided to add some variety to the valley's night - you could almost feel their laughter as hail crashed down from the heavens.
	Despite this, the city still needed its monstrous heroes. No rest for the virtuous, it seemed.

	"Two options, I guess." Connor began. "Either they're Skinners, or former British soldiers themselves."
	"God knows." Ivan sighed, before placing down some C4 charges. The trio cleared the room, and Ivan pulled his detonator.

	Immediately, the post went up in smoke, before the flames died down. The smell of burning mutant blood filled the air, and the two Werewolves grimaced.
	"Good work tonight, you two." Ivan smiled. "We'd better scram, before the police show--"

	The Agent snarled, and drew something from a sheathe on his hip - a blade. As Connor entered melee range, he swung.
	Connor's senses slowed to a crawl as the agony ripped into his arm. This wasn't a normal blade - its edge oscillated at ultrasonic frequency, sawing into his flesh. At the same time, an electric current coursed through it, which danced across his skin before jolting in, sending scorching pain straight into his neurons.

	A train. A high-speed train, zooming over the railway.
	The Crusader picked up the pace, as did Connor. The pair arrived at the railway just as the train crossed paths.
	"So long, traitor!" he roared, jumping a few feet in the air. He pressed his boot against the side of the train, and it made a clamping noise as electromagnets in its soles switched on. He clambered to the roof of the train.
	For a second, the Crusader caught his breath, amazed to be the first of his kindred to survive an encounter with the famed Werewolf. His heart sank, however, as he heard the pounding of metal across the carriage from him.
	Connor gripped onto the train's roof, and snarled viciously.
	The Crusader scowled, and redrew his blade - it crackled in the air as its teeth began to spin once again.
	"Come on then, traitor. Deus Vult."
	"Shut the fuck up." Connor spat into the air, before chitinous blades formed at his wrists. He morphed buckler shields from his forearms, and leapt forward, the momentum of the train launching him towards his prey.

	The clambering of the two combatants shook the train's passengers from their boredom.
	"What's that?" one child asked her mother.
	"It's... it's probably nothing." she replied, trying to look out the window.
	Then, an stun-sword cut through the metal ceiling, before tearing a gash into the train's hull as it ripped back upwards, flinging sparks into Connor's eyes. The passengers screamed in shock and fear.

	Connor stumbled backwards, blinded and dazed. He regained his senses to the sound of a signal bell clanging in the air. He turned around just in time to see a metal flag slam right into him, shooting him backwards - right into the Crusader's blade. The vibrating metal cut through his abdomen, impaling him.
	A normal man would've died. Hell, an abnormal man would've died.
	Connor merely snarled in animalistic fury. He spotted an upcoming tunnel, and knew exactly what to do.
	He wrapped the Crusader into a bear hug, jumped a foot in the air, and dived back down - right into where the blade had ripped into the train moments before.

	He hated this move. But desperate times...
	*HAWK-ptoo!*
	A globule of acid painted the Crusader's faceplate. It sizzled and hissed, as the metal deformed.
	He gave out a raspy breath, before firing up his EMP.
	Connor arched backwards, convulsing violently, as passengers screamed in pure terror. The train's brakes slammed on, and Connor flew backwards, hitting the intercarriage doors. The blade, poking through Connor's back, stabbed into the door, before dislodging. The Crusader ripped off his helm, took a breath, sauntered forward, and grabbed the blade, smiling viciously.
	The train ground to a halt, and the passengers poured out like bees from a burning hive.
	The Crusader slashed his blade forward, cutting across Connor's chest. He weakly rose his arms, and his bucklers shattered on impact.
	His mind ran at a mile a microsecond. He had to do something. Now. His eyes darted around, hoping for some kind of idea.
	And then, he lit up.
	"Oh God, please don't kill me!" he screamed, leaping from the train.
	The Crusader grinned, hopping down. "I'm sorry, traitor? Could you say that again?"
	"I'll do whatever you say, just please... please don't kill me..." Connor begged, falling to his knees.
	"Oh, traitor..." the Crusader snarled, raising his blade. "Be thankful I'm only here to kill *you.*"
	With that, Connor pounced forward, wrapping his arms around the Crusader's waist. He felt the blade rake across his back, electricity and metal cutting into him - but he didn't break. Instead, he threw the Crusader as far as he could.
	The Crusader sailed through the air, his blade falling from his hand as he crashed into a metal grate fence. He came to an abrupt halt, smacking into a metallic structure that hummed ominously.
	As he looked around, his breathing quickened with fear.
	Connor grinned, picking up the blade and looking on at the electrical substation his assailant laid upon. He gripped the hilt hard, and threw.
	The blade spun forwards, and embedded itself through the Crusader's gut and into the metal behind him. Its current surged forwards, and then...
	*BOOM!*
	For a brief moment, Connor was blinded by the brilliant light. The blazing inferno was lit by roaring blue sparks, and the stench of burning flesh filled his nostrils. He rushed to the train, and grabbed a fire extinguisher. Pushing on through his exhaustion, he quickly put out the flame.
	He sank back down to his knees, clinging desperately to consciousness. The distant screams of the passengers echoed in the night, as the sound of a roaring quadbike approached.
	Connor sighed, and readied his fists, only to relax at a familiar scent.
	"Chyort voz'mi, tovarishch..." Ivan gasped, impressed. "You don't do half measures, do you?"
	"Grab his body." he wearily replied. "Get it back to your bosses... reverse engineer it or some shit. Find out a way to stop these things without..." he coughed, before spitting up more blood. "...without me having to cause a power cut."
	"Good idea, bratan." Ivan nodded, ripping the blade from the wreckage and hoisting up their latest kill. Sparky trotted over, and licked Connor's hand.
	He smiled warmly, and un-morphed his mask, rubbing his jaw.
	"Hurts, eh?" Ivan asked, with an almost fatherly concern. "I'm sure we can find a good dentist..."
	"It's not that... it feels like they're growing back. Like I'm teething." Connor shuddered.
	"You can grow back teeth?" Ivan cocked an eyebrow.
	"Oh, that's normal. All my teeth fell out when I was a pup, but they grew back pretty quickly!" Sparky chimed in.
	"I, uh... I think that's different." Connor chuckled, before turning. "That, and I just washed them with acid."
	"Ah. That sounds painful."

	"I can. It's not fun." Sparky yapped.
