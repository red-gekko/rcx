STATS:

	Physicals:
		AGI: Agility
			Affects dodge chance, hit chance, stealth skills, movement speed, etc
		STR: Strength
			Affects melee damage, lift capacity, intimidation, etc
		END: Endurance
			Affects how long a character can act before tiring out, etc
		TGH: Toughness
			Affects incoming damage, etc
		CST: Constitution
			Affects tox resistance, immunity, health regen, etc
	Mentals:
		INT: Logical thinking skills
			Affects skill learning, deduction, memory, etc
		SOC: Social skills
			Affects charm, leadership, group cohesion, etc
		PSI: Psionic affinity
			Affects psionic power, Focus generation, psionic experience gain, etc
		WIL: Willpower
			Affects morale, psionic resistance, etc
		PCN: Perception
			Affects detection, analysis, deduction, etc
	
	LCK: Luck
		Affects chances of success, critical success, and even allows occaisional rerolls

Homeworld:
	Planet Type:
		Predation World
			A planet with significantly dangerous local wildlife. Locals had to be strong to survive.
			+2 to all physical stats. +2 to PCN. +1 to WIL and INT. -4 to SOC. +1 to Fieldcraft, Melee, and Ranged
		Hab World
			Not a planet, but an orbital or interstellar habitat. Locals are good with technology, but physically weak.
			+2 to all mental stats. In addition, +2 to INT and PSI, and +1 to SOC. -1 to all Physical stats. +1 to Charm, Crafting, and Driving
		City World
			A planet with near-complete urban coverage, these worlds have populations in the hundreds of billions. Locals are used to managing big groups, and familiar with advanced tech.
			+5 to SOC. +1 to 5 random stats. +2 to Charm, +1 to Driving
		Agri-World
			A planet dedicated almost entirely to food production, almost all citizens of these worlds have at least basic farmhand experience. Outside of farming, technology on these worlds is fairly limited.
			+3 to SOC. +2 to END. +1 to 5 random stats. +2 to Farming, +1 to Fieldcraft
		Industrial World
			A planet dedicated to mining mineral and chemical resources, and/or processing them. All citizens either work in the quarries, refineries, warehouses, or factories. Black smog cloaks the sky.
			+2 to all Physical stats. In addition, +5 to CST. +2 to Crafting, +1 to Driving
		Fortress World
			A planet dedicated to military service and training. All citizens must at least undergo basic military service.
			+2 to all Physical stats. +1 to all Mental stats. +2 to Ranged, +1 to either Fieldcrafting, Melee, or Driving
		Dead World
			A planet that suffered from either major pollution or nuclear war. Toxic waste is everywhere, and locals struggle to survive.
			+1 to all stats. +5 to CST. -5 to SOC. +3 to Fieldcraft
		Tribal World
			A planet at a tribal stage of development. Locals typically live in small communes, or in early settlements. A small Imperial Outpost is maintained in orbit.
			+1 to all physical stats. +3 to SOC. +1 to 2 random stats. +3 to Fieldcraft
		Resort World
			A rich planet, where all necessities are guaranteed and locals are free to engage in pursuits of the mind.
			+3 to all mental stats. -1 to all physical stats. +1 to Logic, Artistic, and Charm
		Void World
			A planet based partially in the void, being saturated with psionic energy.
			+10 to PSI. -1 to all physical stats. +3 to Psychic Discipline
