EPISODE - Snowfall
============================================================

The sun was only just starting to peek over the horizon. It had snowed in the night, and the garden was frosted over. The light glistened off of the snow, but the air was so cold, it wouldn't even dream of melting.
Connor awoke earlier than normal. He looked over, and saw Trey snoring slightly. He chuckled, morphed up some soft, warm clothes, and went downstairs.
He saw Sparky curled up in Molly's dog bed, cuddling her. The two dogs awoke on his approach.
"Morning, Connor!" Sparky smiled. "I think Molly needs toilet."
Connor smiled back, and let both dogs out. Sparky rolled in the snow.
"Ahh, I love it." he cheered. "It's like ice cream is falling from the sky."
Connor chuckled, endeared. "It's a lot more than usual, too. Haven't seen this much snow since the op in Chechnya."
"It's way better like this. Having a tank shooting at you is very distracting." Sparky nodded, making the closest thing a wolf could make to a snow-angel.
Connor grinned, and took a deep breath through his nose, letting the cold air fill his lungs. Then, he felt a pair of arms wrap around him.
He turned around, and saw Chloe, wrapped up in a onesie. She hugged him properly. He melted into the embrace.
"Morning." she said, half asleep.
"Good morning." he replied, smiling warmly. He kissed her forehead, and she nuzzled into his chest.
"I was just gonna let the dogs out... but looks like you beat me to it." she said, before crouching and giving Molly a cuddle.
"Anything to get away from Trey's snoring." Connor quipped.
She chuckled. "Oh, I know your metabolism has plenty of drawbacks... but on freezing mornings like this, it's nice to have a boyfriend with such a high body temperature."
Connor grinned. "You know, you can just say I'm *hot.*"
She laughed, and playfully whacked his arm. "I know you're a superhero and all, but are the quips necessary?"
"Vital." Connor snarked. "Just imagine how much worse Trey would be."
"Oh, we'd have to just write a comic book about him, at that point." she laughed.
"Morning, Chloe!" Sparky said, trotting over.
"Haha, morning Sparky!" she replied, stroking his ears. "Did you sleep okay?"
"Yeah, I slept well thanks! I kept Molly warm all night." he chatted back.
Chloe beamed. She knelt down, and gave Molly a cuddle.
"Hey, my sweet girl. Who's a good girl? Whosagoodgirrrl?"
Molly replied by licking Chloe, climbing up and wrapping her front legs over her shoulders.
Connor smiled warmly, and knelt down by Sparky. "Whosagoodboyyy?" he went, mimicking.
"I am. Duh." Sparky grinned, winking.
"Oh, I could throttle you sometimes." Connor laughed.
"No, you couldn't. You'd feel terrible."
"...yeah, fair point." Connor smirked, then wrapped Sparky into an embrace.
"Ah! No! I'm a big cool wolf! Not a blanket!" he faux-protested, as Connor tackled him to the ground.
"Aww, is someone trying to pretend he doesn't enjoy cuddles?" Connor giggled.
"I'm almost a year old! I... yeah, I enjoy cuddles." he finally admitted.
Chloe grinned giddily, and Molly wagged her tail in amusement.
Connor rose to his feet. "So... what do you wanna do today? We don't have to head back until late."
"Well, Bea and I were talking..." she began. "You guys were so lovely, showing us all around Drackhill - maybe it's time we showed you around our home town?"
"That'd be really sweet." Connor smiled. "After that, I'll take you to go see your grandad, if you like?"
"Oh, yes please." Chloe nodded quickly. "I know Sveta and dad said he's fine, but..."
"Can't hurt to check." Connor shrugged. He hesitated.
"If he's not... I've still got the super-serum in my car. Or, you know, in my bloodstream. If, big if, you want me to..."
"No." she shook her head. "Not unless he's on the brink of death. Besides, we have no idea what it would do when used on an old person... let alone one with Dementia."
"Shit, that's a good point..." Connor nodded. "The Rages are bad enough even when you've got your faculties."
"Exactly. Anyway, you hungry?"
"I'm always hungry." he grinned.

The smell of hot, sizzling bacon acted as something of a summoning spell. In less than ten minutes, the entire group had filtered their way downstairs.
"Thank god I made so much..." Chloe chuckled to herself.
"Mmm... bacon..." Trey groaned happily, like a zombie who'd just broken into a room full of brains.
Chloe finished cooking, and Connor plated up the sandwiches. The gang all devoured their food like hungry dogs. Except for the actual dogs, who had long since inhaled their food and were now trying their luck at begging for scraps.
"Right..." Chloe began. "Let's all get dressed quickly, and we can get out of here before the grown-ups get up."
Shannon laughed heartily. "Woo, no supervision!"
Connor flashed his eyes bright red. "Did someone say... super-vision?"

Amy gestured at her fluffy pyjamas and fur boots. "There you go - look how fast I got ready."

At this point, Trey had subtly sneaked to the back of the group, along with Shannon.
"Oi..." he whispered. "Watch this."
He made a snowball, and launched it, aiming right for the back of Connor's head. Without skipping a beat or even turning, Connor caught it in his hand.
"Next time, don't say your plan aloud near the guy with super-hearing." he grinned.

"Fly?" Bea asked.

"Yeah, but only Gwen can actually *fly.* Honestly, it's unfair that she gets a free powerup and we're stuck with the same old shit."

Bea looked at them all, incredulously.
"Um... read minds?"

She pulled her hand back, and playfully smacked him.
"No, but... what did you actually see?" she asked.

Minor annoyance mixed with amusement as I started joking. Also, you're uncomfortably cold - but you don't want to make a scene, so you haven't said anything."
She blushed, scratching the back of her head. "That's... very interesting."
He smiled, and took off his jumper, morphing it into a scarf.
"Take it. It's fresh, so it's right at my body temperature."
She hesitated, but smiled, and took it.
"Alright then, what am I thinking about right now?" Amy laughed, grabbing his hand.
"A brontosaurus." Connor chuckled. "Oh - I just caught that. You were quickly trying to think of a number, but instead thought of the letter A."
"Hey, Connor?" Chloe called out.
Connor turned his head, and a snowball hit right into his face.
"Sorry. Couldn't resist." she grinned, kissing him.
"I have super-reflexes, too." he chuckled. "I just didn't expect such a betrayal."
Chloe pouted, and he laughed.
Bea's grin returned. "Smoke bombs? That's gotta be fake."
"Oh no, I can do those." he smirked, as his glove hissed into black fog. "Real bombs too. But I won't do that now, because it's a lot of effort. Also a bomb. That's where I get my bullets from, too - it's all down to air pressure. I can even use it to augment my strikes - like creating a blast at my elbow to increase momentum, or a blast from my fist for extra impact."
"Okay, I get it." she laughed. "Next you'll tell me you can spit acid, or something."
"...yeah, I can do that too."
"Ew!" she grimaced.
"...for the record, I refuse to kiss you for at least 12 hours after doing that." Chloe quipped.
"I'd make it 24. 12 if I get some mouthwash."

The walk ended near the old clock tower. Chloe turned backwards to face the group.
"So, that's Marlingham." she grinned.

"Yeah, that's a common thread. Hence all the old farts here." Bea chuckled.
At that, Sparky stopped in his tracks.
"What's wrong, buddy?" asked Amy.
Connor sniffed the air, and he too stopped. He walked forward, following his nose.
"What is it? Is it Super-Agents?" Chloe asked, concerned.
"No, it's just... you smell that too, right bud?" Connor asked.
"Yeah. I can't place it, but it smells familiar." Sparky nodded.
"Him, over there." Connor motioned quietly at a man across the street. He wore thick clothes and had his hood up, obscuring his features.
"Human." Sparky confirmed.
The man looked up, and pulled his head down. He grinned devilishly.
"Hello Bea." he said.
"Charlie..." Bea whispered, pale as a ghost.

Sparky immediately pounced, slamming right into him. He roared right into his face.
"I WARNED YOU! I SAID I'D KILL YOU!" he barked savagely, saliva dropping from his jowls. He was very, *very* careful to not let Charlie hear his words.

"Sparky!" Connor shouted. "Nothing permanent. We wouldn't want him squealing before his trial."
Sparky grinned savagely, nodded, and bit Charlie's hood, dragging him away into an alley. He began hitting Charlie with his paws, doing his best to avoid cutting him. Still, Charlie cried in pain as beat after beat rained down on him.
"You okay?" Chloe asked, hugging Bea.
"Yeah, I'm fine, just... didn't expect that. Not at all." Bea shuddered, shellshocked.
Connor walked over, and beckoned to Sparky. He hesitated, but returned to Connor's heel.
Charlie stood, bruised and trembling. "I'm going to have that thing put down."
Connor calmly walked over, cornering him. "When your trial comes, plead guilty. Saves Bea having to relive the trauma."
"I didn't do anything wrong." Charlie snarled.
Connor grabbed the hem of Charlie's coat, and pushed him against the wall.
"You don't seem to get it, do you? Do what I just told you to do. Otherwise... well, I can't guarantee your safety. There's wolves about."
Sparky barked viciously. "He's giving you a chance. Take it, or don't. Actually... don't. I'm *very* hungry."
Connor grinned - not happily, or amused. He grinned like a crocodile about to devour its prey.
"You're living with your family again, right? Think how sad your mum'll be when she sees your face on the newspaper. I wonder what the headlines will say? 'Local cunt found ripped apart.' 'Urgent appeal: abusive bastard's kneecaps missing.' 'Throat slit: Much rejoicing.'"

"Oh, believe me, that's the only reason you're still breathing." Connor growled savagely. "Go on, just give me a reason to stop listening to her. I dare you."
Charlie gulped. Connor released his grip, and he and Sparky rejoined the group.
"You're getting better at controlling your speech." Connor noted. "I don't think he heard a word."
"He got the gist." Sparky growled lowly. They arrived to the others.
"He bothers you again, he won't regret it. He won't have enough time to." Sparky smiled, licking Bea's hand.
"Thanks." Bea nodded, forcing a smile. "Come on, let's get out of here. I wanna see my parents before we go back home."

"We'll call you." Amy nodded. "Besides, Bea won't mind."
The group formed another collective hug, and Connor and Chloe went into his car, as the remainers headed into the fields.

Bea walked into their spacious living room, and took another deep breath.
"Bea? Is that you, sweetie?" her mum, Anya, called down from upstairs.
"Hi!" she called back, smiling slightly. Immediately, the whole family rushed down the stairs.
She smiled warmly as her parents embraced her, clutching them back and letting the moment hang. Once they finally released, Tea practically barged past them to hug her big sister.
"Hey, Tea." she grinned, hugging back.
"Hey, Bea." Tea smiled warmly.

"For a little while. It's a school night, but we've not gotta leave yet." Bea nodded, sitting down. They had a big circular sofa, which they used for such 'family meetings.'
"So, how's uni? Enjoying it? Do you still hang out with those friends who picked you up after Christmas?" Patrick smiled.

"W-when did this happen? And how long for?" Anya asked, tears now forming in her eyes.
"Um... well, I didn't realise it, but... he'd been gaslighting me for a little while. But it didn't all kick off until early last month." Bea explained.
"Oh god..." Tea cried. "Why didn't you tell us?"
"I just... I wanted to wait until I'd seen you all. I..." she began, tears streaming down. "I didn't wanna do it over the phone."
Her family immediately embraced her. She let the feelings wash over her, like a crashing wave of pain and relief.
There wasn't much to say. It was wrong. It shouldn't've happened.
But it did.

Arriving at the orthopaedic ward of Marlingham Royal Hospital, Chloe paused for a moment.
"What's wrong?" asked Connor.
"Just... what if this makes his dementia worse? What if he doesn't recognise me, and that upsets him? Should I be here?" she asked, worried.
"Hey..." Connor cut in gently. "He's your grandad. He's ill, and you're nearby. Of course you should be here. And if he gets confused, we'll help him through it."
She smiled, and pulled his hand from her shoulder to her cheek, before they headed in.
Oliver was half-asleep on his hospital bed.
"Hey, grandad..." she began softly. He opened his eyes - the cataracts had gotten worse, and the sclera had bluish and yellowish tints to them.
"Is that you, Sylvia?" he asked, trembling.
