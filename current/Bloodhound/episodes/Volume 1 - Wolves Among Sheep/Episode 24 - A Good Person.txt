VOLUME - Wolves Among Sheep
EPISODE - A Good Person
============================================================

November 2011. Snow on the ground. And for the first time in a decade, Connor had almost forgotten what day it was. Almost.
A group of boys approached him - Trey among them.
"What's up, Barrett?" one of the boys asked, putting his hand on Connor's shoulder. He tensed.
"Nothing. Please don't touch me." Connor replied bluntly.
"Please don't touch meee." the boy said mockingly, tightening his grip.
Connor stood, and smacked the boy's hand away. "Get. Off." he said firmly.
"Or what?" another boy growled, shoving Connor. Connor slipped and his backpack fell to the ground. The boys laughed.
Connor stood up, red-faced. Trey grabbed Connor's backpack and started rifling through it.
"Ooh, what's this?" Trey grinned menacingly.
"Stop that! It's mine!" Connor protested as the boys held him back.
Trey laughed, and tore up some coursework Connor had been working on.
At this, Connor snapped, and broke free, before punching Trey to the ground. Trey rose back up, and the two boys began fighting.
Both boys were clearly stronger than they looked. The other boys tried to split them up, but were tossed aside by the two berserker teens.
Finally, a group of teachers came running over. With considerable effort, both were dragged away from each other.

Half an hour had passed, and both boys sat in the detention room in school. The teacher meant to be supervising them had simply left and locked the door. Both boys, now bruised, sat on opposite ends of the classroom. Both maintained permanent scowls on their faces, and kept their arms folded.
This silence carried on for a good while - longer than most 15-year-olds could've managed. Finally, Connor took a deep breath.
"This is all your fault." he growled.
"Me?!" Trey snapped. "You're the one who went all psycho at me!"
"You're the one who ruined my homework!"
"Well, you're a weirdo!"
"Fuck you!"
Both boys were standing, ready to go for another round. Connor took a deep breath, and sat back down.
"I'm sorry." he said through gritted teeth. "Actually, no, I'm not. Today's a really shitty day, and you made it 10 times worse."
"Oh, boo hoo. The weirdo had a rough day." Trey mocked.
"Shut up!" Connor snarled. "It's Bloody Sunday, if you've gotta know. The tenth one, as well."
"It's bloody Friday, you idiot." Trey scoffed.
"I mean the one in 2001! Oh, whatever. Not like you'd get it, anyway." Connor rolled his eyes.
"What's that supposed to mean?!" Trey slammed his hands on the desk. "You think I'm stupid or something?!"
"Yeah." Connor grinned.
Trey went to be enraged, but instead, he simply chuckled.
"Ugh... look. I'm sorry I ruined your coursework. And I'm sorry I ruined your stupid holiday."
"It's not a holiday." Connor said curtly. "Ugh, I'm fuckin' sick of this shite. I'm sick of living next to you people!"
"The fuck do you mean?" Trey snapped back, offended. "Oh, what, you're a racist now?"
"Fuck off! I mean the English! None of yous even acknowledge what happened! What yous took from me! *WHO* yous took from me!"
Connor's anguished scream echoed in the room. It went deathly silent, and Connor tried - and failed - to regain his composure.
Trey covertly searched "Bloody Sunday 2001" on his smartphone while Connor rocked back and forth. He saw a brief article detailing it - the opening shots of the Bogside War.
He walked over, tentatively.
"Hey, I... I'm sorry that happened to you guys. I didn't know any of that - it's not like they teach it here."
Connor looked over, his eyes bloodshot from silent tears.
"I don't mean *you.*" Connor finally admitted. "I just mean... I'm tired of it. I don't fit in here, everyone calls me a weirdo, I'm just... I'm done."
"Hey..." Trey sighed. "I'm sorry for calling you a weirdo. I was just going along with the guys, that's all."
"Why?" Connor asked.
Trey tried for a good while to think of a decent excuse. He couldn't, so he just sighed again.
"I shouldn't've. I guess... if we're picking on you, then they're not picking on me. And yeah, I know, that doesn't make it okay. Look, I know what it's like to be judged by other people, if it helps." he began.
"How?"
"I'm black, you weir---sorry. I meant 'idiot.'"
They shared a chuckle.
"I'm sorry, too. I overreacted. My uncle always says I've got a bad temper."
"Eh, I gave as good as I got. You're lucky those teachers split us up."
"Yeah, right. If they hadn't come, I'd've thrashed you."
"Sure thing. Keep telling yourself that."
The boys tried to maintain this rough charade, but both cracked, and began to laugh. Once the laughter died down, Trey spoke again.
"You said 'who' we took from you. What did you mean?"
"My grandparents." Connor sighed. "Car bomb."
Trey gasped. "Oh, shit, I... I'm sorry."
"Stop saying that." Connor shook his head. "It's not your fault."
"I mean, I'm sorry for bullying you." Trey sighed. "I just... I'm lonely too. Those guys are the only ones who let me hang out with them."
"You're better off just being a weirdo." Connor replied, tilting his head.
Trey chuckled softly. The two were sitting at the same table now, and their postures were more open.
"I wish my grandparents were dead." Trey began. "My mum's side, that is."
"Why?"
"They blame me for her death." Trey explained. "I don't know why."
"My mum died too." Connor noted sombrely. "In childbirth. They called it the--"
"--the Outbreak." Trey finished, his eyes widening. "*My* mum died from that. Giving birth to *me.*"
"What?" Connor said, stunned.
"Wait...You're saying the same thing that killed my mum..."
"...killed mine?"
"No way." Connor shook his head. "I mean, sure, our year in school is small - but me and Amy were the only ones who survived."
"Amy? Amy Zhao?" Trey tilted his head.
"Yeah. She's my foster-sister."
Trey stroked his chin pensively. "Everyone else in our year had luckier mums than me - that's what we were told. It didn't hit everyone - only 60%."
"Which is why our year's so small. Because half the kids who would've been born were stillbirths."
"But that's the thing..." Trey rubbed his temples. "The Outbreak turned every pregnancy into a stillbirth, right? And it always hurt the mothers, too. They never figured out why I survived - they told my dad it was a mystery, and since I was the only one, they couldn't compare me to anyone else."
"And since I moved to Derry as a baby..." Connor nodded. "No-one ever realised there were more of us."
"Hang on, let me just get this straight - they lied to us?"
"But why would they do that?"
"Beats me. I don't know how to find that out."
"Maybe I could ask my uncle? He's always been begging they open another inquest."
"So's my dad! Hey, maybe if they both joined together, they might be able to get people to listen?"
"And if we survived - who else did?" Connor pondered.
"Yeah! There's gotta be more kids just like us. So many people, who know exactly what it's like..." Trey smiled softly.

An hour later, the teacher arrived with Phil and Vince.
"What's this I hear about fighting?" Vince asked, arms folded.
"And you - I thought we'd spoken about you hanging out with--" Phil added, but both were cut off.
"Dad!" Trey beamed. "This is Connor - he's like me, too!"
"Vince!" Connor rose. "Trey's mum died in the Outbreak, too! Just like me and Amy!"
Both adults looked at each other, aghast.
"No, there's no way..." Phil shook his head. "They told me Trey was the only one who survived..."
"Yeah..." Vince gasped. "The bastards lied to us."
Connor and Trey revealed some notes they'd been making.
The adults looked at the kids, then to each other.
"I guess they're not fighting anymore." Vince shrugged.
"Sounds like they're mates." Phil shrugged back.
"Teenagers." Vince chuckled.

Morning came and bled into afternoon, which lulled into evening. The sun was slowly creeping back down the horizon, as Connor lazed around his new, makeshift home.
Then it came. Keys in the door.
Connor scanned it, and smiled warmly.
"Hey, bro!" he began cheerily. "Everything okay?"
"Hey." Trey began. "I'm not sure..."
"What's wrong?"
Trey took a deep breath. "We tell each other everything, right?"
"I... yeah, of course." Connor replied evasively.
Trey chuckled grimly. "No secrets between us?"
"Trey, what are you getting at?"
"Connor... are you the Werewolf?"
"What?" Connor said, taken aback... and aghast. "I, um... why would you ask that?"
"Are you, or aren't you?" Trey repeated himself.
Connor sighed. He tried to force his lips to lie, but he just... couldn't.
Trey gasped in shock. "You are..." he whispered.
"Trey... listen to me, I--" Connor began.
"You must think I'm some kind of idiot!" he snapped. "You go 'missing' for a week, and the day you come back just so happens to be the same day the Werewolf goes on a rampage? Then suddenly you and Tommy are working together again... and what, you thought I just wouldn't notice how you're always conveniently busy or working when the Werewolf strikes? Or how about last week, when the Werewolf never showed up until we conveniently couldn't see you anymore? Or last night, eh? How about last fuckin' night?!"
Connor sighed, and looked down. His red hoodie darkened and flexed, shifting into kevlar that covered him.
"Oh my god..." Trey stumbled backwards in shock.
His mask melted away. "Trey, wait. I'm still me."
"You... you never told me." he said, aghast.
"I'm sorry. I'm telling you now."
"Who else knows?"
"Amy. Tommy, too. I... I wanted to tell you, but..."
"But what? You can't trust me?!"
"No! It's just... I know you, Trey. You'll get yourself killed trying to follow me."
Trey looked down. "How long have you been like this? Have you always been this way?"
"No. It's only since I went missing - I'd been kidnapped, and... changed. According to Sparky, they were trying to brainwash me, too."
"Sparky?" Trey asked, bewildered.
Connor sighed. "Sparky can talk."
"The FUCK?!"
"Only to me. Anyway..."
"Whoa, whoa, whoa, back up! Don't just 'anyway' a talking dog!"
"It's a long story. He has the same powers I do, and for some reason that gives him human-level intelligence. It also means we have this weird... I dunno, psychic bond or something."
Trey slumped back, stunned. He crossed his arms, and sighed.
"You snitched on... on yourself? And you went on and on about how 'the Werewolf was no hero,' when all along you were talking about yourself! Was that some kind of sick game?!"
"No, God no!" Connor shook his head. "It... it was the truth. I failed you, the night you got jumped by the Skinners. As for the cops... that was a ruse. I had to shake them off my tail."
"You have a tail?"
"No, I... figure of speech. I can still make those."
"Don't talk to me like that! You don't get the right to! Last I checked, you didn't have fuckin' claws either, so don't you fucking DARE make me feel stupid for asking questions I don't have the answers to!"
Connor rubbed his temples. "You... you're right, I'm sorry. Listen, I... I hate the lie. And I wanted to tell you--not like this."
"When?" Trey pressed. "When were you gonna tell me?"
"Soon. I'm gonna tell everyone soon. Once I've figured out what they're planning, I can... I can be honest again."
Trey paused, then rose. "Connor..." he began. "Answer honestly. How many people have you killed? Can you... can you even remember?"
"I remember every single one." Connor replied grimly. "73. Mostly Super-Agents."
"Super-What?"
"The bulk of my enemies. They used to be humans, until a serum turned them into... people like me."
"And the normal people?"
"For some, I wasn't in control at the time." Connor said clinically. "They were attacking me, and I got carried away and killed them instead of just injuring them. Other than that, there was Omar Hattab, an Algerian would-be tyrant; and Alan Hughes - we spoke about him the day I killed him. I also took out a few of Hughes's men."
"Why? Why kill them?"
"Partly because they had it coming. Partly because I was paid to. And partly because they were in on the conspiracy."
"Conspiracy?"
"The Optimates." he answered grimly. "A cabal of rich people, trying to take over the world. They invented these... powers of mine. And they've used it to make an army."
"Fuck..." Trey whispered, shuddering at the thought.
Connor paused, the sadness evident on his face.
Trey looked up at him.
"You shot Charlie."
"Yeah, I... I kinda enjoyed that."
The two locked eyes. Then, they shared a quiet chuckle.
"Trey, I... I wanted to tell you. And I was gonna. I just... I wanted to make sure that the worst of it was over."
"That wasn't your choice to make."
Connor sat down, unable to reply. Then, he felt his earpiece beep.
"Sorry, one sec." he said awkwardly. "Verdict, what's up?"
"Bratan... we have a problem. A big problem."
"How big?"
"8 feet tall."
"Shit... where?"
"Just outside Angleport. Me and Sparky are on overwatch, but they'll be on the move soon."
"On my way." Connor nodded. He turned to Trey.
"Listen, there's... there's some mutants in Angleport. If I don't stop them now, they'll kill a lot of people. I... I have to go."
"Then go." Trey sighed, rising.
"Wait."
He hesitated, but led Trey into the basement, and unveiled his lockbox.
"Whoa..." Trey gasped in awe, looking at the Crusader-pattern powersuit. Connor moved it aside, and took out his laptop.
"This... is my secret." he began awkwardly. "On this computer is all the intel we have on... well, everything."
"Why are you showing me this?"
"To prove that I trust you. I have to go. But please - find the answers in there. And I'll answer any more questions when I get back."
Trey hesitated, but hugged his best friend. Connor returned the embrace, and took off into the night.

Ivan spied over the warehousing unit with a pair of electronic binoculars. Next to him, Sparky's burning red eyes scanned through.
They both felt the impact behind them, as Connor dropped from his glide onto their rooftop perch.
"Evening, tovarishch." Ivan nodded. "You see them?"
Connor's eyes flashed, and he grimaced.
"Fifteen Super-Agents, all armed. And the big fucker in the middle, guarding the serum."
"They're expecting us." Sparky noted. "They're on edge. If we strike now, we'll be overwhelmed."
"You're right..."
"Translation?" Ivan grinned.
"We can't attack head-on." Connor sighed. "There's too many of them. We need to take out the gunmen first, and then focus on the Jotunn."
"15 gunmen. That's 5 each." Ivan stroked his beard. "Then we can triple-team big guy."
"Speaking of..." Sparky began, focusing his eye. "The Jotunn has some kind of... 'fuzz' about him."
"That's electricity, isn't it?" Connor tilted his head. "He's got some tech on him."
"I don't like the sound of that..." Ivan grimaced. "So, how do you think we proceed?"
"You're the expert. We're just the blunt instruments." Connor shrugged. "Still... I think we can use surprise on our side."
Ivan nodded, pointing. "The roof is pretty weak. We should attack through there. I'll shoot from the rafters while you two jump down."
Sparky rose, stretching. "I'll take left, you'll take right."
"Sounds like a plan."
The three heroes pounced over to the roof, and threw their weight downwards. Ivan caught himself on the rafters, and hung upside down, pulling back Isabella's bolt.
"CONTACT!" a Super-Agent roared, as Connor landed down, flattening him. Shards of glass rained down, embedding into the bloody mess.
The Werewolves tore through the Agent ranks, sprinting as fast as possible while they planted blades into chests. Meanwhile, a hail of bullets popped into their skulls.
Connor caught a Super-Agent's rifle barrel, bent it upward, and drove the man's own bayonet through his throat. Beside him, Sparky ripped a leg from another Agent and used it to sweep a third off his feet. Ivan's rifle cracked twice. Two heads snapped back.
It was a hard push, but it worked. All Agents were down.
Connor turned to face the Jotunn, but before he could...
*SHH-SHIK!*
He looked down, and saw a blade poking from his abdomen. At first, it didn't hurt.
Then, he felt a surge. He convulsed in agony, throwing himself forward. He stumbled, and braced himself against a crate of serum, looking up.
The Jotunn rose, stretching out. It was clad in a powered exoskeleton, holding an oscillating blade.
Ivan's sniper rounds slammed into it, barely denting the metal. A large collar plate guarded its neck, offering it surprising mobility.
"Chyort! They gave it Crusader armour!" Ivan snarled. "Try to get me a clear shot!"
"On it!" Connor spat, as he and Sparky leapt forward.
Sparky kicked a small propane tank in the air. Connor grabbed it, and hurled it at their titanic foe. As it hit, Ivan's bullet crashed through, and a fiery shockwave staggered the beast.
The Werewolves didn't hesitate. The pair pounced, with Sparky hooking his claws underneath a chest plate, using his strength to pry it loose. Connor meanwhile focused his attention on the monster's sword-arm, aiming his strikes at a small gap by the elbow.
The Jotunn's free hand quickly darted to his gauntlet, and pressed a button. Then, the three convulsed in unison, clutching their heads in agony.
A horrific screaming noise pierced through their earpieces, as they howled. The Jotunn grinned sadistically underneath his helm, and sliced his assailants with his oscillating blade.
Ivan crashed down from his ceiling perch, scowling in pain. He stumbled to his feet, desperately fumbling in his ear - trying to reach the hijacked device.
"TURN IT OFF! TURN IT OFF!" Connor roared, as he scrambled away from the blade.
Sparky grabbed his collar, and tore it free. He raised his claws, and drove them into Connor's ear. Blood ripped out, but the agony subsided.
Ivan finally pulled his own out - blood running from his eardrum. His balance was gone, as he tried to aim into the carnage - wary of shooting his comrades.
The Jotunn responded by kicking Connor with enough force to smash him into a shelving unit. A number of heavy crates fell down, and Connor held on desperately to consciousness.
It raised its blade up, which hummed with power. Sparky barely dodged the first swing, as the second cut into his snout.
*BANG!*
Ivan was many things - but he was not a man who would let his comrades down. A career as a Commissar will do that.
He launched into the fray, Isabella's underbarrel shotgun in hand, and blasted the blade out of the Jotunn's hand. It spun outwards, the mechanism on the hilt sparking and failing, and it sank into the ground.
The Jotunn smirked beneath his helm, as Ivan looked up.
"Oh, blyat..." he sighed.
He felt the back of a cerametallic gauntlet slam into him, and he sailed through the air, coming to a sudden stop against a brick wall.
The Jotunn grabbed hold of Sparky, held him in a bear hug, and squeezed. Sparky's bones began to crack, but that was the least of his worries. Electricity coursed through his veins, as his fur stood to attention and he screamed in agony.

Trey leaned back from the computer, amazed. Everything made a hell of a lot more sense now. Then, he heard the jingling of keys, and darted upwards.
"Shit. Shit. Shit." he gasped, hurriedly putting the laptop back into the wardrobe. He closed the door, then walked into the garage, just as Amy did.
"Yo, dickhead. Are you--oh." she began. "Uh, hey man. Is Connor in?"
"Uh, he's busy." Trey shook his head.
"Okay... so you're just here on your own?"
He nodded, then sat down on the sofa. She joined him.
"So... what's wrong?"
"What do you mean?"
"You've got a look on your face. The kind you normally have when you're in a mopey mood." she teased.
He sighed. "Amy, I... I know."
"Know what?" Amy prodded cautiously.
"Not to mess with Connor on a full moon." he snarked.
She folded her arms. "Say it out loud."
"He's the Werewolf."
She sighed, and rubbed her temples. "He told you?"
"No." Trey whispered, his face turning grim. "I figured it out last night."
Amy nodded, her expression changing to a more caring one. "How are you taking it?"
"My best friend has killed 73 people. How do you think I'm taking it?" Trey snapped.
Amy folded her legs. "A lot worse than I took it, clearly."
"Yeah, well..." Trey began. "You already knew he had it in him."
"Fuck you." Amy snapped. "Don't you dare."
Trey sighed. "I'm sorry... I didn't mean it like that."
Amy relaxed. "I know. Just... sensitive subject."
Trey nodded sympathetically. "It's just... he's killed so many people."
"Fascists." Amy countered. "Super-powered fascists."
Trey went to speak, but struggled to think of the words.
"Ask yourself. If you could do what he does, would you not?" she pressed.
Trey sighed. "I... I probably would."
"Exactly. So why are you acting like this?"
"Because he lied! He hid this from me."
"Yeah, he did. Seems he was right to, if this is how you're taking it." Amy retorted.
"Hey, that's not fair." he snapped.
"Isn't it?" she countered. "You find out about this horrendous burden he has to carry, and your first thought is 'boo hoo, he lied to me.'"
"That is not how it is!" Trey argued. "He has this burden, yeah, and he never spoke to me about it! What kind of best friend am I, if he can't even come and talk to me about it?"
Amy sighed. "He was trying to protect you."
"Protect me? Or protect himself?"
"Maybe he's entitled to that?! Have you even considered that, just maybe, he enjoys being a human, and would prefer it if everyone saw him as one?" Amy snapped. "Maybe, when he spends time with his best friend, he just wants to be Connor, instead of the Werewolf?!"
Trey sighed deeply. "I... I know, but..."
"How long have you known Connor?"
"4 years." he answered.
"4 years. And in those 4 years, he's always been...?" she pressed.
"Honest. Caring." Trey replied. "...good."
"Exactly. 'Good.' Connor is a good man." she began. "So, I don't care that he lied to us, because I know in my heart that when he lied, he was doing it for a good reason. Was it correct? I don't know. But I do know that he wouldn't have done it if he didn't think it was right. He was trying to protect us, and if you can't see that, you don't know him as well as you think you do."
Trey sat there for a moment, deep in thought.
"I... you... you're right." he admitted. "I just... I needed to talk about it."
"No problem." Amy smiled softly. "I know it's hard, but... he's still Connor. The Werewolf is just an extension of that."
Trey nodded. "I guess it's not all bad... if I'm out on the pull, I can always use the 'My best mate is a superhero' card now." he chuckled.
Amy laughed. "You are so fucking lame."

"GET OFF ME!" Sparky roared, as lightning coursed through his veins.
Connor pushed off the last crate, aching and bleeding. He looked up, and it didn't matter anymore. His packmate needed him.
"DIE!" he screamed, lurching forward, his eyes aflame. He slammed into the titan's chest, with enough force to slightly dent its cerametallic carapace. Sparky used his remaining strength to break free.
The Jotunn's fists clenched in fury, and it wrapped them around Connor's neck.
If he could still breathe, he'd have roared in furious defiance. Instead, he rattled, as his limbs flailed at the monster holding him aloft.
Sparky desperately struck at its legs, each time fighting through the pain of the shocks.
Connor spat. He sputtered. He kicked and scowled and clung to life.
His eyes dimmed, and started to roll back.
His ear - the one not deafened by his earpiece - pricked up ever-so-slightly. It heard something his conscious mind did not.
*phit-SHIK!*
The Jotunn screamed in fury and pain, slamming Connor to the ground. He choked and looked up, watching as it plucked the obsidian-tipped arrow from its armpit.
*phit-SHIK!*
Another arrow sailed through the air, and swung up, striking a gap in the armour's back.
His eyes weakly moved to the side, and saw her. A figure clad in a black leather catsuit. She still wore her domino mask, but now with a hawkish, beaked cowl.
"Gwen?" he whispered incredulously.
She drew another arrow, and fired again.

The newscaster brought his finger down from his earpiece, and sighed.
"*Ahem.* We've just received a breaking news report of some kind of fight in Angleport. We, uh... we go now to our live correspondent. Zack, what's going on down there?"
"Thanks, Tim." Zack began, stepping to the camera's side to give a clearer view. "As you can see, the police have established a cordon around the warehouse. Gunshots were reported earlier this evening, but now the noise indicates some intense battle going on. We've heard crashes, explosions, and even some electrical sounds."
"What are the police doing?" Tim asked.
"Nothing, by the looks of it." Zack muttered. "It seems their plan is to let this pan out, and then move in and apprehend the survivors."
At that, a section of wall gave way, and the Jotunn's cerametallic gauntlet was caught by the camera as it swung forwards.
Zack cleared his throat, and ushered the cameraman to a safer spot.
Watching all this, Trey and Amy sat at the edge of the sofa, their hearts racing.

The arrow twisted in the air, curving on an invisible line and burying itself in a soft point - the Jotunn's elbow.
It roared, grabbing a crate and launching it at its assailant archer. She dropped her bow and placed her palms out, gently catching it and sending it careening into a wall by the side.
A distraction. The Jotunn closed the gap just as she turned to face it, and belly-flopped onto her.
She used her powers to soften the blow, but screamed out as she surged with electricity.
"GET AWAY FROM HER!" Connor howled, as he and Sparky slammed into the beast. They twitched as they made contact, but felt Gwen's force push them forwards.
It fell, and the three inhumans rose.
"Fancy seeing you here..." she sputtered.
"Its armour can electrify you on contact." Connor said firmly. "We need a different strategy."
"Look!" Sparky barked, motioning. "It looks less bright, now."
Connor's eyes flashed, and he nodded. "It's low on power. We need some way to finish it off, then make it vulnerable."
"We can grapple it." Sparky chimed in. "If we're both on it, it should run out faster."
Connor raised his fists. "Good idea. Gwen--uh, Whiplash - we'll take the brunt of it. Once it's down, kill it."
She looked down at Sparky in shock, but shook her head. "Got it, Werewolf."

The two pounced forward, wrapping themselves over the Jotunn. Immediately, agony flowed through their bodies, and they forced themselves to hold on.
It burned. It ripped. It tore. Every neuron was ablaze. The Jotunn tried to slam into a wall, but felt the air thicken and resist its push.
Gwen's hands tremored as she forced the beast to stay still. Inside, she cursed herself for not studying electrokinesis in the labs. Still, she did her best to help.
Screams stopped, as throats ripped from strain. The boys arched in the purest agony.
Finally, it stopped. Both fell down, cushioned by force. The Jotunn slammed down, smoke billowing from ports in the suit.
Gwen took a deep breath, drew an arrow, and fired.
Bullseye. Or rather, Jotunn's eye.
It roared, but to her horror, rose. Its suit was fried and dead, but the flesh within still raged.
She drew another arrow, but too late. Its fists slammed down, and clipped her as she dodged. She smacked into the ground, dazed.
The Jotunn raised its cerametallic boot, and planted it on her chest. She forced the air to come back to her lungs, scowling in defiance.
It shifted its weight against her force, sinking into her ribcage. It could've done this quicker - but no. It wanted to make her suffer.
"Get off... get off..." she spat.
The Jotunn grinned savagely under its helm. And then, it heard something that stopped its hearts.
Connor rose.

The Werewolf's chest heaved a bloody, ragged breath. Jagged burns covered his hide, like lightning scars. His eyes blazed with hellish furor.
The Jotunn turned, but not quick enough. Connor wrapped his arms around its helm, and his legs around its shoulders.
Ivan crawled out from his own pile of wreckage, shakily and weakly aiming a hushpuppy. The bullet pinged off of its carapace.
"Yob tvoyu mat..." he spat. "Vanguard! Its armour is too strong!"
"*Its bones aren't.*" he snarled.
He grimaced, as his stomach heaved.
*HAWK-ptoo!*
The acid burned over the monster's neckbrace, and Connor took advantage of the weakness.
He began to twist.
Gwen looked up, and her eyes flashed. Her forces pushed Connor one way, and the Jotunn the other - like taking off a bottlecap.
The Jotunn groaned. It reached up frantically. It tried not to die.
A noise crawled from its mouth. It sounded like a beg.
*SNAP!*
The monster twitched, and fell. Gwen rolled away, as the impact cracked the ground.
Connor collapsed, and rose again.
